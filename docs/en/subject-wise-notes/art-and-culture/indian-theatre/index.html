Historical Evolution
Major Historical Phases of Indian Theatre
Evolution of theatre from the ancient period to the modern era
Theatrical TraditionPeriodKey Identifier
Classical Sanskrit TheatreAncient Period (2nd century BCE – 10th century CE)Natyashastra-compliant, Sutradhara, Nandi, Rasa-Bhava, and aristocratic courts
Traditional Folk TheatreMedieval Period (14th – 19th century)Open stage, devotional and heroic tales, regional vernaculars, and music
Parsi TheatreColonial Period (1850 – 1930)Commercial stage, drop-curtain technology, grand sets, Urdu poetry, and melodrama
IPTA & Realistic TheatreModern Period (Post-1940)Social discourse, political awakening, street plays (Nukkad Natak), and experimentation
