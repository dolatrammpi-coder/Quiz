Literary TraditionLanguage / PeriodKey Feature

Epic and Sutra LiteratureSanskrit (600–200 BCE)Ramayana, Mahabharata, Vedangas, Dharmasutras, and Grihyasutras

Sangam LiteratureOld Tamil (300 BCE–300 CE)Realistic poetry of Akam (inner love) and Puram (heroism and war)
Classical Sanskrit LiteratureClassical (Laukika) Sanskrit (300–1200 CE)Plays and epics by Kalidasa, Bhavabhuti, Bharavi, Dandin, and Banabhatta
Medieval Bhakti and SufiAwadhi, Braj, Tamil, Bengali (1200–1700)Devotion to God, mysticism, rise of vernacular languages, and social equality
Modern Renaissance LiteratureModern Indian Languages (post-1850)National movement, realism, social reform, and critical discourse
