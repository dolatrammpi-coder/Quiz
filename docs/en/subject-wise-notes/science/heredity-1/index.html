Basic Concepts of Genetics, Mendel's Experiments, Laws of Inheritance, and Hybridization

🔍
Quick Reference
Important Facts and Contributions
Heredity 1
Genetic Concept / LawExperiment or RatioConclusion / Example
Law of DominancePure Tall (TT) × Pure Dwarf (tt) -> All hybrid tall (Tt) in F1In the heterozygous state, only the dominant allele (T) is expressed while the recessive allele (t) remains masked
Law of SegregationSelf-pollination of F1 (Tt × Tt) -> F2 phenotypic ratio 3:1, genotypic ratio 1:2:1During gamete formation, both alleles separate cleanly without blending (Law of Purity of Gametes)
Law of Independent AssortmentDihybrid cross (Round-Yellow RRYY × Wrinkled-Green rryy) -> F2 phenotypic ratio 9:3:3:1The inheritance of two or more traits in gametes is completely independent of one another (for unlinked genes)
Test CrossF1 heterozygous (Tt) × recessive parent (tt) -> Progeny ratio 1:1Accurate determination of the genotype (homozygous TT or heterozygous Tt) of an individual with an unknown dominant phenotype
Back CrossCross of an F1 hybrid (Tt) with either parent (TT or tt)Re-establishing specific desirable parental traits in hybrid progeny during plant breeding

Important Facts and Contributions
Heredity 1
Genetic Concept / LawExperiment or RatioConclusion / Example
7 Contrasting Traits in Pea7 pairs of distinct contrasting traits selected by Mendel in garden pea (Pisum sativum)Plant height, flower position, flower color, pod shape, pod color, seed shape, seed color (yellow cotyledon is dominant)
Incomplete DominanceIn Mirabilis jalapa, Red (RR) × White (rr) -> F1 Pink (Rr), F2 ratio 1:2:1Neither allele is completely dominant; emergence of an intermediate phenotype; clear deviation from Mendel's law
Co-dominancePresence of both IA and IB alleles in human ABO blood group -> AB blood groupBoth alleles are equally expressed in the heterozygote; roan coat color in cattle
Chromosomal Theory of InheritanceCorrelation of chromosome behavior during meiosis with Mendel's laws by Sutton and Boveri (1902)Genes are located in a linear order on chromosomes; the actual physical basis of Mendelian factors
Human Sex Determination (XX-XY)Female (44 + XX) × Male (44 + XY) -> 50% Daughter (XX) : 50% Son (XY)The sex of the offspring is determined entirely by the father's sperm (carrying either X or Y)
