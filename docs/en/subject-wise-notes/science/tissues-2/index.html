Animal Tissues - From Epithelium to Neuron (Tissues 2)

Structure and Physiology of Animal Tissues, Epithelial Tissue, Connective Tissue, Muscular Tissue, and Nervous Tissue

🔍
Quick Reference
Key Facts & Contributions
Tissues 2
Animal TissueStructural FeatureFunction/Key Examples
Simple Squamous EpitheliumSingle layer of flattened, tile-like polygonal cells (pavement epithelium), central flattened nucleusDiffusion and filtration boundary; lung alveoli, endothelium of blood capillaries, Bowman's capsule
Simple Cuboidal EpitheliumCube-like cells of equal height and width, central spherical nucleus; brush border (microvilli) in renal tubulesSecretion and absorption; PCT and DCT of nephron, thyroid follicles, salivary gland ducts, germinal epithelium
Simple Columnar EpitheliumTall pillar-like cells, basal oval nucleus; microvilli for absorption in intestinal mucosaAbsorption and mucus secretion; inner lining of stomach and small intestine, gall bladder; includes goblet cells
Ciliated EpitheliumFine hair-like cilia on the free surface of columnar or cuboidal cellsPropels mucus or particles in a specific direction; trachea, bronchioles, Fallopian tubes (oviducts)
Areolar Connective TissueCollagen and elastin fibres scattered in a semi-fluid matrix, fibroblasts, macrophages, and mast cellsBinds skin to underlying muscles (subcutaneous), fills spaces between organs, tissue repair
Adipose TissueAdipocytes filled with lipid droplets; flattened peripheral nucleusEnergy storage, shock-absorbing cushion for internal organs (around heart, kidneys), thermal insulation (blubber)

Key Facts & Contributions
Tissues 2
Animal TissueStructural FeatureFunction/Key Examples
Tendon & LigamentDense regular connective tissue; Tendon = white collagen (inelastic); Ligament = yellow elastin (elastic)Tendon: connects muscle to bone; Ligament: connects bone to bone
CartilageSolid yet pliable matrix (chondrin protein); chondrocytes inside lacunae; avascularReduces friction at bone joints; ear pinna, tip of nose, C-rings of trachea, intervertebral discs
Bone (Osseous Tissue)Hard, non-pliable matrix rich in ossein and calcium phosphate (hydroxyapatite); Haversian systemEndoskeleton of the body, protection of soft organs, RBC production (red bone marrow), mineral reservoir of Ca and P
Blood (Vascular Tissue)Fluid matrix (plasma ~55%) and suspended corpuscles (RBC, WBC, platelets ~45%); fibrelessFluid transport of gases (O₂, CO₂), nutrients, hormones, and wastes; immunity and clotting
Skeletal MuscleCylindrical, unbranched, multinucleated (syncytial), voluntary, alternating dark-light bands (striated)Attached to skeletal system; limb movements, walking-running, facial expressions; rapid contraction but fatigues quickly
Cardiac MuscleCylindrical, branched, uninucleate, involuntary, striated; intercalated discs (gap junctions)Wall of the heart (myocardium); continuous rhythmic contraction and relaxation without fatigue throughout life
Nervous TissueExcitable neurons (cyton, dendron, axon) and supportive non-excitable neuroglia cellsReception of stimuli, rapid electrochemical transmission of nerve impulses; brain, spinal cord, and nerves
