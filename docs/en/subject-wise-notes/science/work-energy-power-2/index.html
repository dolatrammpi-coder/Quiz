Average PowerP_avg = Total Work / Total Time = ΔW / Δt ; Unit: Watt (W)Assessing the average work-execution rate of machines and motors
Instantaneous PowerP = dW / dt = F · v = F · v · cos θ ; Unit: Watt (W)Instantaneous power generated by a moving vehicle or crane at a specific moment from force and velocity
Horsepower (HP)1 Metric HP = 735.5 W ; 1 British HP = 746 WPower rating of automobile engines, tractors, irrigation pumps, and heavy industrial machinery

Universal Law of Conservation of EnergyTotal energy of the universe E_total = Constant ; ΔE = 0Energy can neither be created nor destroyed; it only transforms from one form to another
Law of Conservation of Mechanical EnergyE = K + U = ½mv² + mgh = Constant (for conservative forces only)Motion of a simple pendulum, roller coasters, orbital motion of satellites, and spring oscillations
