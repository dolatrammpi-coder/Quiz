Compound Class/Functional GroupGeneral Formula or Structural FeatureExamples/Key Properties
AlkaneCnH2n+2 (Single covalent bond, sp³ hybridization, paraffins)Methane (CH4 - marsh gas), main components of LPG and CNG
AlkeneCnH2n (Double bond C=C, sp² hybridization, olefins)Ethene/Ethylene (C2H4 - natural fruit-ripening hormone)
AlkyneCnH2n-2 (Triple bond C≡C, sp hybridization)Ethyne/Acetylene (C2H2 - oxy-acetylene welding flame)
Alcohol (-OH)R-OH (Hydroxyl group, IUPAC suffix: -ol)Ethanol (C2H5OH - liquor & gasohol), Methanol (toxic)
Aldehyde (-CHO)R-CHO (H attached to carbonyl group, IUPAC suffix: -al)Formaldehyde (HCHO - biological preservative in formalin)
Ketone (>C=O)R-CO-R' (Divalent carbonyl group, IUPAC suffix: -one)Acetone (CH3COCH3 - nail polish remover & solvent)
Carboxylic Acid (-COOH)R-COOH (Carboxyl group, IUPAC suffix: -oic acid)Acetic acid (vinegar), Formic acid (ant sting)
Ester (-COOR)R-COOR' (Esterification reaction of acid + alcohol)Ethyl acetate (sweet fruity aroma, perfume industry)
Ether (-O-)R-O-R' (Divalent oxygen bridge)Diethyl ether (early surgical anaesthetic)
Haloalkane (R-X)R-X (Halogen group: -Cl, -Br, -I substituted)Chloroform (CHCl3 - anaesthesia), CCl4 (fire extinguisher)
