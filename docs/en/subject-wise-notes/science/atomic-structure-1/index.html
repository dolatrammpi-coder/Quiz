Important Facts and Contributions
Atomic Structure 1
Atomic Model / ScientistKey Postulate or ExperimentMajor Limitation / Contribution
Maharshi Kanada (Indian Philosophy)The smallest indivisible constituent of matter is called 'Paramanu', which cannot exist independentlyLaid the earliest philosophical foundation of the atomic concept
John Dalton (1808)Indivisible spherical atomic theory based on the laws of chemical combinationProvided scientific basis; refuted after the discovery of isotopes and subatomic particles
J. J. Thomson (1904)Plum pudding model; electrons are embedded in a positively charged sphere like seeds in a watermelonIncorporated the first subatomic particle (electron); failed to explain Rutherford's scattering
Ernest Rutherford (1911)Alpha-particle scattering experiment on gold foil; discovery of the central positively charged nucleusFoundation of nuclear physics; could not explain atomic stability or line spectra
Niels Bohr (1913)Discrete stationary orbits (mvr = nh/2π) and energy levels based on quantum theorySuccessfully explained hydrogen spectrum and stability; failed to explain Zeeman and Stark effects
Arnold Sommerfeld (1916)Introduced elliptical orbits to extend Bohr's circular orbitsPartial attempt to explain the fine structure of spectral lines
