Disease / Immune ComponentCause or Biological ProcessPrevention / Examples
Typhoid (Enteric Fever)Salmonella typhi bacterium; intestinal infection via contaminated water and food; diagnosed by Widal testSafe drinking water, sanitation, and TAB vaccine
Tuberculosis (TB)Mycobacterium tuberculosis bacterium; tubercle formation and tissue necrosis in lungsBCG vaccine and DOTS (Directly Observed Treatment, Short-course)

AIDSHuman Immunodeficiency Virus (HIV - retrovirus); destruction of helper T-lymphocytes (CD4 cells)Safe blood transfusion, sterile needles, safe sexual practices, and ART therapy
RingwormMicrosporum and Trichophyton fungi; dry, scaly, itchy circular lesions on skinPersonal hygiene, antifungal ointments, and avoiding sharing infected clothing
Elephantiasis (Filariasis)Wuchereria bancrofti nematode; inflammation of lymphatic vessels via female Culex mosquito biteDEC (Diethylcarbamazine) medication and mosquito breeding control

Key Facts & Contributions
Human Health 1
Disease / Immune ComponentCause or Biological ProcessPrevention / Examples
Innate ImmunityInborn non-specific defense; skin, mucus, gastric HCl, lysozyme, and phagocytic cellsFirst and second lines of defense; automatic barrier against entry of external pathogens
Acquired ImmunityMemory-based specific defense developed after pathogen exposure (B and T lymphocytes)Lifelong or long-term protection following vaccination or natural infection
Immunoglobulin G (IgG)Most abundant antibody in blood plasma (~80%); smallest in molecular sizeOnly antibody capable of crossing the placenta to provide passive immunity to the fetus
Immunoglobulin A (IgA)Secretory antibody; present in mother's first thick yellow milk (colostrum), saliva, and tearsProtects newborn's digestive tract from infection and safeguards mucous membranes
Immunoglobulin E (IgE)Antibody binding to mast cells and basophils to trigger histamine releaseDefense against allergic reactions (hay fever, asthma) and helminthic (worm) infections
Active ImmunizationIntroducing killed or attenuated pathogens to stimulate host antibody and memory cell productionPolio vaccine (OPV/IPV), MMR, and Hepatitis B vaccine
Passive ImmunizationDirect administration of preformed (ready-made) antibodies into the patient for immediate protectionTetanus ATS injection and anti-rabies serum
