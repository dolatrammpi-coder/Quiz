Hormones and Plant Coordination - From Endocrine System to Auxin (Control Coordination 2)

Endocrine Glands, Human Hormones, Plant Hormones, and Chemical Coordination

🔍
Quick Reference
Key Facts and Contributions
Control Coordination 2
Gland / Hormone / Plant HormoneSecretion or SourceMajor Functions / Effects
Pituitary Gland (Master Gland)Anterior pituitary (Adenohypophysis) and posterior pituitary (Neurohypophysis)Secretion of Growth Hormone (GH), TSH, ACTH, plus ADH for water balance and Oxytocin for parturition
Thyroid GlandThyroxine (T4) and Triiodothyronine (T3) — Source: Follicular cellsRegulation of Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR); deficiency causes goitre and cretinism in children
Parathyroid GlandParathormone (PTH) — 4 small glands located on the posterior surface of the thyroid glandIncreases blood calcium levels by mobilizing Ca²⁺ from bones (hypercalcemic hormone)
Pancreas (Islets of Langerhans)Insulin from beta cells and Glucagon from alpha cellsBlood glucose homeostasis; insulin lowers levels (deficiency causes diabetes mellitus), glucagon raises levels
Adrenal GlandCortisol and aldosterone from the cortex; adrenaline (epinephrine) from the medullaEmergency 'fight or flight' response, water-electrolyte balance, and stress management
Pineal GlandMelatonin hormone — located on the dorsal side of the forebrainRegulation of the 24-hour circadian rhythm (sleep-wake cycle) and internal biological clock
Thymus GlandThymosin hormone — located on the dorsal side of the aorta near the heartSite of T-lymphocyte maturation; primary training centre for adaptive immunity
Quick Reference
Key Facts and Contributions
Control Coordination 2
Gland / Hormone / Plant HormoneSecretion or SourceMajor Functions / Effects
Auxin (IAA)Shoot and root apices of stems and rootsCell elongation, apical dominance, phototropism, and parthenocarpic (seedless) fruit formation
Gibberellin (GA3)Germinating seeds, young leaves, and fungus (Gibberella fujikuroi)Elongation of genetically dwarf plants, bolting (internode elongation), and breaking seed dormancy
CytokininRapidly dividing tissues (root apices, developing fruits, and embryos)Rapid cell division, growth of lateral buds, and delaying leaf senescence (ageing)
Ethylene (C2H4)Ripening fruits and senescing tissues (the only gaseous plant hormone)Natural ripening of unripe fruits, transverse growth, and increasing the number of female flowers
Abscisic Acid (ABA)Leaves, desiccated tissues, and vascular tissues (stress hormone / growth inhibitor)Closing stomata during water stress, leaf abscission (shedding), and inducing seed dormancy
Plant Movements (Tropism & Nastic)Direction of stimulus (light, gravity) and touch (Touch-me-not — Mimosa pudica)Adaptation of plant organs; bending toward light and drooping of leaves due to turgor pressure changes
