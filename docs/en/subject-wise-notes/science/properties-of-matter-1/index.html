Physical Properties (Density, Elasticity) (Properties Of Matter 1)

Density, Relative Density, Anomalous Expansion of Water, Elasticity, Stress, Strain, and Hooke's Law

🔍
Quick Reference
Important Facts and Contributions
Properties Of Matter 1
Physical PropertyFormula / MeasurementApplications / Physical Significance
Densityρ = m / V (Mass / Volume); SI unit: kg/m³; CGS unit: g/cm³ (1 g/cm³ = 1000 kg/m³)Testing the purity of substances; fundamental condition of flotation (a body floats only when its average density is less than or equal to that of the fluid)
Relative DensityRD = Density of substance / Density of pure water at 4°C (unitless and dimensionless ratio)Measuring liquid density using a hydrometer; rapid testing of milk purity and fat percentage using a lactometer
Anomalous Expansion of WaterVolume contraction and density increase upon heating from 0°C to 4°C (at 4°C, ρ_max = 1000 kg/m³)Survival of aquatic organisms in 4°C water at the bottom of lakes during severe winters even after the surface freezes
Flotation and BuoyancyTotal buoyant force acting on a body (F_B = V_sub · ρ_liquid · g) balances the weight of the bodyFloating of massive iron ships on the sea; diving and surfacing of submarines using ballast tanks
Stressσ = Internal restoring force / Cross-sectional area = F / A; SI unit: N/m² or Pascal (Pa)Load-bearing capacity and safe working stress design of bridges, buildings, crane cables, and pillars
Strainε = Change in configuration / Original configuration (ΔL/L, ΔV/V, or θ); unitless and dimensionless quantityQuantifying deformation of structural components; assessing the elastic limit and breaking point

Important Facts and Contributions
Properties Of Matter 1
Physical PropertyFormula / MeasurementApplications / Physical Significance
Hooke's LawWithin the elastic limit: Stress ∝ Strain (Stress / Strain = E = Modulus of Elasticity)Calibration of spring balances; fundamental principle of elastic behavior in physics and engineering
Young's Modulus of Elasticity (Y)Y = Longitudinal stress / Longitudinal strain = (F · L) / (A · ΔL); Unit: N/m² (for solids only)Steel is more elastic than rubber; manufacturing steel ropes for elevators, cable cars, and suspension bridges
Bulk Modulus of Elasticity (B)B = -ΔP / (ΔV / V); Compressibility K = 1 / B (applicable to solids, liquids, and gases)Studying water compression at oceanic depths; industrial applications of the high compressibility of gases
Modulus of Rigidity (η)η = Tangential stress / Shear strain = (F / A) / θ; Unit: N/m² (for solids only)Resistance to torsion in rotating engine shafts, drill bits, and screws
I-Shaped GirdersDepression (bending) δ = (W · L³) / (4 Y b d³) (inversely proportional to the cube of depth 'd')High resistance to bending with minimum weight in railway tracks, overbridges, and industrial sheds
Poisson's Ratioσ = Lateral strain / Longitudinal strain = -(Δd / d) / (ΔL / L); Theoretical range: -1 to +0.5Rubber (σ ≈ 0.5) and cork (σ ≈ 0); cork's lack of lateral expansion makes it ideal for airtight bottle stoppers
