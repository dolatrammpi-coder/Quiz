Sex Determination and Genetic Disorders - From X-Y to Down Syndrome (Heredity 2)

Sex Determination, X-Y Chromosomes, Sex-Linked Disorders, Syndromes, and Genetic Counseling

🔍
Quick Reference
Important Facts and Contributions
Heredity 2
Chromosome / DisorderGenetic BasisKey Symptoms / Counseling Facts
Down Syndrome (Mongolism)Trisomy of the 21st autosome (47 chromosomes, 45 + XX/XY; non-disjunction)Round flat face, Mongolian eyes, protruding furrowed tongue, simian crease on palm, and intellectual disability; high risk in mothers over 35 years of age
Klinefelter SyndromePresence of an extra X chromosome in males (47 chromosomes, 44 + XXY)Tall stature, sterile male, underdeveloped testes, abnormal breast enlargement (gynecomastia); 1 Barr body present
Turner SyndromeAbsence of one X chromosome in females (45 chromosomes, 44 + XO monosomy)Short stature, webbed neck, underdeveloped ovaries, sterile female, absence of secondary sexual characteristics; 0 Barr bodies
Edwards SyndromeTrisomy of the 18th autosome (47 chromosomes, 45 + XX/XY)Clenched fists, prominent occiput, malformed ears, severe heart defects; most infants die at an early age
Patau SyndromeTrisomy of the 13th autosome (47 chromosomes, 45 + XX/XY)Cleft lip and palate, small eyes (microphthalmia), extra fingers (polydactyly), nervous system failure
Cri-du-chat Syndrome (Cat Cry Syndrome)Partial deletion of the short arm of the 5th chromosome (5p- deletion)Newborn's cry resembling a cat's meow, small head (microcephaly), and severe intellectual disability
Quick Reference
Important Facts and Contributions
Heredity 2
Chromosome / DisorderGenetic BasisKey Symptoms / Counseling Facts
Hemophilia (Royal Disease)X-linked recessive disorder; deficiency of blood clotting factor VIII (A) or IX (B)Continuous bleeding even from minor injuries; higher expression in males, females mainly carriers (criss-cross inheritance)
Red-Green Color Blindness (Daltonism)X-linked recessive gene mutation; defect in red-green cones of the retinaInability to distinguish between red and green colors; tested using Ishihara chart; 8% prevalence in males and 0.4% in females
Sickle Cell AnemiaHBB gene mutation on the 11th autosome (GAG to GUG; glutamic acid replaced by valine at the 6th position)Sickle-shaped RBCs, impaired oxygen transport; heterozygous carriers (HbA HbS) have natural protection against falciparum malaria
Thalassemia (Alpha / Beta)Reduced synthesis of globin chains on the 16th (alpha) or 11th (beta) chromosomeSevere anemia, need for repeated blood transfusions; pre-marital hemoglobin testing and genetic counseling essential
Huntington's DiseaseCAG trinucleotide repeat mutation on the 4th autosome (autosomal dominant disorder)Uncontrolled limb movements (chorea) at age 35-40, depression, and progressive degeneration of nerve cells; 50% transmission risk
AmniocentesisFetal karyotyping by sampling amniotic fluid at 15-20 weeks of pregnancyEarly detection of chromosomal disorders (Down syndrome) in the fetus; strictly banned for sex determination in India under the PCPNDT Act
