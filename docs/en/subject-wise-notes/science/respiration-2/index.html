Key Facts & Contributions
Respiration 2
Respiratory Organ/StageGas Exchange ProcessKey Function/Feature
InspirationEntry of atmospheric O₂-rich air into the lungs down a pressure gradientFlattening of the diaphragm + contraction of external intercostal muscles; increase in thoracic cavity volume, intrapulmonary pressure -1 to -3 mmHg
ExpirationExpulsion of alveolar air containing CO₂ from the lungs into the atmosphereDiaphragm returns to dome shape + relaxation of muscles (passive process); decrease in thoracic cavity volume and elastic recoil of lungs
O₂ Transport97% by hemoglobin as oxyhemoglobin (Hb₄O₈), 3% dissolved in plasmaSigmoid curve (Oxygen Dissociation Curve); Bohr effect (release of O₂ in tissues at high CO₂ and low pH)
CO₂ Transport70% as bicarbonate (HCO₃⁻), 20–25% as carbaminohemoglobin, 7% dissolved in plasmaRapid conversion by carbonic anhydrase enzyme; Hamburger phenomenon (Chloride Shift: influx of Cl⁻ into RBCs)
Respiratory CentersAutomatic regulation of breathing rate and depth based on pCO₂ and H⁺ concentration in bloodRespiratory rhythm center in the medulla oblongata and pneumotaxic center in the pons
EmphysemaPermanent damage and breakdown of alveolar walls due to smoking and air pollutionDrastic reduction in effective surface area for gas exchange; loss of lung elasticity leading to breathlessness
