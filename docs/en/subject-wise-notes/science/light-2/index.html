Refraction of Light and Lenses - From Snell's Law to Lens Formula (Light 2)

Refraction of light, Snell's law, refractive index, lenses, lens formula, and power of a lens

🔍
Quick Reference
Key Facts & Contributions
Light 2
Refraction / Lens ConceptLaw / FormulaImage / Applications
Refraction of LightSnell's Law: sin i / sin r = n₂₁ = v₁ / v₂ (incident ray, refracted ray, and normal lie in the same plane)Change in speed and direction of light when changing medium; a pencil appearing bent, a coin appearing raised
Absolute Refractive Indexn = c / v (speed of light in vacuum / speed of light in medium); vacuum n=1, water 1.33, glass 1.5, diamond 2.42Measure of optical density of a medium; the higher the n, the lower the speed of light
Apparent Depth and ShiftApparent depth h' = h / n ; Apparent shift Δx = h(1 - 1/n)The bottom of a water-filled pond or beaker appearing shallower (raised) than its actual depth
Atmospheric RefractionContinuous gradual bending of light by atmospheric layers of varying densityTwinkling of stars, advance sunrise (2 minutes earlier), and delayed sunset (2 minutes later)
Total Internal Reflection (TIR)Angle of incidence i > Critical angle C ; sin C = 1 / n (traveling from denser to rarer medium is mandatory)Brilliance of a diamond, mirage in deserts, optical fiber, and endoscopy
Convex Lens (Converging Lens)Focal length f > 0 (positive); converges parallel rays at the principal focusSimple microscope, magnifying glass, astronomical telescope, camera, and correction of hypermetropia (farsightedness)

Key Facts & Contributions
Light 2
Refraction / Lens ConceptLaw / FormulaImage / Applications
Concave Lens (Diverging Lens)Focal length f < 0 (negative); diverges parallel light rays outwardAlways forms a virtual, erect, and diminished image; Galilean telescope and correction of myopia (nearsightedness)
Lens Formula1/f = 1/v - 1/u (with sign convention for u, v, f)Exact mathematical relationship between object distance (u), image distance (v), and focal length (f) from the lens
Linear Magnificationm = h'/h = v/u (image height / object height)m > 0 indicates a virtual and erect image; m < 0 indicates a real and inverted image
Lens Maker's Formula1/f = (n - 1) [1/R₁ - 1/R₂]Manufacturing specific spectacle lenses; change in focal length and nature of a lens when changing the surrounding medium
Power of a LensP = 1 / f (in meters) or P = 100 / f (in cm) ; Unit: Dioptre (D)Convex lens power is positive (+D), concave lens power is negative (-D); determining spectacle prescriptions
Combination of LensesEquivalent power P = P₁ + P₂ ; Equivalent focal length 1/F = 1/f₁ + 1/f₂ ; Magnification m = m₁ × m₂Used in objective and eyepiece of microscopes and telescopes to improve clarity and remove chromatic aberration
