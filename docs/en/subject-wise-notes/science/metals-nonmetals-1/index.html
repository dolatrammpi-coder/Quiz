Metals Nonmetals 1
Metal / Non-Metal PropertyChemical ReactionUses / Examples
Reaction of Metals with Oxygen2Mg + O₂ → 2MgO (forms basic oxide); dissolves in water to form Mg(OH)₂Burning of magnesium ribbon; turns red litmus blue; antacid (Milk of Magnesia)
Amphoteric OxidesAl₂O₃ + 6HCl → 2AlCl₃ + 3H₂O ; Al₂O₃ + 2NaOH → 2NaAlO₂ + H₂OAluminium oxide and zinc oxide; react with both acids and bases to form salt and water
Reaction of Highly Reactive Metals with Water2Na + 2H₂O → 2NaOH + H₂ ↑ + Heat (vigorous exothermic reaction)Sodium and potassium; stored in kerosene to prevent catching fire in air and water
Acidic Oxides of Non-MetalsS + O₂ → SO₂ ; SO₂ + H₂O → H₂SO₃ (Sulphurous acid)Sulphur dioxide and carbon dioxide; turn blue litmus red; major components of acid rain
Neutral Non-Metallic Oxides2C + O₂ (limited) → 2CO ; N₂ + O₂ → 2NO (no effect on litmus)Carbon monoxide (CO), nitrous oxide (N₂O - laughing gas), and water (H₂O)
Reaction of Metals with Dilute AcidsZn + 2HCl → ZnCl₂ + H₂ ↑ (burns with a pop sound)Laboratory preparation of hydrogen gas; avoiding storage of acidic foods in copper or brass vessels
