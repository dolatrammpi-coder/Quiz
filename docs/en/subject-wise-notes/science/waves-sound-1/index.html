Properties and Types of Waves - Amplitude, Wavelength, Frequency (Waves Sound 1)

Concept of waves, transverse and longitudinal waves, amplitude, wavelength, frequency, time period, and the wave equation

🔍
Quick Reference
Key Facts & Contributions
Waves Sound 1
Wave Quantity / TypeDefinition / RelationshipFormula / Examples
Mechanical WavesRequire a material medium (solid, liquid, gas) for propagation; transfer energy and momentum without permanent displacement of medium particlesSound waves, water surface waves, seismic waves
Electromagnetic (EM) WavesRequire no material medium for propagation; travel through a vacuum; generated by accelerating electric chargesLight waves, X-rays, gamma rays, radio waves; speed in vacuum c = 3 × 10⁸ m/s
Transverse WavesParticles of the medium vibrate perpendicular (at 90°) to the direction of wave propagation; form crests and troughsLight waves, waves on a stretched string; possible only in solids and on liquid surfaces
Longitudinal WavesParticles of the medium vibrate parallel to the direction of wave propagation; form compressions and rarefactionsSound waves in air, primary (P) seismic waves; possible in solids, liquids, and gases
Wave EquationUniversal relationship between wave speed, frequency, and wavelengthv = ν · λ (Speed = Frequency × Wavelength); ω = 2πν and k = 2π/λ
Time Period & Frequency RelationTime taken for one complete oscillation is Time Period (T), and number of oscillations per second is Frequency (ν)ν = 1 / T ; SI unit: Hertz (Hz or s⁻¹)
Quick Reference
Key Facts & Contributions
Waves Sound 1
Wave Quantity / TypeDefinition / RelationshipFormula / Examples
Amplitude (A)Maximum displacement of a particle from its mean position; determines the energy carried by a waveEnergy E ∝ A² (doubling the amplitude increases wave energy four-fold)
Wavelength (λ)Minimum distance between two consecutive points in the same phase (distance between two crests or two troughs)SI unit: metre (m); also measured in Angstrom (Å) or nanometre (nm)
Wave Speed / Velocity (v)Distance travelled by a wave per unit timev = d / t ; maximum in solids, minimum in gases
Progressive Wave EquationDisplacement function of a simple harmonic wave at any instanty(x, t) = A sin(ωt − kx) or y = A sin(2π(t/T − x/λ))
Speed of Sound in a Medium (Newton-Laplace Formula)Dependence on elasticity and density of the mediumv = √(γP / ρ) (Laplace correction treated propagation as adiabatic rather than isothermal)
Phase & Phase DifferenceDifference in the state of motion of two points on a waveΔφ = (2π / λ) · Δx (relation between path difference and phase difference)
