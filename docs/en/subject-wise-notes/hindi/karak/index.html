Quick Reference
All Eight Cases (Karak), Their Case Markers, and Primary Uses
Karak
Case (Karak)Case Marker (Vibhakti)Primary Usage
Karta Karak (Nominative Case)ne (or zero/null marker)Indicates the independent doer (subject) performing the action
Karma Karak (Accusative Case)ko (or zero/null marker)Indicates the person or object directly affected by the verb's action
Karan Karak (Instrumental Case)se, ke dwara (with, by means of)Indicates the instrument, tool, or medium through which an action is accomplished
Sampradan Karak (Dative Case)ko, ke liye, hetu (to, for)Indicates the recipient or beneficiary for whom an action or donation is performed
Apadan Karak (Ablative Case)se (indicating separation)Indicates separation, fear, shyness, comparison, or distance
Sambandh Karak (Genitive/Possessive Case)ka, ki, ke, ra, ri, re, na, ni, neShows mutual relationship or possession between two nouns or pronouns
Adhikaran Karak (Locative Case)mein, par, pe (in, on, at)Indicates the place or time serving as the base/locus of the action
Sambodhan Karak (Vocative Case)He!, Are!, O!, Aji!Used to call out, address, or alert someone
