Key Differences Between an Idiom (Muhavara) and a Proverb (Lokokti)
Muhavare
Basis of ComparisonIdiom (Muhavara)Proverb (Lokokti)
Form / StructureIt is a phrase (vakyansh), not a complete sentenceIt is an independent, complete sentence
Usage in a SentenceUsed strictly within a sentenceUsed independently to substantiate a statement
Grammatical InflectionChanges according to gender, number, and tenseIts original form always remains fixed
Word Power (Shabda Shakti)Based on Lakshana (figurative/indicative meaning)Based on Vyanjana (suggestive/implied meaning)
IdentificationUsually ends with the verb suffix '-na' (ना)Ending with '-na' (ना) is not mandatory
Origin / BasisOriginates from human behavior and actionsOriginates from folk experience or folk tales
