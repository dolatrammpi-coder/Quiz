Major Poetic Streams of Ritikal and Their Characteristics
Ritikal
Poetic StreamRepresentative PoetsKey Characteristic
Ritibaddha (Bound by Poetics)Keshavdas, Matiram, Padmakar, DevClassical tradition of defining poetic rules (Lakshana) and giving examples (Udaharana)
Ritisiddha (Master of Poetics without Treatises)Biharilal, Rasnidhi, Nrip ShambhuSuperb adherence to Riti rules without writing formal treatises on poetics
Ritimukta (Free from Poetic Conventions)Ghananand, Bodha, Alam, ThakurSpontaneous, convention-free expression of inner agony of love (Prem ki Peer)
Riti-Veera Kavya (Heroic Riti Poetry)Poet Bhushan, Sudan, Lal KaviHistorical heroic sagas of patrons and nationalistic consciousness
Riti-Niti Kavya (Didactic Riti Poetry)Vrind, Girdhar Kaviray, BaitalLessons on ethics, moral counsel, proverbs, and worldly conduct
