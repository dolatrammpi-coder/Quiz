Matra Structure and Identification of Major Matrik Chhands
Chhand
Chhand (Meter)Matra / Varna StructureIdentification / Characteristic
Chaupai16 Matras in each Charan (quarter-verse)Sam Matrik (equal-beat); Guru-Laghu prohibited at the end (usually no Jagana/Tagana)
Doha13 Matras in odd Charans, 11 in even Charans (total 24)Ardhasam Matrik (half-equal); Guru-Laghu (ऽ।) mandatory at the end
Sortha11 Matras in odd Charans, 13 in even Charans (total 24)Ardhasam Matrik; exact reverse of Doha; rhyme (Tuk) in odd Charans
Rola24 Matras in each Charan (Yati/pause at 11-13)Sam Matrik meter; two Gurus or two Laghus at the end
Barvai12 Matras in odd Charans, 7 in even Charans (total 19)Ardhasam Matrik; most favored meter of the Awadhi language
Geetika26 Matras in each Charan (Yati at 14-12)Sam Matrik meter; rule of Laghu-Guru (।ऽ) at the end
Harigeetika28 Matras in each Charan (Yati at 16-12)Sam Matrik meter; Laghu-Guru (।ऽ) mandatory at the end
Kundaliya6 Charans (2 Charans of Doha + 4 Charans of Rola)Visham Matrik (unequal); ends with the exact word it begins with
Chhappay6 Charans (4 Charans of Rola + 2 Charans of Ullala)Visham Matrik meter; 28 Matras in Ullala
