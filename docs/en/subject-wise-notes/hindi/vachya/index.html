Identification and Examples of Kartrivachya, Karmavachya, and Bhavvachya
Vachya
Voice (Vachya)Identification / RuleExamples
Kartrivachya (Active Voice)Subject (Karta) is predominant; verb agrees with subject's gender and number; uses both transitive and intransitive verbsRam pustak padhta hai (Ram reads a book), Mohan daudta hai (Mohan runs)
Karmavachya (Passive Voice)Object (Karma) is predominant; verb agrees with object's gender and number; only transitive verbs; subject takes 'ke dwara' or 'se'Ram dwara pustak padhi jati hai (A book is read by Ram), Patra likha gaya (The letter was written)
Bhavvachya (Impersonal Voice)Action/emotion (Bhav) is predominant; verb is always masculine, singular, 3rd person; only intransitive verbs; expresses inabilityMujhse baitha nahi jata (I cannot sit), Rogi se chala nahi jata (The patient cannot walk)
Kartrivachya (Past Tense)In transitive verbs, subject takes 'ne' marker; verb agrees with the object (Karmani Prayog)Ram ne roti khai (Ram ate bread), Usne patra likha (He wrote a letter)
Karmavachya (Administrative / Legal)Unknown agent or official/legal order; direct predominance of the objectChor pakda gaya (The thief was caught), Soochna prakashit ki gayi (The notice was published)
Bhavvachya (Affirmative)Used without negation to express permission, suggestion, or willingnessAb chala jaye (Let us leave now), Thoda soya jaye (Let us sleep a little)

Quick Reference
Major Rules and Examples of Voice Transformation (Vachya Parivartan)
Vachya
Voice TransformationRuleExamples
Kartrivachya to KarmavachyaAdd 'se' or 'ke dwara' with the subject, make the object predominant, convert the main verb to simple past and add the tense-appropriate form of 'jana'Mohan patra likhta hai -> Mohan dwara patra likha jata hai
Kartrivachya to Karmavachya (Past Tense)Remove 'ne' and add 'ke dwara'; use the past tense form of 'jana' (gaya/gayi/gaye)Adhyapak ne path padhaya -> Adhyapak dwara path padhaya gaya
Kartrivachya to Karmavachya (Future Tense)Add 'dwara' with the subject; use the future tense form of 'jana' (jayega/jayegi)Sarkar sahayata degi -> Sarkar dwara sahayata di jayegi
Kartrivachya to BhavvachyaAdd 'se' with the subject, put the main verb in simple past, and keep 'jana' in masculine singular 3rd personBachcha nahi rota -> Bachche se roya nahi jata
Kartrivachya to Bhavvachya (Inability)Use negative 'nahi' and place 'se' after the subjectVriddha chal nahi sakta -> Vriddha se chala nahi jata
Karmavachya to KartrivachyaRemove 'se'/'ke dwara', remove object predominance, and make the verb agree with the subjectMali dwara paudhe seenche jate hain -> Mali paudhe seenchta hai
Bhavvachya to KartrivachyaRemove 'se' from the subject and restore the verb to a normal subject-based formMujhse hansa nahi jata -> Main hans nahi sakta
