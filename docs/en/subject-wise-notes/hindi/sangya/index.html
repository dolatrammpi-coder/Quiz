Major Types of Sangya (Noun), Identification, and Examples
Sangya
Type of Noun (Sangya)IdentificationExamples
Vyaktivachak Sangya (Proper Noun)Names a single specific person, object, or placeRam, Sita, Himalaya, Ganga, Bharat, Ramayana
Jativachak Sangya (Common Noun)Denotes an entire class, species, or category of living beings, objects, or placesNadi (river), Parvat (mountain), Ladka (boy), Pustak (book), Nagar (city), Gay (cow), Vriksha (tree)
Bhavvachak Sangya (Abstract Noun)Denotes an intangible quality, defect, state, condition, emotion, or actionSundarta (beauty), Bachpan (childhood), Mithas (sweetness), Budhapa (old age), Krodh (anger), Daya (compassion)
Dravyavachak Sangya (Material Noun)Denotes metals, non-metals, solids, or liquids that are measured or weighedSona (gold), Chandi (silver), Tamba (copper), Pani (water), Ghee, Tel (oil), Doodh (milk), Gehun (wheat)
Samoohvachak Sangya (Collective Noun)Denotes a group, community, or collection of people or objectsSena (army), Sabha (assembly), Kaksha (class), Bheed (crowd), Parivar (family), Dal (group), Guchchha (bunch)

Root Word / CategorySuffix / RuleBhavvachak Sangya (Abstract Noun)

Jativachak Sangya (Budha, Ladka, Pashu)'-pa', '-pan', '-tva' suffixesBudhapa (old age), Ladakpan (boyhood), Pashutva (animality)
Sarvanam (Apna, Nij, Mam)'-pan/-tva', '-tva', '-ta/-tva' suffixesApnapan (affinity), Nijatva (privacy/individuality), Mamta/Mamatva (maternal affection)
Sarvanam (Aham, Paraya, Sarva)'-kar', '-pan', '-sva' suffixesAhankar (ego), Parayapan (estrangement), Sarvasva (everything/one's all)
Visheshan (Sundar, Meetha, Chatur)'-ta/-ya', '-aas', '-ai/-ta' suffixesSundarta/Saundarya (beauty), Mithas (sweetness), Chaturai (cleverness)
Visheshan (Kathor, Aalsi, Bhookha)'-ta', '-ya', '-aas' (zero-suffix stem) patternsKathorta (harshness), Aalasya (laziness), Bhookh (hunger)
Kriya (Likhna, Padhna, Hansna)'-ai', '-ai', '-i' suffixesLikhai (writing), Padhai (study), Hansi (laughter)
Kriya (Udna, Chadhna, Thakna)'-aan', '-ai', '-avat' suffixesUdaan (flight), Chadhai (ascent), Thakavat (fatigue)
Avyay (Door, Sameep, Dhik)'-i', '-ya', '-kar' suffixesDoori (distance), Samipya (proximity), Dhikkar (reproach/curse)
Avyay (Sheeghra, Mana, Neeche)'-ta', '-hi', '-ai' suffixesSheeghrata (swiftness), Manahi (prohibition), Nichai (lowness)
