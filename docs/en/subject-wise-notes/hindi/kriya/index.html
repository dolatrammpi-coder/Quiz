Identification and Classification of Major Types of Kriya (Verbs)
Kriya
Type of Verb (Kriya)IdentificationExample
Sakarmak Kriya (Transitive Verb)Presence or possibility of an object (Karma)Mohan phal khata hai (Mohan eats fruit)
Akarmak Kriya (Intransitive Verb)Absence of an object; action's effect falls on the subjectPakshi aakash mein udte hain (Birds fly in the sky)
Dwikarmak Kriya (Ditransitive Verb)Presence of two objects (primary/direct and secondary/indirect)Adhyapak chhatron ko Hindi padhate hain (The teacher teaches Hindi to the students)
Preranarthak Kriya (Causative Verb)Subject does not act directly, but gets the action done by othersMaa naukar se safai karwati hai (Mother gets the servant to clean)
Purvakalik Kriya (Past-Participle / Prior Action Verb)Action completed immediately before the main verbWah padhkar so gaya (He slept after studying)
Namdhatu Kriya (Nominal Verb)Formed from nouns, pronouns, or adjectivesbatiyana from baat, garmana from garm
Sahayak Kriya (Auxiliary Verb)Indicates the tense of the main verbWah ja raha hai (He is going)
Sanyukt Kriya (Compound Verb)Formed by combining two or more verb roots (dhatu)Wah patra likh chuka (He has finished writing the letter)
