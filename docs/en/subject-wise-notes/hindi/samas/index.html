Identification and Examples of Major Samas Types
Samas
SamasIdentification / Dissolution (Vigraha)Examples
Avyayibhava Samas (Adverbial Compound)First constituent (Purvapada) is dominant and an indeclinable (Avyaya: Yatha, Prati, Aa, Bhar)Yathashakti, Aajeevan, Pratidin, Bharpet
Tatpurusha Samas (Determinative Compound)Second constituent (Uttarapada) is dominant, with elision of case markers (Karaka Chihna)Rajputra, Rasoighar, Rinamukt, Aapbeeti
Karmadharaya Samas (Appositional Compound)Adjective-noun (Visheshana-Visheshya) or standard-subject (Upamana-Upameya) relationshipNeelkamal, Chandramukh, Charankamal, Mahatma
Dvigu Samas (Numeral Compound)First constituent is a numeral adjective indicating a collective group (Samahara)Chauraha, Panchavati, Triphala, Shatabdi
Dvandva Samas (Copulative Compound)Both constituents are equally dominant, with elision of 'aur' (and) or 'ya' (or)Mata-Pita, Sukh-Dukh, Daal-Roti
Bahuvrihi Samas (Exocentric Compound)Neither constituent is dominant; together they refer to a third implied meaning (Anya Pada)Dashanan, Lambodar, Peetambar, Chakrapani

Major Subtypes and Dissolution (Vigraha) of Tatpurusha Samas
Samas
SubtypeIdentification / Dissolution (Vigraha)Examples
Karma Tatpurusha (Accusative / Dvitiya)Elision of the accusative case marker 'ko'Gaganchumbi (gagan ko choomne wala — sky-kissing)
Karana Tatpurusha (Instrumental / Tritiya)Elision of 'se' or 'ke dwara' (by / with)Rekhankit (rekha se ankit — marked by lines)
Sampradana Tatpurusha (Dative / Chaturthi)Elision of the dative case marker 'ke liye' (for)Hathkadi (haath ke liye kadi — handcuff)
Apadana Tatpurusha (Ablative / Panchami)Elision of 'se' denoting separation (Alagaav)Dhanheen (dhan se heen — devoid of wealth)
Sambandha Tatpurusha (Genitive / Shashthi)Elision of 'ka', 'ki', 'ke' (of)Rajmahal (raja ka mahal — king's palace)
Adhikarana Tatpurusha (Locative / Saptami)Elision of 'mein' (in) or 'par' (on)Shokamagna (shok mein magna — immersed in grief)
Nan Tatpurusha (Negative Determinative)Negative prefix 'a' or 'an' as the first constituentAnachar (na achar — misconduct), Adharma (na dharma — unrighteousness)
Aluk Tatpurusha (Non-elided Case)Case inflection of the first constituent is not droppedYudhishthira (yuddha mein sthir — steady in battle)
Upapada TatpurushaSecond constituent is not independent, functioning as a participial suffix (Kridanta)Jalchar (jal mein vicharne wala — aquatic creature)
Madhyamapadalopi Tatpurusha (Middle-term Elided)Elision of an entire intervening phrase between the two constituentsDahibada (dahi mein dooba bada — vada soaked in curd)
