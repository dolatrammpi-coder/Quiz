Key Facts & Contributions
Banking Reforms Part 2
Committee / ReformKey Recommendation or FeatureImpact on Banking
Narasimham Committee-II (1998)View Answer Raising Capital Adequacy Ratio (CRAR) to 10%, 'Narrow Banking' for weak banks, and reducing government stake to 33%.View Answer Brought international financial strength to bank balance sheets, increased regulatory independence, and modernized risk management.
Bank Merger & Consolidation PolicyView Answer Consolidation of 27 public sector banks into 12 large banks during 2017–2020 (SBI associate merger, Bank of Baroda merger, and mega-merger of 10 banks into 4).View Answer Creation of globally scaled banks, economies of scale, reduction in operating costs, and expansion of lending capacity.
Entry of Private Sector Banks (1993, 2001, 2013)View Answer Licensing of HDFC, ICICI, Axis, Kotak Mahindra, Bandhan, and IDFC FIRST Bank under strict capital and corporate governance norms.View Answer Healthy competition in the banking sector, introduction of innovative financial products, revolutionary improvement in customer service, and private asset share reaching ~35–40%.
Differentiated BanksView Answer Establishment of Payments Banks and Small Finance Banks (SFBs) on the recommendation of the Nachiket Mor Committee.View Answer Last-mile financial inclusion, convenient digital payments for migrant workers, and formal micro-credit for small businesses and marginal farmers.
Core Banking Solutions (CBS)View Answer Connecting all bank branches to a central server, transforming a customer from a 'customer of a branch' into a 'customer of the entire bank'.View Answer Enabled inter-branch banking, anywhere-anytime transactions, faster cheque clearing, and real-time account operations.
National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI)View Answer Apex umbrella organization for retail payments established in 2008 by the RBI and IBA under the Payment and Settlement Systems Act, 2007.View Answer Development of indigenous, world-class payment systems such as IMPS, RuPay, UPI, BBPS, and AePS.
Unified Payments Interface (UPI)View Answer 24x7 instant inter-bank fund transfers and merchant QR payments via mobile number and Virtual Payment Address (VPA).View Answer India became the leader of the global digital retail payment revolution; sharp decline in cash dependency and unprecedented surge in the fintech ecosystem.
Central Bank Digital Currency (CBDC / e₹)View Answer Pilot testing of sovereign digital currency (Digital Rupee) by the RBI in wholesale (e₹-W) and retail (e₹-R) segments (since 2022).View Answer Reduction in printing and management costs of paper notes, faster cross-border payment settlements, and sovereign guarantee in financial security.

One-Liner Questions

In which year was the Narasimham Committee-II (Committee on Banking Sector Reforms) constituted by the Government of India?
In December 1997 (and the committee submitted its landmark report in April 1998).
Tap to view answer
What was the fundamental difference between the primary mandates of the Narasimham Committee-I (1991) and Narasimham Committee-II (1998)?
Committee-I focused on removing financial repression and liberalization, whereas Committee-II focused primarily on strengthening the banking system, institutional restructuring, and international financial integration.
Tap to view answer
To what percentage did the Narasimham Committee-II recommend raising banks' Capital Adequacy Ratio (CRAR) from 8%?
Recommended raising it to 9% by the year 2000 and 10% in the long term.
Tap to view answer
Which special concept did the Narasimham Committee-II propound to avert crises in weak and ailing banks?
'Narrow Banking', under which weak banks are barred from risky commercial lending and restricted to investing only in risk-free government securities.
Tap to view answer
To what percentage did the Narasimham Committee-II suggest reducing the Government of India's shareholding in public sector banks?
Recommended reducing it to a minimum of 33%, enabling banks to raise capital from the market and gain management autonomy.
Tap to view answer
What strict principle did the Narasimham Committee-II lay down regarding mergers of public sector banks?
Mergers should occur only between two strong banks for commercial synergy; a strong bank must never be forcibly merged with a weak bank on administrative grounds.
Tap to view answer
Which financial institution did the Narasimham Committee-II strongly recommend establishing for the speedy resolution of NPAs?
Asset Reconstruction Companies (ARCs).
Tap to view answer
What objection did the Narasimham Committee-II record regarding the dual role of the Reserve Bank of India (RBI)?
The RBI cannot simultaneously be the regulator of banks and the owner of public institutions (such as SBI, NHB, NABARD); hence, it should divest its ownership stakes.
Tap to view answer
When did the RBI sell its entire shareholding in the State Bank of India (SBI) to the Central Government on the recommendation of the Narasimham Committee-II?
In 2007-08 (SBI's 59.7% stake was transferred to the government for approximately ₹35,531 crore).
Tap to view answer
What legal reform did the Narasimham Committee-II suggest regarding cheque bouncing under the Negotiable Instruments Act, 1881?
Making cheque bouncing a strict criminal offense and adopting a special procedure for speedy disposal.
Tap to view answer
What recommendation did the Narasimham Committee-II make regarding the constitution of banks' Boards of Directors?
Completely eliminating political appointments on boards and inducting only professional, independent, and financial experts.
Tap to view answer
What liberal approach did the Narasimham Committee-II suggest adopting toward the expansion of foreign banks in India?
Allowing foreign banks to operate as Wholly Owned Subsidiaries (WOS) and acquire stakes in Indian private banks.
Tap to view answer
What autonomy did the Narasimham Committee-II seek regarding recruitment and human resource management in public sector banks?
Abolishing the Banking Service Recruitment Board (BSRB) and granting each bank the freedom to directly recruit talent at market rates.
Tap to view answer
In which year was the Banking Service Recruitment Board (BSRB) formally abolished?

Tap to view answer
What system did the Narasimham Committee-II suggest to manage banks' foreign exchange (Forex) exposure and interest rate risk?
Mandatory implementation of an Asset-Liability Management (ALM) system in every bank.
Tap to view answer
What directive did the Narasimham Committee-II give for the use of information technology in Indian banking?
Complete computerization of all major branches, inter-bank connectivity, and rapid expansion of e-banking.
Tap to view answer
What did the Narasimham Committee-II recommend to make the Banking Ombudsman Scheme more effective?

Tap to view answer
What model did the Narasimham Committee-II present for restructuring Regional Rural Banks (RRBs) in rural credit?

Which audit system did the Narasimham Committee-II recommend making mandatory for banks' internal control?

What suggestion did the Narasimham Committee-II reiterate regarding Priority Sector Lending (PSL)?

Tap to view answer

What strict target did the Narasimham Committee-II set for reducing banks' Net NPAs?
Bringing Net NPAs below 5% by 2000 and below 3% by 2002.

What did the Narasimham Committee-II state regarding incorporating Market Risk into banks' capital adequacy framework?
