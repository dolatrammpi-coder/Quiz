Key Facts and Contributions
Service Sector Part 2
Service Sector ActivityKey FeatureEconomic / Employment Impact
IT-BPM IndustryGlobal hub for software development, BPM, KPO, and digital transformation services; $250B+ revenue.Over 54 lakh highly skilled direct jobs, massive foreign exchange earnings, and a leading role in service exports.
Startups & Innovation EcosystemWorld's third-largest startup ecosystem; rapid innovation in FinTech, EdTech, HealthTech, and SaaS.100+ unicorn companies, lakhs of new direct and indirect jobs, and growth of entrepreneurship among youth.
E-commerce & Digital RetailB2B and B2C online marketplaces, digital payment (UPI) integration, and Direct-to-Consumer (D2C) brands.National market access for small sellers and MSMEs, and massive job creation in logistics and warehousing.
Healthcare & Medical ServicesPublic and private hospitals, telemedicine (eSanjeevani), and Medical Value Tourism.Human capital development, improved life expectancy, and positioning India as a global medical tourism hub.
Digital Public Infrastructure (DPI / India Stack)Open API-based digital public layers such as Aadhaar, UPI, DigiLocker, and Account Aggregator.Sharp reduction in financial inclusion costs, elimination of corruption and leakages, and democratization of the digital economy.
Tourism & Hospitality ServicesCultural, religious, business, and medical tourism; hotels, aviation, and travel services.6-7% contribution to GDP, over 8% of total employment, and a high employment multiplier effect.
Gig & Platform EconomyDigital app-based on-demand freelance services (ride-hailing, food delivery, home services).Flexible short-term employment for urban and semi-urban youth; protected under the Code on Social Security, 2020.
International Financial Services (GIFT City IFSC)India's first and only International Financial Services Centre; offshore financial transactions.Attracting global capital, bringing global financial giants to India, and creating high-value financial jobs.
