Concept and Definition of Unemployment

What is the standard definition of 'Unemployment' in economics?
The inability to find work despite being able and willing to work at the prevailing wage rate

Which age group is generally included in the working-age population for calculating unemployment?
The working-age labour force group of 15 to 59 years (or 64 years)
Tap to view answer
Which individuals are included in the Labour Force?
Those who are able and willing to work (whether they are employed or unemployed)

Which comprehensive survey is conducted by the NSO to measure unemployment?

Which three approaches were previously used by the NSSO to measure unemployment in India?
Usual Principal Status (UPS), Weekly Status (WSS), and Current Daily Status (CDS)

Over what reference period does Usual Principal Status (UPS) assess unemployment?
A long-term reference period of the preceding 365 days (one year)

Over what reference period does Weekly Status (WSS) assess unemployment?
A reference period of the preceding 7 days (one week)

What does Current Daily Status (CDS) unemployment measure?
The activity status on each day of the survey week (it is the most comprehensive measure of unemployment)

Open Unemployment

What is the meaning of Open Unemployment?
When an able and willing worker cannot find any work at all in the labour market
Tap to view answer
How does open unemployment affect an individual's economic condition?
The person is left completely without income and becomes dependent on others for livelihood
Tap to view answer
Where is open unemployment primarily observed in India?
In urban areas and among youth migrating from rural to urban areas
Tap to view answer
In what form does unemployment among the educated class (graduates and postgraduates) generally manifest?

What is considered the primary cause of open unemployment?
A slow rate of job creation relative to economic growth

Can data on open unemployment be easily recorded and measured?

How do rapid population growth in urban areas and rural migration affect open unemployment?

Is open unemployment a serious socio-economic problem for developing economies?

What psychological issues can arise among youth affected by open unemployment?

Does open unemployment rise during a slowdown in the industrial and service sectors?

Disguised Unemployment

What is meant by Disguised (or Hidden) Unemployment?
A situation where more people are engaged in a task than are actually required
Tap to view answer
What is the Marginal Productivity of surplus labour in disguised unemployment?
Zero
Tap to view answer
If disguisedly unemployed surplus workers are withdrawn from work, what will be the impact on total output?
Total output will not decline
Tap to view answer
In which sector is disguised unemployment most widespread in India?
In agriculture and allied rural sectors
Tap to view answer
What is the main cause of disguised unemployment in Indian agriculture?
Continuous subdivision of agricultural land, small landholding sizes, and lack of alternative employment
Tap to view answer
Do all family members appear to be working on the farm even when their labour is not actually needed?
Yes, this is precisely what is called hidden or disguised unemployment
Tap to view answer
Is disguised unemployment confined only to rural agriculture, or is it also found in urban informal sectors?

How does disguised unemployment affect Per Capita Income?
Per capita income becomes very low due to low labour productivity

What key insight did renowned economist Ragnar Nurkse offer regarding disguised unemployment?

Is disguised unemployment easily captured in conventional employment surveys?
No, because it is hidden and therefore difficult to measure
