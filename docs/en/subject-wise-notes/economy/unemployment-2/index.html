Structure of Employment in India

What has been the defining trend in the structure of employment in the Indian economy?
A gradual shift of labour from the primary sector (agriculture) toward the secondary (manufacturing) and tertiary (services) sectors

Youth Unemployment

Why is the problem of Youth Unemployment considered critical in India?
Because a large young population (demographic dividend) lacks adequate livelihood opportunities
Tap to view answer
Which age group is generally covered in youth unemployment statistics?
Youth in the 15 to 29 years age group
Tap to view answer
Why is the unemployment rate higher among educated youth than among unskilled youth?
Lack of market-relevant employability skills despite holding traditional academic degrees
Tap to view answer
Which fundamental flaw in the school and higher education system exacerbates youth unemployment?
Overemphasis on rote bookish knowledge and neglect of practical and vocational training
Tap to view answer
What is a major social reason behind 'Educated Youth Unemployment' in India?

Rural and Urban Unemployment

What is the fundamental difference between rural unemployment and urban unemployment?
Rural areas face more seasonal and disguised unemployment, whereas urban areas experience more open and educated unemployment

How does the Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Act (MGNREGA) reduce rural unemployment?
By providing a legal guarantee of at least 100 days of unskilled manual wage employment per financial year to every rural household
Tap to view answer
What is considered the biggest driver of unemployment in urban areas?

Female Labour Force Participation (FLFP)

What is the meaning of Female Labour Force Participation (FLFP) Rate?
The percentage of working-age women who are either employed or actively seeking work
Tap to view answer
How has India's FLFP rate historically compared with the global average and major developing countries?
Relatively quite low (Low FLFP Rate)
Tap to view answer
What does the 'U-shaped Curve Hypothesis' mean in the context of India's female labour force participation?
Women's participation initially falls as household income rises, and rises again at higher levels of education and income

What is a major social reason behind Indian women staying out of the labour market?
