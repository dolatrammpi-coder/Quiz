GST Structure & Core Concepts

Through which Constitutional Amendment Act was Goods and Services Tax (GST) introduced in India?
GST was incorporated into the Indian Constitution under the <strong class='highlight'>101st Constitutional Amendment Act, 2016</strong>.
Tap to view answer
What was the Bill number of the 101st Constitutional Amendment Bill in Parliament?
It was the <strong class='highlight'>122nd Constitutional Amendment Bill, 2014</strong>, which upon passage became the 101st Amendment Act.
Tap to view answer
From which date was Goods and Services Tax (GST) formally implemented in India?
GST came into effect in India from <strong class='highlight'>July 1, 2017</strong>.
Tap to view answer
What is the fundamental nature of GST as a tax?
GST is a comprehensive, multi-stage, <strong class='highlight'>destination-based indirect consumption tax</strong>.
Tap to view answer
Why is GST called a destination-based tax?
Because under this system, the final tax revenue accrues to the state <strong class='highlight'>where the final consumption of the good or service takes place</strong>.
Tap to view answer
On which country's model is India's Dual GST framework based?
India has adopted a Dual GST framework modeled on <strong class='highlight'>Canada</strong>.
Tap to view answer
What is the core meaning of the Dual GST model?
A system where both the <strong class='highlight'>Centre and the State Governments simultaneously and independently</strong> levy tax on the supply of goods and services.
Tap to view answer
Under which constitutional provision was compensation to states provided for implementing GST?
Under <strong class='highlight'>Section 18 of the 101st Constitutional Amendment Act</strong> and the GST (Compensation to States) Act, 2017.
Tap to view answer
Which Article of the Constitution grants concurrent power to the Centre and States to legislate on GST?
<strong class='highlight'>Article 246A</strong> empowers both Parliament and State Legislatures to make laws with respect to GST.
Tap to view answer
Which Article vests exclusive power in the Centre to levy and collect GST on inter-state trade?
Under <strong class='highlight'>Article 269A</strong>, IGST on inter-state supplies is levied and collected exclusively by the Central Government.
Tap to view answer
Under which Article of the Constitution is the GST Council constituted?
The GST Council is established by Presidential Order under <strong class='highlight'>Article 279A</strong> of the Constitution.
Tap to view answer
Who is the Ex-officio Chairperson of the GST Council?
The <strong class='highlight'>Union Finance Minister</strong> of India serves as the Ex-officio Chairperson of the GST Council.
Tap to view answer
Who represents the States in the GST Council?
The <strong class='highlight'>Minister in charge of Finance or Taxation</strong>, or any other Minister nominated by each State Government.
Tap to view answer
How is voting power divided between the Centre and the States in the GST Council?
The Central Government holds a weightage of <strong class='highlight'>1/3 (33.33%)</strong> of total votes cast, while all State Governments combined hold <strong class='highlight'>2/3 (66.67%)</strong>.
Tap to view answer
What majority is required to pass any decision in the GST Council?
A decision requires at least a <strong class='highlight'>3/4 weighted majority (75%)</strong> of the members present and voting.
Tap to view answer
What is the quorum required for meetings of the GST Council?
The presence of <strong class='highlight'>50% (one-half)</strong> of the total number of members of the GST Council constitutes the quorum.
Tap to view answer
Structurally, how many components are included in the GST system?
There are four main components: <strong class='highlight'>CGST, SGST, UTGST, and IGST</strong>.
Tap to view answer
What is the full form of CGST?
CGST stands for <strong class='highlight'>Central Goods and Services Tax</strong>.
Tap to view answer
What is the full form of SGST?
SGST stands for <strong class='highlight'>State Goods and Services Tax</strong>.
Tap to view answer
What is the full form of IGST?
IGST stands for <strong class='highlight'>Integrated Goods and Services Tax</strong>.
Tap to view answer
What is the full form of UTGST?
UTGST stands for <strong class='highlight'>Union Territory Goods and Services Tax</strong>.
Tap to view answer
What is meant by Intra-State supply?
When both the supplier and the recipient of goods or services are <strong class='highlight'>located within the same State or Union Territory</strong>.
Tap to view answer
Which taxes apply to an Intra-State supply?
On transactions within the same state, both <strong class='highlight'>CGST and SGST</strong> are levied simultaneously in equal proportion.
Tap to view answer
What is meant by Inter-State supply?
When the location of the supplier and the place of supply are in <strong class='highlight'>two different States or Union Territories</strong>.
Tap to view answer
Which tax is levied on an Inter-State supply?
Only <strong class='highlight'>IGST (Integrated GST)</strong> is levied on inter-state transactions.

Central Goods and Services Tax (CGST)

In Union Territories without a Legislature, what applies in place of SGST on intra-state supplies?
In such Union Territories, <strong class='highlight'>UTGST</strong> applies in place of SGST (alongside CGST).
Tap to view answer
Which tax applies in Union Territories with a Legislature (such as Delhi, Puducherry, and Jammu & Kashmir)?
In UTs with a Legislature, <strong class='highlight'>SGST</strong> applies just like in a State (alongside CGST).
Tap to view answer
If the total GST rate on a good is 18% and the sale is intra-state, what will be the CGST and SGST rates?
The CGST rate will be <strong class='highlight'>9%</strong> and the SGST rate will be <strong class='highlight'>9%</strong>.
Tap to view answer
If the GST rate on a good is 18% and the sale is inter-state, what will be the IGST rate?
The total IGST rate will be <strong class='highlight'>18%</strong>.
Tap to view answer
In which fund is the revenue collected under CGST deposited?
The entire revenue from CGST is deposited into the <strong class='highlight'>Consolidated Fund of India</strong>.
Tap to view answer
In which fund is the tax collected under SGST deposited?
It is deposited into the <strong class='highlight'>Consolidated Fund of the respective State</strong>.
Tap to view answer
How is the revenue collected as IGST apportioned?
The Central Government collects IGST and apportions it <strong class='highlight'>between the Centre and the destination State as per the recommendations of the GST Council</strong>.
Tap to view answer
How are imports of goods and services treated under the GST system?
Imports are treated as <strong class='highlight'>Inter-State Supplies</strong> and are subject to IGST.
Tap to view answer
How are exports of goods and services classified under the GST system?
Exports are accorded the status of <strong class='highlight'>'Zero-Rated Supply'</strong>.
Tap to view answer
What is the fundamental difference between a Zero-Rated Supply and an Exempt Supply?
In a Zero-Rated Supply, <strong class='highlight'>refund of Input Tax Credit (ITC) is available</strong>, whereas in an Exempt Supply, no ITC refund is permitted.
Tap to view answer
What is Input Tax Credit (ITC) under the GST regime?
The mechanism of <strong class='highlight'>deducting the tax already paid on inputs (raw materials or services) from the tax payable on the final output sale</strong>.
Tap to view answer
What is the primary economic objective of the ITC mechanism?
To completely eliminate tax-on-tax, i.e., the <strong class='highlight'>cascading effect of taxation</strong>.
Tap to view answer
According to GST rules, what is the prescribed order of priority for utilizing Input Tax Credit (ITC)?
First, the <strong class='highlight'>IGST credit must be exhausted completely</strong>, and only thereafter can CGST and SGST credits be utilized.
Tap to view answer
Can CGST credit be used to pay SGST liability?
<strong class='highlight'>No</strong>, cross-utilization of CGST credit to discharge SGST liability is strictly prohibited.
Tap to view answer
Can SGST credit be used to pay CGST liability?
<strong class='highlight'>No</strong>, SGST credit is strictly prohibited from being used to pay CGST liability.
Tap to view answer
Which tax liabilities can be discharged using IGST input credit?
IGST credit can be utilized in sequence to pay <strong class='highlight'>IGST, CGST, and then SGST/UTGST</strong> liabilities.
Tap to view answer
Which taxes can be paid using CGST input credit?
First towards <strong class='highlight'>CGST</strong> liability, and any remaining balance towards <strong class='highlight'>IGST</strong> liability.
Tap to view answer
Which taxes can be paid using SGST input credit?
First towards <strong class='highlight'>SGST</strong> liability, and any remaining balance towards <strong class='highlight'>IGST</strong> liability.
Tap to view answer
How many main tax slabs exist in the current Indian GST structure?
There are four primary tax slabs: <strong class='highlight'>5%, 12%, 18%, and 28%</strong> (along with 0% on essential items).
Tap to view answer
What is the special GST rate prescribed for precious metals like gold and silver?
A special GST rate of <strong class='highlight'>3%</strong> applies to precious metals.
Tap to view answer
What is the GST rate on rough precious stones?
A concessional GST rate of <strong class='highlight'>0.25%</strong> applies to rough precious stones.
Tap to view answer
What is levied over and above 28% GST on sin goods and luxury items?
A <strong class='highlight'>GST Compensation Cess</strong> is levied over and above the 28% tax rate on such goods.
Tap to view answer
For what purpose are the proceeds from the GST Compensation Cess utilized?
To <strong class='highlight'>compensate States for any revenue loss arising from the implementation of GST</strong>.
Tap to view answer
What was the Base Year for assessing states' revenue loss under the GST Compensation Act, 2017?
The base year was set as <strong class='highlight'>2015-16</strong>, assuming a projected annual revenue growth rate of 14%.
Tap to view answer
What was the original constitutional period fixed for providing revenue compensation to States?
For <strong class='highlight'>5 years (until June 30, 2022)</strong> from the date of GST rollout (July 1, 2017).

State Goods and Services Tax (SGST)

Which five petroleum products are currently kept outside the purview of GST?
<strong class='highlight'>Crude oil, petrol, high-speed diesel, natural gas, and Aviation Turbine Fuel (ATF)</strong>.
Tap to view answer
Who holds the authority to decide whether to bring petroleum products under GST?
They can be brought under GST only on the recommendations of the <strong class='highlight'>GST Council</strong>.
Tap to view answer
What is the constitutional basis for keeping alcohol for human consumption outside GST?
Under the <strong class='highlight'>101st Constitutional Amendment Act</strong> itself, Article 366(12A) explicitly excludes it from the definition of GST.
Tap to view answer
Who currently holds the power to tax alcohol for human consumption?
The exclusive right to tax it rests solely with <strong class='highlight'>State Governments (via State Excise Duty and VAT)</strong>.
Tap to view answer
Why does GST not apply to electricity at present?
Electricity is kept exempt from GST, and States levy <strong class='highlight'>Electricity Duty</strong> on it.
Tap to view answer
Which part of Real Estate attracts GST and which does not?
<strong class='highlight'>Under-construction properties</strong> attract GST, whereas completed properties (with a Completion Certificate) and sale of land are outside GST.
Tap to view answer
What is GSTN (Goods and Services Tax Network)?
It is a non-government, non-profit organization that operates the entire <strong class='highlight'>IT infrastructure and digital network portal</strong> for the GST system.
Tap to view answer
Under which law was GSTN incorporated?
Under <strong class='highlight'>Section 8</strong> of the Companies Act, 2013.
Tap to view answer
What is the current shareholding (ownership) structure of GSTN?
GSTN is a wholly government-owned company, with <strong class='highlight'>50% equity held by the Central Government and 50% jointly by State Governments/UTs</strong>.
Tap to view answer
What is the full form of GSTIN?
GSTIN stands for <strong class='highlight'>Goods and Services Tax Identification Number</strong>.
Tap to view answer
How many characters are there in a GSTIN alphanumeric code?
A GSTIN is a <strong class='highlight'>15-digit alphanumeric code</strong>.
Tap to view answer
What do the first two digits of a GSTIN represent?
The first two digits represent the <strong class='highlight'>State Code</strong> (for example, 09 for Uttar Pradesh).
Tap to view answer
What do the 10 characters from position 3 to 12 in a GSTIN represent?
They represent the taxpayer's <strong class='highlight'>PAN (Permanent Account Number)</strong>.
Tap to view answer
What does the 13th character of a GSTIN denote?
The 13th character denotes the <strong class='highlight'>Entity Number</strong> of the taxpayer's registrations within the same state.
Tap to view answer
What is the 14th character of a GSTIN by default?
The 14th character is the alphabet <strong class='highlight'>'Z'</strong> by default.
Tap to view answer
What is the purpose of the 15th character in a GSTIN?
The 15th character is a <strong class='highlight'>Check Digit</strong> used for error detection.
Tap to view answer
What is the mandatory turnover threshold for GST registration for suppliers of goods in normal states?
Registration is mandatory if annual aggregate turnover exceeds <strong class='highlight'>Rs. 40 lakh</strong>.
Tap to view answer
What is the GST registration threshold for service providers in normal states?
Registration is mandatory if annual aggregate turnover exceeds <strong class='highlight'>Rs. 20 lakh</strong>.

What is the minimum turnover threshold for GST registration in Special Category States (North-Eastern and hilly states)?
For Special Category States (such as Nagaland, Manipur, Mizoram, and Tripura), this threshold is fixed at <strong class='highlight'>Rs. 10 lakh</strong>.

What is the main objective of the Composition Scheme under GST?
To <strong class='highlight'>relieve small taxpayers from complex compliance paperwork</strong> and allow them to pay tax at a fixed low rate on turnover.
Tap to view answer
What is the maximum turnover limit to opt for the Composition Scheme?
Annual turnover must be up to <strong class='highlight'>Rs. 1.5 crore</strong> in normal states (Rs. 75 lakh for North-Eastern states).
Tap to view answer
Can a dealer opting for the Composition Scheme collect GST from customers?
<strong class='highlight'>No</strong>, a composition dealer can neither collect GST from consumers nor claim Input Tax Credit (ITC).
Tap to view answer
What is the GST rate for traders/manufacturers under the Composition Scheme?
<strong class='highlight'>1% of total turnover (0.5% CGST + 0.5% SGST)</strong>.
Tap to view answer
What is the GST rate for restaurant services under the Composition Scheme?
<strong class='highlight'>5% of turnover (2.5% CGST + 2.5% SGST)</strong>.
Tap to view answer
What is the Reverse Charge Mechanism (RCM)?
A mechanism where the liability to pay tax rests on the <strong class='highlight'>recipient of goods or services</strong> rather than the supplier.

Integrated Goods and Services Tax (IGST)

When a registered person procures goods or services from an <strong class='highlight'>unregistered seller</strong>, or in the case of specially notified services.

When is generating an E-Way Bill mandatory?
Whenever goods valued at more than Rs. 50,000 are transported for <strong class='highlight'>inter-state or intra-state movement</strong>.

HSN stands for <strong class='highlight'>Harmonized System of Nomenclature</strong>, used for the global classification of goods.

Which code is used in GST for the systematic classification of services?
<strong class='highlight'>SAC (Services Accounting Code)</strong> is used for classifying services.
Tap to view answer
What is the function of the Anti-Profiteering Mechanism in GST?
To ensure that the benefit of GST rate reductions or ITC is <strong class='highlight'>passed on to final consumers through lower prices</strong>.

Following the winding up of NAA, its functions have been entrusted to the <strong class='highlight'>Competition Commission of India (CCI)</strong>.

Taxation Part 2: Comparative Summary of Key Concepts, Processes, and Economic Impacts
Comparative Study of GST Components
GST ComponentKey CharacteristicRole in the Tax System
CGST (Central GST)Tax levied and collected by the Central Government on intra-state supplies.Strengthens federal revenue and provides financial resources to the Central Government.
SGST (State GST)Tax collected by the respective State Government on intra-state supplies.Enhances fiscal autonomy of states and safeguards state revenue sources.
IGST (Integrated GST)Tax collected by the Centre on inter-state trade between two states and on imports.Enforces the destination-based taxation principle and transfers revenue to the consuming state.
What is a Mixed Supply?
A combination of two or more individual goods or services that are not naturally bundled together but are sold for a single price (e.g., a packaged gift box containing juice, chocolates, and utensils).

Which GST rate applies to a Mixed Supply?
