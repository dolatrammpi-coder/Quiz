NITI Aayog Part 2

Comprehensive analysis of NITI Aayog's flagship programmes, SDG India Index, Aspirational Districts & Blocks Programmes, Atal Innovation Mission (AIM), and data-driven governance

Quick Reference
Key Facts & Contributions
NITI Aayog Part 2
NITI Aayog ProgrammePrimary ObjectiveContribution to Development
SDG India IndexView Answer Comprehensive data-driven measurement and classification of the progress of States and Union Territories toward Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs 2030).View Answer Promoting cooperative and competitive federalism; evidence-based setting of policy priorities and motivating lagging states to improve.
Aspirational Districts Programme (ADP)View Answer Rapid transformation of India's 112 most underdeveloped districts across 5 key socio-economic themes (health, education, agriculture, financial inclusion, infrastructure).View Answer Bridging regional disparities through the 3-C model of Convergence, Collaboration, and Competition, and ensuring last-mile delivery of basic services.
Aspirational Blocks Programme (ABP)View Answer Building on ADP's success, achieving hyper-local saturation of indicators across 500 most backward blocks in 329 districts.View Answer Taking administrative decentralization to the grassroots, fostering healthy competition at the block level, and delivering welfare benefits to the last person.
Atal Innovation Mission (AIM)View Answer Developing a comprehensive ecosystem of innovation, scientific curiosity, and entrepreneurship across schools, universities, and research institutions.View Answer Nurturing future tech entrepreneurs and innovators through 10,000+ Atal Tinkering Labs (ATLs) and Atal Incubation Centres (AICs).
National Multidimensional Poverty Index (National MPI)View Answer Multidimensional assessment of household deprivations across 12 indicators in health, education, and standard of living based on the Oxford OPHI methodology.View Answer Identifying real material deprivations beyond income-based poverty; tracking the exit of 24.8 crore people from multidimensional poverty between 2013-14 and 2022-23.
SATH Programme (Education & Health)View Answer Partnering with states for systemic structural restructuring of education and health systems and sustainable transformation of human capital.View Answer Qualitative leaps in school consolidation, learning outcomes, and primary healthcare efficiency in role-model states (Jharkhand, Odisha, Madhya Pradesh, etc.).
Atal New India Challenge (ANIC)View Answer Providing direct grants of up to ₹1 crore to startups and MSMEs to solve complex challenges of national importance and social relevance.View Answer Commercializing viable indigenous innovations in sectors such as clean energy, water management, agri-tech, and waste management.
Women Entrepreneurship Platform (WEP)View Answer A unified digital platform providing incubation, funding, mentorship, and market access to women entrepreneurs across the country.View Answer Bridging gender inequality, boosting Female Labour Force Participation Rate (FLFPR), and providing financial strength to women-owned micro and small enterprises.

One-Liner Questions

In which year was the first edition of the 'SDG India Index' released by NITI Aayog?
In December 2018 (in collaboration with the United Nations).

What is the primary objective of the SDG India Index?
To provide a comprehensive and objective assessment of the progress of all Indian States and Union Territories toward the UN 2030 Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs).
Tap to view answer
On what scale is the overall composite score of states measured in the SDG India Index?
On a scale of 0 to 100 (where 0 indicates minimum performance and 100 indicates full achievement of targets).
Tap to view answer
Into which four official categories are States and Union Territories classified based on their scores under the SDG India Index?
1. Aspirant (0-49), 2. Performer (50-64), 3. Front Runner (65-99), and 4. Achiever (100).

Out of the 17 Sustainable Development Goals, how many goals are evaluated at the state level in the SDG India Index?
A total of 16 goals (Goal 17 'Partnerships for the Goals' is measured only at the national level).

Which state has consistently held the top position in overall performance across editions of the SDG India Index?
Kerala.

Which Union Territory has consistently been the top performer among UTs in the SDG India Index?
Chandigarh.

What has been the biggest impact of the SDG India Index on Indian polity and administration?
