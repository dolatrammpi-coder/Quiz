Which German economist propounded the first scientific and classical theory of industrial location in 1909?
Alfred Weber ('Theory of the Location of Industries')

On which core concept is Alfred Weber's theory of industrial location based?
Least Transport Cost Theory

Which two physical variables did Weber consider most important in determining transport cost?
Weight of the material and Distance to be transported
Tap to view answer
What is the definition of 'Material Index' (MI) in Weber's theory?
Ratio of the weight of localized raw materials to the weight of the finished product (MI = Weight of Raw Material / Weight of Product)
Tap to view answer
According to Weber, when a raw material has a Material Index greater than 1 (MI > 1), what kind of raw material is it?
Weight-losing / Impure Material (such as sugarcane, iron ore)
Tap to view answer
Where should an industry based on weight-losing raw materials (MI > 1) be located according to Weber?
Near the source of raw materials (Material-oriented location — such as sugar mills)
Tap to view answer
When the Material Index is equal to or less than 1 (MI ≤ 1), what type of material is it called?
Pure or Ubiquitous Material (such as cotton, bakery products)
Tap to view answer
Where is it favourable to locate an industry based on pure raw materials (MI = 1)?
Near the market (Market-oriented) or anywhere along the transport route
