Godavari River (Dakshin Ganga)
View Answer
Originates from Trimbakeshwar in Nashik district of Maharashtra; flows 1,465 km eastward into the Bay of Bengal
View Answer
Drains Maharashtra, Telangana, Andhra Pradesh, Chhattisgarh & Odisha; Pranhita, Indravati, Manjira, Penganga & Wainganga are major tributaries; forms a broad delta below Rajahmundry
Krishna River
View Answer
Originates from Mahabaleshwar (1,338 m) in the Western Ghats of Maharashtra; 1,401 km eastward course
View Answer
Drains Maharashtra, Karnataka, Telangana & Andhra Pradesh; Tungabhadra, Bhima, Koyna, Panchganga, Malaprabha & Musi tributaries; Almatti, Srisailam & Nagarjuna Sagar dams
Kaveri River (Ganga of South India)
View Answer
Originates from Talakaveri (1,341 m) on the Brahmagiri Range in Kodagu (Coorg), Karnataka; 800 km course
View Answer
Drains Karnataka, Kerala & Tamil Nadu; forms Sivasamudram & Srirangapatna river islands; Amaravati, Bhavani, Hemavati & Kabini tributaries; Mettur Dam & Poompuhar delta
Mahanadi River
View Answer
Originates from the Sihawa range in Dhamtari district of Chhattisgarh; 851 km eastward drainage
View Answer
Drains Chhattisgarh & Odisha; Seonath, Hasdeo, Mand, Ib, Jonk & Tel tributaries; world-famous Hirakud Dam near Sambalpur; forms a vast delta at Cuttack
Narmada River
View Answer
Originates from the Amarkantak Plateau (1,057 m) in Anuppur district of Madhya Pradesh; flows 1,312 km westward in a rift valley between Vindhya & Satpura
View Answer
Drains Madhya Pradesh, Maharashtra & Gujarat; Bhedaghat Marble Rocks & Dhuandhar Falls; Tawa, Shakkar & Dudhi tributaries; Sardar Sarovar Dam & estuary near Bharuch
Tapti (Tapi) River
View Answer
Originates from the Satpura range near Multai (752 m) in Betul district of Madhya Pradesh; 724 km westward course
View Answer
Drains Madhya Pradesh, Maharashtra & Gujarat; flows in a rift valley between Satpura & Ajanta ranges; Purna, Girna & Panjhra tributaries; Ukai & Kakrapar projects; drains into Gulf of Khambhat near Surat

River / Water BodyFlow / LocationKey Facts & Basin
Kunchikal Falls (455 m)
View Answer
Cascading waterfall located on the Varahi River in the Western Ghats in Shimoga district of Karnataka
View Answer
Highest tiered/cascading waterfall in India; primary water source for the Varahi Underground Hydroelectric Project
Jog / Gersoppa Falls (253 m)
View Answer
Waterfall on the Sharavati River in Shimoga district of Karnataka descending in four distinct cascades (Raja, Rani, Rocket, Roarer)
View Answer
Also known as Mahatma Gandhi Waterfall; one of India's highest untiered plunge waterfalls
Sivasamudram Falls (98 m)
View Answer
Segmented twin waterfall (Gaganachukki and Bharachukki) on the Kaveri River along the Mandya-Chamarajanagar border in Karnataka
View Answer
Site of Asia's first major hydroelectric power station (commissioned in 1902), supplying electricity to Mysore and the Kolar Gold Fields
Dhuandhar Falls (30 m)
View Answer
Located on the Narmada River through the narrow marble gorge at Bhedaghat in Jabalpur district of Madhya Pradesh
View Answer
Named 'Dhuandhar' (smoke cascade) due to the thick mist created by plunging water; major international tourist attraction
Chitrakote Falls (29 m)
View Answer
300-metre-wide horseshoe-shaped waterfall on the Indravati River near Jagdalpur in Bastar district of Chhattisgarh
View Answer
Popularly called the 'Niagara of India' owing to its wide horseshoe span and massive monsoon discharge
Hundru Falls (98 m)
View Answer
Formed by the Subarnarekha River descending the scarp of the Ranchi Plateau in Ranchi district of Jharkhand
View Answer
Classic geomorphic example of knickpoint rejuvenation and scarp fall; supports the Subarnarekha Hydroelectric Project
Chilika Lake
View Answer
India's largest brackish-water coastal lagoon situated south of the Mahanadi delta at the mouth of the Daya and Bhargavi rivers in Odisha
View Answer
Designated as India's first Ramsar Wetland of International Importance (1981); hosts Nalbana Bird Sanctuary and endangered Irrawaddy dolphins
Vembanad Lake
View Answer
96-km-long Kayal (backwater lagoon) running parallel to the Arabian Sea coast across Kottayam, Alappuzha & Ernakulam in Kerala
View Answer
Longest lake in India; houses Willingdon Island (Kochi Port) and hosts the famous Nehru Trophy Boat Race (Vallamkali)
Lonar Lake
View Answer
Formed by a hypervelocity meteorite impact crater in the Deccan basaltic traps in Buldhana district of Maharashtra
View Answer
Saline and highly alkaline crater lake; declared a Ramsar site in 2020; water turns pink due to Haloarchaea microbes
Loktak Lake
View Answer
Largest natural freshwater wetland lake in North-East India, located in Bishnupur district of Manipur
View Answer
Famous for floating heterogeneous biomass islands called 'Phumdis'; hosts 'Keibul Lamjao', the world's only floating national park

Geologically, how does the Peninsular drainage system compare with the Himalayan drainage system?
It is much older and has reached the mature stage of fluvial development

How do the valleys of Peninsular rivers compare with the deep V-shaped valleys of Himalayan rivers?
They are broad, shallow, and have a very gentle, graded longitudinal profile

On what does the water flow of Peninsular rivers primarily depend?

Why do most rivers of Peninsular India flow from west to east and drain into the Bay of Bengal?
