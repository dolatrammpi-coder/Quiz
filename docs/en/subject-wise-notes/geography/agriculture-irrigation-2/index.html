Quick Reference
Important Facts and Contributions
Agriculture Irrigation 2
Crop / Agricultural RegionRequired Geographical ConditionsMajor Regions
Tube-well & Well Irrigation
View Answer
Soft alluvial soil, high water table, and abundant electricity/diesel supply; highest share (~62–64%) in total irrigated area
View Answer
Uttar Pradesh (highest number of tube-wells in India), Rajasthan, Punjab, Madhya Pradesh, Gujarat, Bihar
Canal Irrigation
View Answer
Extensive flat plains, perennial (Himalayan) rivers, impervious alluvial soil; accounts for ~24–26% of total irrigated area
View Answer
Uttar Pradesh (largest canal-irrigated area), Rajasthan (Indira Gandhi Canal), Andhra Pradesh, Punjab, Haryana
Tank Irrigation
View Answer
Hard impervious igneous and metamorphic rocky terrain, natural depressions, storage of seasonal monsoon rain; non-alluvial plateaus
View Answer
Tamil Nadu (leader in tank irrigation), Andhra Pradesh, Telangana, Karnataka, Odisha
Micro-Irrigation (Drip & Sprinkler)
View Answer
Low-rainfall arid/semi-arid regions, undulating sandy terrain, acute water scarcity; 40–70% water saving & up to 90% water-use efficiency
View Answer
Rajasthan, Maharashtra, Andhra Pradesh, Gujarat, Karnataka, Haryana
Western Himalayan Agro-Climatic Region
View Answer
Cold temperate to alpine climate, steep slopes, skeletal and forest soils, rainfall 100–250 cm; terrace farming & temperate horticulture
View Answer
Jammu & Kashmir, Ladakh, Himachal Pradesh, Uttarakhand (apples, walnuts, saffron, basmati)
Western Dry Agro-Climatic Region
View Answer
Extremely high summer temperatures, rainfall below 30 cm, intense evaporation, sandy desert soil; completely dependent on canal/tube-well irrigation
View Answer
Western Rajasthan (Thar Desert — Barmer, Jaisalmer, Bikaner, Jodhpur)
