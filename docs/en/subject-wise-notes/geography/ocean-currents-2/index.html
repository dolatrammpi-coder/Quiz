Ocean Currents - Part 2 (Ocean Currents 2)

Currents of the Pacific and Indian Oceans (Kuroshio, Oyashio, Agulhas, Somali) and Impact on Local Climate & Fishing

🔍
Quick Reference
Important Facts & Contributions
Ocean Currents 2
CurrentOcean / RegionMajor Impact
Kuroshio Current (Japan Current)
View Answer
North Pacific Ocean (from the Philippines and Taiwan along the eastern coast of Japan); Warm Current
View Answer
Warms the coastal climate of Japan and moderates winter temperatures; fast, deep indigo-blue current ('Black Stream') analogous to the Atlantic Gulf Stream
Oyashio (Kurile) Current
View Answer
North Pacific Ocean (from the Bering Strait and Sea of Okhotsk southward to Hokkaido, Japan); Cold Current
View Answer
Lowers temperatures along northern Japan; meets the warm Kuroshio to produce dense advection fog and one of the world's richest fishing banks
California Current
View Answer
Eastern North Pacific Ocean (along the western coast of the USA and Baja California, Mexico); Cold Current
View Answer
Drives coastal upwelling, lowers summer temperatures, produces coastal fog (San Francisco), and contributes to the Mojave and Sonoran deserts
Peru (Humboldt) Current
View Answer
South-Eastern Pacific Ocean (along the western coast of Chile and Peru); Cold Current
View Answer
Intense nutrient upwelling supports the world's largest anchovy fishery; primary cause of the extreme aridity of the Atacama Desert
East Australian Current (EAC)
View Answer
South-Western Pacific Ocean (flowing southward along the eastern coast of Australia); Warm Current
View Answer
Raises temperature and humidity along eastern Australia (Brisbane, Sydney), promotes coastal rainfall, and sustains the Great Barrier Reef
Agulhas Current
View Answer
South-Western Indian Ocean (along the south-eastern coast of Mozambique and South Africa); Warm Current
View Answer
Formed by the confluence of the Mozambique and Madagascar currents; warms the Durban coast and generates hazardous rogue waves off Cape Agulhas
Somali Current
View Answer
North-Western Indian Ocean (along the Horn of Africa / Somalia and Oman coast); Seasonally Reversing Current
View Answer
Flows northward in summer as a fast, cold upwelling current under the SW Monsoon; reverses southward in winter as a slower warm current under the NE Monsoon
West Australian Current
View Answer
South-Eastern Indian Ocean (flowing northward along the western coast of Australia); Cold Current
View Answer
Lowers coastal temperatures, suppresses evaporation, and contributes to the Great Sandy, Gibson, and Great Victoria deserts of Western Australia

Important Facts & Contributions
Ocean Currents 2
Current / PhenomenonOcean / RegionMajor Impact
El Niño
View Answer
Central and Eastern Equatorial Pacific Ocean (off the coast of Peru and Ecuador); Periodic Warm Water Anomaly
View Answer
Suppresses the cold Humboldt Current and upwelling; causes plankton/fishery collapse in Peru, heavy rain in South America, and drought risk in India
La Niña
View Answer
Central and Eastern Equatorial Pacific Ocean; Periodic Cold Water Anomaly
View Answer
Intensifies cold upwelling off Peru; boosts Peruvian fisheries, brings abundant monsoon rainfall to India, and increases flood risk in Australia
Tsushima Current
View Answer
North-Western Pacific Ocean (western branch of the Kuroshio entering the Sea of Japan via the Korea Strait); Warm Current
View Answer
Keeps western Japanese ports ice-free in winter and supplies moisture for heavy winter snowfall along the 'Snow Country' coast of western Japan
Mozambique Current
View Answer
Western Indian Ocean (flowing southward through the Mozambique Channel between Madagascar and Africa); Warm Current
View Answer
Maintains warm, humid conditions along the East African coast and merges with the East Madagascar Current to form the Agulhas Current
North-East Monsoon Drift
View Answer
Northern Indian Ocean (Arabian Sea and Bay of Bengal); Winter Seasonal Current
View Answer
Driven westward (counter-clockwise gyre) by the North-East Monsoon winds from November to March; aids winter rainfall along the Coromandel Coast

What is the periodic abnormal warming of surface ocean waters off the coast of Peru and Ecuador—typically peaking around Christmas every 2 to 7 years—called?
El Niño (Spanish for 'The Little Boy' or 'Christ Child')

What happens to the normal cold Humboldt Current and coastal upwelling off Peru during an El Niño event?
The cold Humboldt Current and nutrient upwelling are suppressed/replaced by warm equatorial water, raising sea surface temperatures by 3°C to 8°C

What devastating impact does a strong El Niño have on Peru's marine ecosystem and economy?
Nutrient upwelling stops, phytoplankton die off, anchovy fish stocks collapse or migrate away, and millions of seabirds starve

What adverse impact does El Niño generally have on India's summer South-West Monsoon?
It weakens the Walker Circulation and Indian Monsoon winds, often causing below-normal rainfall and drought in India

In contrast to El Niño, what is the periodic abnormal cooling of surface waters in the central and eastern equatorial Pacific Ocean called?

What favourable impact does a La Niña event generally have on the Indian Monsoon?
It strengthens the Walker Circulation and South-West Monsoon, bringing normal to above-normal rainfall across India (and heavy rain in Australia/Indonesia)

What is the coupled ocean-atmosphere phenomenon combining El Niño/La Niña ocean temperature shifts with the Southern Oscillation of air pressure called?

What is the most unique geographical feature of the Indian Ocean that distinguishes its northern circulation from the Atlantic and Pacific Oceans?

How many times a year does the surface current system of the Northern Indian Ocean completely reverse direction by ~180°?

Why are almost all major hot tropical deserts of the world situated along the western margins of continents between 15° and 30° latitudes?

What climatic impact do Warm Ocean Currents flowing along the eastern margins of continents between 20° and 40° latitudes have?
They raise coastal temperatures and humidity, producing warm, wet summers and supporting humid subtropical / monsoon climates (e.g., Gulf Stream, Kuroshio, Brazil, Agulhas, EAC)

How do warm and cold ocean currents contrastingly affect high-latitude seaports during winter?

What is meant by a 'Positive Indian Ocean Dipole' (Positive IOD) phase?
When sea surface temperatures in the western Indian Ocean (Arabian Sea / off East Africa) become warmer than normal while the eastern Indian Ocean (off Indonesia) becomes cooler than normal

What effect does a Positive Indian Ocean Dipole (Positive IOD) have on the Indian Summer Monsoon?

What happens during a 'Negative Indian Ocean Dipole' (Negative IOD) phase?
