What is the mathematical reason behind the 2-hour difference in local time between Arunachal Pradesh and Gujarat?
A longitudinal difference of approximately 30° (1° longitude = 4 minutes; hence 30 × 4 = 120 minutes)

Which meridian is recognized as India's Standard Meridian or Indian Standard Time (IST)?
82°30' East Longitude (82.5° E Longitude)
Tap to view answer
Why did the Government of India choose 82°30' East longitude as the Standard Meridian?
Because it passes roughly through the middle of the country and is an exact multiple of the global standard interval of 7°30' (30 minutes)
Tap to view answer
By how much is Indian Standard Time (IST) ahead of Greenwich Mean Time (GMT / UTC)?

Tap to view answer
Why is IST 5 hours 30 minutes ahead of Greenwich Mean Time?
82.5° × 4 minutes = 330 minutes = 5 hours 30 minutes (being east of the Prime Meridian, time is ahead)
