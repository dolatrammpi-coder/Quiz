Wildlife Conservation in India: National Parks, Tiger Reserves and Elephant Reserves (Wildlife 1)

Wildlife Protection Act, Major National Parks and Wildlife Sanctuaries

🔍

One-Liner Questions

When was the Wild Life (Protection) Act (WPA) enacted in India?
On 9 September 1972.
Tap to view answer
How many Schedules were there in the original framework of the Wild Life (Protection) Act, 1972?
6 Schedules.
Tap to view answer
To how many Schedules was the count rationalized under the Wild Life (Protection) Amendment Act, 2022?
4 Schedules (effective from 1 April 2023).
Tap to view answer
Which species are included in Schedule I of the amended Wild Life (Protection) Act?
Endangered wildlife species accorded the highest level of legal protection.
Tap to view answer
To what does Schedule III of the amended Wild Life (Protection) Act (2022) pertain?
Protected plant species.
Tap to view answer
What has been incorporated into Schedule IV under the Wild Life (Protection) Amendment Act, 2022?
Specimens listed in the Appendices under CITES (Scheduled Specimens).
Tap to view answer
Under which Article of the Indian Constitution is the protection and improvement of forests and wildlife included as a Directive Principle of State Policy?
Article 48A (inserted by the 42nd Constitutional Amendment Act, 1976).
Tap to view answer
Under which Article of the Constitution is it a Fundamental Duty of every citizen to protect wildlife and have compassion for living creatures?
Article 51A(g).
Tap to view answer
To which List of the Seventh Schedule were 'Forests' and 'Protection of Wild Animals and Birds' transferred by the 42nd Constitutional Amendment (1976)?
Concurrent List (Entries 17A and 17B).
Tap to view answer
Who serves as the ex-officio Chairperson of the National Board for Wildlife (NBWL)?
The Prime Minister of India.
Tap to view answer
Who chairs the State Board for Wildlife (SBWL)?
The Chief Minister of the respective state (or Administrator in UTs).
Tap to view answer
By which amendment act was the National Tiger Conservation Authority (NTCA) established as a statutory body?
Wild Life (Protection) Amendment Act, 2006 (under Section 38L).
Tap to view answer
Where is the headquarters of the Wildlife Crime Control Bureau (WCCB) located?
New Delhi (established in 2007).
Tap to view answer
Through which year's amendment to the WPA was the Central Zoo Authority (CZA) established?
By the 1991 Amendment Act (constituted in 1992).
Tap to view answer
Under which Section of the Wild Life (Protection) Act is a 'National Park' notified?
Section 35.
Tap to view answer
Under which Sections of the Wild Life (Protection) Act is a 'Wildlife Sanctuary' declared?
Section 18 and Section 26A.
Tap to view answer
Whose recommendation is mandatory for altering the boundaries of a National Park or a Wildlife Sanctuary?
The National Board for Wildlife (NBWL).
Tap to view answer
What is the legal status of human activities and livestock grazing inside a National Park?
Strictly prohibited (no private rights or livestock grazing are permitted).
Tap to view answer
Who is empowered to regulate or permit limited grazing or community rights inside a Wildlife Sanctuary?
The Chief Wildlife Warden.
Tap to view answer
On what category of land is a 'Community Reserve' established?
On private or community land (under Section 36C, introduced by the 2002 Amendment).
Tap to view answer
In which areas are 'Conservation Reserves' declared?
On government-owned lands adjacent to National Parks and Sanctuaries that serve as buffer zones or corridors.
Tap to view answer
Which was notified as the first Community Reserve in India?
Keshopur-Chhamb Community Reserve (Gurdaspur, Punjab, 2007).
Tap to view answer
What is the full form of CITES?
Convention on International Trade in Endangered Species of Wild Fauna and Flora (entered into force in 1975).
Tap to view answer
What is the primary mandate of the organization 'TRAFFIC'?
Monitoring wildlife trade to ensure it is legal and sustainable (a joint programme of WWF and IUCN established in 1976).
Tap to view answer
Into how many main categories are species classified in the IUCN 'Red List of Threatened Species'?
9 categories (Extinct, Extinct in the Wild, Critically Endangered, Endangered, Vulnerable, Near Threatened, Least Concern, Data Deficient, Not Evaluated).
Tap to view answer
In which year was India's first National Wildlife Action Plan (NWAP) adopted?
In 1983.
Tap to view answer
Which National Wildlife Action Plan is currently in operation in India?
The Third National Wildlife Action Plan (2017–2031).
Tap to view answer
What are Eco-Sensitive Zones (ESZ) notified under the Environment (Protection) Act, 1986?
Fragile transition 'shock-absorber' zones extending up to 10 km around National Parks and Wildlife Sanctuaries.
Tap to view answer
What are some prominent enforcement operations conducted by the Wildlife Crime Control Bureau (WCCB)?
Operation Save Kurma (turtles), Operation Lesknow, Operation Thunderbird, and Operation Clean Art (mongoose hair brushes).
Tap to view answer
Which law imposes a complete ban on the hunting of scheduled wild animals and illegal extraction of specified endemic plants in India?
The Wild Life (Protection) Act, 1972.
Tap to view answer
Into which National Park were African Cheetahs translocated from Namibia and South Africa under Project Cheetah?
Kuno National Park (Madhya Pradesh).
Tap to view answer
In which year was the Asiatic Cheetah officially declared extinct in India?
In 1952.
Tap to view answer
On which date was the first batch of cheetahs released into Kuno National Park?
On 17 September 2022.
Tap to view answer
On which date was the Gangetic River Dolphin declared the National Aquatic Animal of India?
On 5 October 2009 (5 October is observed annually as 'National Dolphin Day').
Tap to view answer
In which year was the Indian Elephant declared the National Heritage Animal of India?
In 2010.
