BiomeGeographical ExtentCharacteristic Flora & Features
Taiga Biome (Boreal Coniferous Forest)
View Answer
Between 50°–70° N latitudes across Eurasia (Siberia, Scandinavia) & North America (Canada, Alaska)
View Answer
Evergreen needle-leaf coniferous trees; Pine, Spruce, Larch, Fir, Birch & Alder; world's largest reservoir of commercial softwood timber
Tundra Biome (Polar Barren Lands)
View Answer
Arctic coastal belt of Canada, Greenland, and Siberia north of the Arctic Circle
View Answer
Treeless landscape dominated by Mosses, Lichens, Reindeer Moss, Sedges, Arctic Willow (Dwarf Willow) & Dwarf Birch shrubs over Permafrost
Savanna Biome (Tropical Grasslands)
View Answer
Africa (Sudan, Kenya, Tanzania), South America (Llanos, Campos) & Northern Australia
View Answer
Tall, coarse Elephant Grass; scattered umbrella-canopied Acacia trees & bottle-trunked Baobab trees; 'Parkland' landscape
Temperate Grassland Biome (Steppe / Prairie)
View Answer
Mid-latitude continental interiors: Prairies (USA/Canada), Pampas (Argentina), Steppes (Eurasia), Veld & Downs
View Answer
Nutritious short, soft turf grasses; Alfalfa, Fescue, Bluegrass; practically treeless; 'Breadbaskets of the World' (extensive wheat farming)
Mediterranean Biome (Sclerophyllous Woodland)
View Answer
Western margins of continents between 30°–45° N/S: Mediterranean Basin, California, Central Chile, Cape Province & SW Australia
View Answer
Evergreen drought-resistant shrubs and trees with thick leathery waxy leaves (Maquis & Chaparral); Olive, Cork Oak, Fig, Grapevine & Citrus fruits
