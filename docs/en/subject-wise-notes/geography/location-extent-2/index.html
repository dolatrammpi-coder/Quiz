India-Bangladesh Border

Total length: 4,096.7 km; borders 5 states (West Bengal, Assam, Meghalaya, Tripura, Mizoram)

View Answer

View Answer
McMahon Line (1914 Simla Accord); three sectors (Western, Middle, Eastern); Chinese claims over Aksai Chin, Galwan Valley, Pangong Tso (Fingers dispute), Doklam tri-junction & Tawang; Guarded by: ITBP
India-Pakistan Border (LoC/IB)
View Answer
Total length: 3,323 km; borders Gujarat, Rajasthan, Punjab, Jammu & Kashmir & Ladakh
View Answer
Radcliffe Line (1947); Line of Control (LoC — 1972 Simla Agreement); 96 km Sir Creek tidal dispute; Siachen Glacier (Operation Meghdoot 1984, Point NJ9842 & AGPL); Guarded by: BSF & Indian Army
India-Nepal Border
View Answer
Total length: 1,751 km; borders Uttarakhand, Uttar Pradesh, Bihar, West Bengal & Sikkim
View Answer
Treaty of Sugauli (1816); open border (visa-free); Mahakali River demarcation; Kalapani, Lipulekh & Limpiyadhura (Pithoragarh) and Susta area (Gandak River) disputes; Guarded by: Sashastra Seema Bal (SSB)
India-Myanmar Border
View Answer
Total length: 1,643 km; borders Arunachal Pradesh, Nagaland, Manipur & Mizoram
View Answer
Arakan Yoma & Patkai natural boundary; termination of Free Movement Regime (FMR) & border fencing decision; Kaladan Multi-Modal Transit Transport Project (Sittwe Port); Guarded by: Assam Rifles
India-Bhutan Border
View Answer
Total length: 699 km; borders Assam, West Bengal, Sikkim & Arunachal Pradesh
View Answer
India-Bhutan Treaty of Friendship (1949/2007); completely peaceful open border; strategic security coordination at Doklam Plateau tri-junction; Guarded by: Sashastra Seema Bal (SSB)
India-Afghanistan Border
View Answer
Total length: 106 km; borders Ladakh (in Pakistan-Occupied Kashmir — POK region)
View Answer
Durand Line (demarcated in 1893 by Sir Mortimer Durand); India's shortest international land border; touches the Wakhan Corridor
