Important Facts and Contributions
Mineral Energy 1
ResourceType / CharacteristicMajor Uses
Iron Ore (Hematite & Magnetite)
View Answer
Metallic (Ferrous); Dharwar & Cuddapah rock systems; Hematite (red ore, 60–70% Fe) and Magnetite (black magnetic ore, ~72% Fe)
View Answer
Primary basic raw material for the iron and steel industry, heavy machinery, automobiles, bridges, and railway construction
Manganese (Pyrolusite & Psilomelane)
View Answer
Metallic (Ferro-alloy); Dharwar system; dense concentration in Madhya Pradesh (Balaghat) & Maharashtra (Nagpur-Bhandara)
View Answer
Steel manufacturing (~10 kg manganese per tonne of steel), ferro-manganese alloys, dry cell batteries, bleaching powder, and glass industry
Bauxite (Aluminium Ore)
View Answer
Metallic (Non-ferrous); associated with Tertiary laterite rocks; Odisha (Koraput-Panchpatmali & Kalahandi) produces 50%+ of India's total
View Answer
Extraction of aluminium metal; aviation industry, electrical cables & appliances, automobile bodies, foils, and utensils
Copper (Chalcopyrite & Malachite)
View Answer
Metallic (Non-ferrous); highly ductile, malleable & excellent electrical conductor; Malanjkhand (Balaghat, MP) & Khetri (Rajasthan)
View Answer
Electrical cables and wires, electronics, transformers, generators, copperware, and brass & bronze alloys
Mica (Muscovite & Biotite)
View Answer
Non-metallic (sheet/layered mineral); high dielectric strength, transparent, heat-resistant & excellent electrical insulator
View Answer
Electrical & electronic industries, condensers, heating coils, insulators, radar equipment, paints, varnishes, and cosmetics
Limestone (Calcium Carbonate)
View Answer
Non-metallic (sedimentary rock mineral); Vindhyan & Cuddapah limestone beds; Rajasthan (Chittorgarh) & MP (Katni-Satna) lead
View Answer
Primary raw material for cement industry (60–65%), flux in iron-steel blast furnaces, sugar refining, fertilizers, and chemicals
Dolomite (CaMg(CO₃)₂)
View Answer
Non-metallic (magnesium-rich limestone); Odisha (Sundargarh-Birmitrapur) & Chhattisgarh (Bilaspur) are major producers
View Answer
Refractory brick linings for iron-steel furnaces, ferro-alloys, glass manufacturing, and soil conditioning
Gypsum (CaSO₄·2H₂O)
View Answer
Non-metallic (hydrated calcium sulphate); Rajasthan holds ~99% monopoly (Jamsar in Bikaner is India's largest mine; Nagaur)
View Answer
Retarder in cement setting, Plaster of Paris (POP), gypsum boards, ammonium sulphate fertilizer, and reclamation of alkaline (Usar) soils

Mineral Energy 1
Resource / BeltType / CharacteristicMajor Minerals & Significance
North-Eastern Peninsular Belt
View Answer
Chota Nagpur Plateau, Odisha, West Bengal & Chhattisgarh; the richest 'Mineral Heart of India'
View Answer
Iron ore, coking coal, manganese, bauxite, mica, copper, and limestone; concentrates 80%+ of India's heavy industries
Central Mineral Belt
View Answer
Chhattisgarh, Madhya Pradesh, eastern Maharashtra & Andhra Pradesh; junction of Vindhyan and Dharwar formations
View Answer
High-grade Bailadila iron ore, Balaghat manganese & copper (Malanjkhand), bauxite (Amarkantak), and India's sole diamond reserve at Panna
Southern Mineral Belt
View Answer
Karnataka Plateau & Tamil Nadu uplands; ancient Archaean and Dharwar crystalline rocks
View Answer
High-grade iron ore (Ballari-Sandur, Kudremukh), gold (Hutti & Kolar mines), bauxite (Shevaroy), and Neyveli lignite coal
South-Western Belt
View Answer
Southern Karnataka & entire Goa state; laterite and Dharwar formations of the Western Ghats
View Answer
Iron ore (exported to Japan via Goa's Mormugao Port), manganese, and bauxite; high logistical & water-transport advantage
North-Western Mineral Belt
View Answer
Rajasthan & Gujarat; Aravalli mountain system & Kutch-Kathiawar basin
View Answer
Non-ferrous metals (Lead-Zinc: Zawar/Rampura-Agucha; Copper: Khetri), gypsum, marble, granite, rock phosphate, lignite, and petroleum
Lead-Zinc (Galena & Sphalerite)
View Answer
Twin Minerals; Rajasthan holds ~90%+ monopoly; Rampura-Agucha in Bhilwara is world-famous
View Answer
Zinc: galvanization to prevent rusting of iron, brass alloy; Lead: batteries, cable sheathing, radiation shielding, and ammunition
