Renewable Energy and Nuclear Minerals in India (Mineral Energy 2)

Energy Minerals (Coal, Petroleum, Natural Gas), Nuclear Energy Minerals (Uranium, Thorium) and Renewable Energy Sources (Solar and Wind Energy)

🔍
Quick Reference
Important Facts and Contributions
Mineral Energy 2
ResourceMajor RegionsMajor Uses
Gondwana Coal (Bituminous)
View Answer
Damodar Valley (Jharia, Raniganj, Bokaro), Son Valley (Singrauli), Mahanadi Valley (Talcher) & Godavari Valley (Singareni)
View Answer
Over 98% of India's total coal production; power generation in thermal power plants, smelting in iron-steel blast furnaces, and cement industry
Tertiary Coal & Lignite
View Answer
Tamil Nadu (Neyveli — 90%+ of lignite reserves), Rajasthan (Palana, Barsingsar), Gujarat (Umarsar) & Assam (Makum)
View Answer
Brown coal (lower carbon, higher moisture); Neyveli lignite thermal power stations, fertilizer manufacturing, gas, and local industrial fuel
Petroleum (Crude Mineral Oil)
View Answer
Western Offshore (Bombay High, Bassein), Rajasthan (Barmer — Mangala, Bhagyam), Gujarat (Ankleshwar, Kalol) & Assam (Digboi, Naharkatiya)
View Answer
Transport fuels (petrol, diesel, ATF), petrochemical industry, lubricants, synthetic rubber, plastics, and petroleum jelly
Natural Gas
View Answer
Eastern Offshore (Krishna-Godavari Basin / KG-D6), Western Offshore (Bassein, Mumbai High), Rajasthan (Jaisalmer) & Assam
View Answer
CNG (transport fuel), PNG (domestic cooking gas), feedstock for urea manufacturing in fertilizer plants, and gas-based power stations
Uranium (Pitchblende / Uraninite)
View Answer
Jharkhand (Jaduguda, Narwapahar, Turamdih), Andhra Pradesh (Tummalapalle) & Rajasthan (Rohil, Sikar)
View Answer
Electricity generation via controlled nuclear fission in nuclear reactors, cancer radiotherapy, isotope production, and defence applications
Thorium (Monazite Sand)
View Answer
Malabar Coast of Kerala (Kollam, Alappuzha), Odisha Coast (Chhatrapur) & Visakhapatnam coast of Andhra Pradesh
View Answer
Future nuclear fuel for the third stage (Thorium-based breeder reactors) of India's Three-Stage Nuclear Power Programme

Important Facts and Contributions
Mineral Energy 2
ResourceMajor RegionsMajor Uses
Solar Energy
View Answer
Rajasthan (Bhadla Solar Park — 2,245 MW), Karnataka (Pavagada — 2,050 MW), Andhra Pradesh (Kurnool) & MP (Rewa and Omkareshwar)
View Answer
Grid-connected photovoltaic solar power, PM-KUSUM agricultural solar pumps, rooftop solar installations, solar water heaters, and green hydrogen production
Wind Energy
View Answer
Gujarat (Kutch and Jamnagar coasts), Tamil Nadu (Muppandal Wind Farm — Kanyakumari), Rajasthan (Jaisalmer) & Maharashtra
View Answer
Clean electricity generation using wind turbines; India ranks fourth in the world in installed wind power capacity
Geothermal Energy
View Answer
Ladakh (Puga Valley — first experimental plant), Himachal Pradesh (Manikaran) & Chhattisgarh (Tatapani)
View Answer
Green electricity generation from subterranean hot water and steam, cold storage, greenhouse heating, and eco-tourism
Tidal and Wave Energy
View Answer
Gulf of Khambhat & Gulf of Kutch in Gujarat, and Sundarbans (Durgaduani Creek) in West Bengal
View Answer
Pollution-free electricity generation in coastal regions by driving turbines with the kinetic and potential energy of ocean tides
