Forest Resources Notes: ISFR 2023, Forest Types and State Rankings

The India State of Forest Report (ISFR) 2023, forest and tree cover, major forest types of India, mangroves, and State-wise status are the core topics of this chapter. The notes organize forest classification, ecological significance, National Forest Policy targets, and major forest movements from an exam perspective.

What to Study in This Chapter?

  • ISFR 2023 and Forest-Tree Cover: Categories and classification of forest and tree cover used in the Forest Survey of India report.
  • Major Forest Types of India: Characteristics of tropical evergreen, deciduous, thorn, and montane forests.
  • Mangroves and State-wise Rankings: Comparative facts on mangrove area and area/percentage of forest cover.
  • National Forest Policy and Forest Movements: Environmental benefits of forests, policy targets, and revision of major people's movements.

Important Points for Competitive Exams

Study the climatic and vegetational characteristics of forest types, the difference between forest cover and tree cover in ISFR, mangroves, and State-wise data separately. Confirm the latest rankings and figures from the original report before memorizing.
