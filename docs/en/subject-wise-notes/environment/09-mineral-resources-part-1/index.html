Mineral Resources of India: Major Mineral Belts, Distribution and Critical Minerals

The distribution of minerals in India is linked to geological structure and regional conditions. In this part, understand the classification of metallic, non-metallic, and energy minerals, major mineral belts such as Chota Nagpur, Dharwar, and Aravalli, as well as iron ore, manganese, bauxite, copper, mica, limestone, uranium-thorium, lithium, and rare earth elements.

Key Topics Covered in This Chapter

  • Mineral Classification & Mineral Belts: Ferrous/non-ferrous metallic, non-metallic, and energy minerals, along with India's major geological belts.
  • Metallic Minerals: Distribution of iron ore, manganese, bauxite, copper, chromite, gold, and lead-zinc.
  • Non-Metallic & Atomic Minerals: Key facts related to mica, limestone, diamond, gypsum, uranium, and thorium.
  • Critical & Rare Minerals: Critical minerals, lithium discoveries, rare earth elements, and energy minerals such as coal and petroleum.

Frequently Asked Questions by Students

Which are the major mineral belts of India? For exams, focus on major mineral/geological belts such as Chota Nagpur, Dharwar, and Aravalli, along with examples of minerals associated with them.

Why are critical minerals important? They are considered critical due to supply-chain risks and their strategic uses in clean energy, batteries, electronics, and other key sectors.
