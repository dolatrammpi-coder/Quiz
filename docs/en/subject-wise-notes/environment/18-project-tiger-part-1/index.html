Project Tiger 1973: NTCA, Core–Buffer Zone and Tiger Reserves

Project Tiger is India's flagship programme for conserving the tiger and its habitat. In this part, study its 1973 launch, initial Tiger Reserves, the tiger's role as an umbrella species, the National Tiger Conservation Authority (NTCA), core and buffer zones, tiger estimation, and the geographical features of Tiger Reserves.

Key Topics Covered in This Chapter

Common Student Questions

When was Project Tiger launched? Project Tiger was launched in April 1973. Read facts related to its early background, original reserves, and institutional evolution in this chapter.

What is the difference between the core and buffer zones in a Tiger Reserve? In the core zone, conservation of tigers and their habitat is given priority; in the buffer zone, management of the surrounding landscape and human activities alongside conservation is also important.
