Energy Resources Notes: Renewable Energy, Solar and Wind Power

In this part, understand India's renewable energy targets, solar energy capacity, major solar parks, wind energy, and hydropower projects as exam-focused notes. Comparisons of energy sources and their limitations provide a useful foundation for objective questions on energy and the environment.

What to Study in This Chapter?

  • Renewable Energy Targets: Key points related to India's clean energy targets and energy mix.
  • Solar Energy and Solar Parks: Facts related to solar energy capacity, major schemes, and solar parks.
  • Wind and Hydropower Energy: Wind energy capacity, major wind parks, and hydropower projects.
  • Comparison of Energy Sources: Limitations of hydropower, geothermal energy, biomass, and difference between conventional and non-conventional sources.

Important Points for Competitive Exams

For UPSC, UPPSC, UKPSC, and State-level examinations, revise questions related to renewable energy, solar parks, wind energy, hydropower, and comparisons of energy sources. Verify changing figures such as capacity or rankings from the latest official sources.
