Environment Rules, EIA 2006 and CRZ 2019 Notes

In this part, study topics related to the Environment (Protection) Rules 1986, Environmental Impact Assessment (EIA) Notification 2006, Coastal Regulation Zone (CRZ) Notification 2019, Waste Management Rules, and the Jan Vishwas (Amendment of Provisions) Act 2023. Pay attention to the distinction between rules, notifications, and applicable statutes.

What to Study in This Chapter?

  • Environment Protection Rules 1986 and EIA 2006: Role of rules, stages of EIA, and project clearance process.
  • CRZ Notification 2019: Key points related to regulation of coastal zones and environmental protection.
  • Waste Rules and Plastic Ban: Topics on solid waste, bio-medical waste, single-use plastic, and noise standards.
  • Jan Vishwas Amendment 2023: Changes regarding penalties/enforcement in environmental laws and review of relevant sections of the Act.

Important Points for Competitive Exams

Distinguish separately between the stages of EIA 2006, CRZ notifications, Waste Management Rules, and the Jan Vishwas Amendment 2023. Before the exam, verify the status of amended notifications, rules, and sections from official government sources.

Test yourself: What is EIA 2006 and what are its major stages? What is CRZ 2019? What changes did the Jan Vishwas Amendment 2023 bring to environmental laws?
