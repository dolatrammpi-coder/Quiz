Powers and Landmark Judgments of NGT: Suo Motu, Appeals, and Environmental Compensation

In this part, study the judicial developments regarding NGT's suo motu power, major environmental cases, penal provisions, environmental compensation, and the appeal process. Also understand institutional challenges such as limitation periods, NGT vs Civil Court, and compliance with orders.

Key Topics Covered in This Chapter

  • Suo Motu and Landmark Verdicts: Legal position on suo motu cognizance and major environmental cases related to rivers, construction, vehicles, and mining.
  • Section 26 and Environmental Compensation: Penalties for violations, determination of compensation, and the role of concerned regulatory bodies.
  • Appeal under Section 22: Appeal before the Supreme Court, limitation period, and the legal procedure to challenge NGT orders.
  • Limitations and Compliance: Role of NGT, difference from Civil Courts, vacancies, compliance with verdicts, and environmental jurisprudence.

Common Student Questions

Can NGT take suo motu cognizance? The question of suo motu cognizance has been significant in judicial verdicts concerning NGT. Read the relevant judgments and applicable legal position together in this chapter.

Where is an appeal filed against an NGT order? Under Section 22 of the NGT Act, an appeal can be filed before the Supreme Court; refer to the current text of the Act for the applicable time limit and procedure.
