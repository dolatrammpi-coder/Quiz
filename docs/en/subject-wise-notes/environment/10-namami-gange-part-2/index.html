Section 1 — Hybrid Annuity Model (HAM), 'One City One Operator', STP & CETP Infrastructure

Which PPP model was adopted under Namami Gange to bring a transformative change in the construction and long-term operation of Sewage Treatment Plants (STPs)?
Hybrid Annuity-based PPP Model (HAM - Hybrid Annuity Model)
Tap to view answer
Which was the first programme in India to implement the HAM model in the wastewater sector?
Namami Gange Programme (approved in 2016)
Tap to view answer
Under the HAM model, what percentage of the capital cost is paid by the government (NMCG) during the construction of an STP?
40% payment (at various stages of construction)
Tap to view answer
Under the HAM model, over how many years of operation is the remaining 60% of the capital cost paid to the private operator?
Over a period of 15 years (along with interest and O&M charges)
Tap to view answer
On what condition does the private developer receive the 60% annuity and O&M payments under the HAM model?
Only upon successful achievement of treated water quality standards (BOD, TSS, etc.) — Performance-linked
Tap to view answer
What is the biggest advantage of the HAM model compared to the older model (GAP-I/II)?
It eliminates the 'build and forget' problem and ensures proper STP operation for 15 years

Namami Gange Part 2: HAM Model, STP, Ganga Gram & Arth Ganga

Sewage treatment, industrial effluent control, real-time monitoring, afforestation, and public participation are key themes in the implementation of Namami Gange. In this part, revise the Hybrid Annuity Model (HAM), STP/CETP, OCEMS, PRAYAG portal, GIS/drone monitoring, Ganga Gram, Ganga Praharis, and the Arth Ganga model.

Key Topics Covered in This Chapter

  • HAM, STP & CETP: Roles of the Hybrid Annuity Model, Sewage Treatment Plants, and Common Effluent Treatment Plants.
  • Technology & Monitoring: OCEMS, PRAYAG portal, GIS/drone-based monitoring, and riverbank afforestation.
  • Public Participation & Ganga Gram: Roles of Ganga Praharis, Ganga Task Force, Ganga Utsav, and local communities.
  • Arth Ganga Model: The concept of linking river conservation with livelihoods, local products, tourism, and community-centric activities.

Frequently Asked Questions by Students

What is the difference between STP and CETP? An STP is primarily meant for treating domestic sewage; a CETP is built for the collective treatment of wastewater from multiple industrial units.

What is the Arth Ganga model? Arth Ganga is an approach that links river conservation with local livelihoods and economic activities; verify the current components of the scheme from official sources.
