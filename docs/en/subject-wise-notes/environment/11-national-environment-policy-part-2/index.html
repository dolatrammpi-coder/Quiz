NEP 2006 Part 2: EIA, CRZ, ESZ and Environmental Market Instruments

Measures linked to implementing the National Environment Policy include Environmental Impact Assessment (EIA), Coastal Regulation Zone (CRZ), Eco-Sensitive Zones (ESZ), environmental audits, Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR), CEPI, and market-based instruments. This part connects the policy's principles with regulatory mechanisms and new environmental initiatives.

Key Topics Covered in This Chapter

  • Regulatory and Market-Based Instruments: Difference between command-and-control approaches and Market-Based Instruments (MBIs).
  • EIA 2006, PARIVESH and CRZ: Project environmental clearance, Category A/B, coastal management, and related digital processes.
  • Western Ghats, ESZ and Environmental Audit: Gadgil and Kasturirangan committees, eco-sensitive zones, and the role of audits.
  • EPR, CEPI and New Green Initiatives: Extended Producer Responsibility, polluted industrial area index, Green Credit, CCTS, MISHTI, and Amrit Dharohar.

Common Student Questions

What is the objective of EIA 2006? The Environmental Impact Assessment process helps evaluate the potential environmental impacts of projects and make decisions in accordance with applicable provisions.

What is the full form of EPR? Extended Producer Responsibility; it fixes the responsibilities of producers in managing the concerned product or waste.
