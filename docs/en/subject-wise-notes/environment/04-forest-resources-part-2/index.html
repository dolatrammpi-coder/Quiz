Forest Policy 1988, Forest Conservation Act and CAMPA Notes

This part covers the classification of forest administration, National Forest Policy 1988, Forest (Conservation) Act 1980, social and agro-forestry, the Chipko Movement, the Forest Survey of India (FSI), CAMPA, and major afforestation schemes. Focus on the differences in the roles of the respective institutions and schemes.

What to Study in This Chapter?

  • Forest Administration and Law: Administrative classification of Reserved, Protected, and Unclassed forests and Forest Conservation Act 1980.
  • Social Forestry and Forest Movements: Significance of social forestry, agroforestry, Van Mahotsav, and Chipko Movement.
  • FSI and ISFR: Role of Forest Survey of India, State of Forest Report, and assessment system.
  • CAMPA and Afforestation Schemes: Basic information on CAF Act 2016, compensatory afforestation, NPV, MISHTI, GIM, and REDD+.

Important Points for Competitive Exams

Understand the differences in objectives and implementation of the National Forest Policy 1988, forest conservation laws, FSI, CAMPA, and afforestation schemes. Refer to official notifications for the current status of laws or schemes.
