Section 2 — District Mineral Foundation (DMF), PMKKKY, NMET & KABIL

Under which section of the MMDR Act is the District Mineral Foundation (DMF) established?
Section 9B (2015 Amendment)
Tap to view answer
What type of institution is a DMF?
A Non-Profit Statutory Trust
Tap to view answer
In which districts is a District Mineral Foundation (DMF) constituted?
In all mining-affected districts of the country (by State Government notification)
Tap to view answer
What is the primary objective of the DMF?
To work for the interest and benefit of persons and areas affected by mining-related operations
Tap to view answer
What percentage of royalty do leaseholders of (old) mines granted before 12 January 2015 have to contribute to the DMF?
30% of royalty
Tap to view answer
What percentage of royalty do new mines allocated via auction after 12 January 2015 have to contribute to the DMF?
10% of royalty
Tap to view answer
Which scheme did the Central Government launch in September 2015 to ensure proper utilization of funds collected under the DMF?
Pradhan Mantri Khanij Kshetra Kalyan Yojana (PMKKKY)
Tap to view answer
According to PMKKKY guidelines, at least what percentage of DMF funds must be spent on 'high-priority sectors'?
At least 60% (up to 70% under the revised guidelines of January 2024)
Tap to view answer
Which activities are included in the 'high-priority sectors' of PMKKKY?
Drinking water supply, environmental preservation/pollution control, health, education, women and child welfare, skill development
Tap to view answer
What maximum share of DMF funds can be spent on other sectors such as physical infrastructure (roads, bridges, irrigation)?
Maximum 30% to 40%
Tap to view answer
According to the revised PMKKKY guidelines of January 2024, up to how many kilometres from the mine boundary is the directly mining-affected area defined?
Up to a 15-kilometre radius (directly affected) and up to 25 km (indirectly affected)
Tap to view answer
Whose approval has been made mandatory for utilizing DMF funds in Scheduled Areas (Fifth Schedule)?
Gram Sabha (in accordance with the PESA Act)
Tap to view answer
Under which section of the MMDR Act is the National Mineral Exploration Trust (NMET) established?
Section 9C (constituted in August 2015)
Tap to view answer
What is the main objective of NMET?
To mobilize financial resources for regional and detailed mineral exploration in India
Tap to view answer
What percentage of royalty is every mining leaseholder required to deposit into NMET?
2% of royalty

Union Minister of Mines

Section 3 — Environmental Impacts of Mining, Acid Mine Drainage (AMD), Rat-Hole Mining & Health Hazards

Which are the two main methods of mining?
Opencast / Surface Mining and Underground Mining
Tap to view answer
Which mining method causes the highest deforestation, topsoil destruction, and landscape alteration?
Opencast Mining
Tap to view answer
What are the huge heaps of unusable soil and rock removed to extract minerals in an opencast mine called?
Overburden or Spoil Dump
Tap to view answer
What is the fine chemical waste slurry left over after metal extraction or washing called?
Tailings
Tap to view answer
What is the primary chemical cause of Acid Mine Drainage (AMD)?
Oxidation of sulphide minerals (especially pyrite - FeS₂) upon exposure to air and water
Tap to view answer
Which strong acid is formed by the oxidation of pyrite (FeS₂ - also called 'Fool's Gold')?
Sulphuric Acid (H₂SO₄)
Tap to view answer
What effect does Acid Mine Drainage (AMD) have on the pH level and heavy metals of water bodies?
The pH drops drastically (acidic), and toxic metals like arsenic, lead, and cadmium dissolve into the water
Tap to view answer
What is the orange-red precipitate that settles on riverbeds due to Acid Mine Drainage (AMD) called?
Yellow Boy (Ferric Hydroxide - Fe(OH)₃)
Tap to view answer
Where is the problem of Acid Mine Drainage (AMD) most severely observed in India?
In the Jaintia Hills coal mines of Meghalaya and the coal and copper mines of Jharkhand/Chhattisgarh
Tap to view answer
In which state of India is 'Rat-hole Mining' a traditional yet dangerous method of extracting coal?
Meghalaya (especially in Jaintia, Khasi, and Garo Hills)
Tap to view answer
Why was rat-hole mining used in Meghalaya?
Because the coal seams there are very thin (less than 1 to 2 metres)
Tap to view answer
In which year did the National Green Tribunal (NGT) ban illegal and unscientific rat-hole coal mining in Meghalaya?
In April 2014
Tap to view answer
Which river in Meghalaya turned into a 'dead river' with a bluish-green colour due to rat-hole coal mining and Acid Mine Drainage?
Lukha River and Kopili River
Tap to view answer
Which technique was used at the end of the Uttarakhand Silkyara tunnel rescue (November 2023) to evacuate the 41 trapped workers?
Manual rat-hole mining technique
Tap to view answer
What is the sudden sinking of land after underground coal mines are emptied called?
Land Subsidence
Tap to view answer
Which coalfield in India has faced severe underground coalfield fires and land subsidence for over a century?
Jharia Coalfield (Dhanbad, Jharkhand)
Tap to view answer
Which is the main flammable gas ('firedamp') responsible for explosions in coal mines?
Methane gas (CH₄)
Tap to view answer
Which incurable lung disease affects workers in stone quarries, quartz, and silica mining?

Which lung disease affects workers in asbestos mines?

National Mineral Policy 2019, MMDR Act, DMF & Sustainable Mining Notes

To understand India's mining sector, learn the role of the National Mineral Policy 2019, the Mines and Minerals (Development and Regulation) Act, District Mineral Foundation (DMF), PMKKKY, NMET, and KABIL. Also read about the environmental aspects of acid mine drainage, rat-hole mining, mine safety, sustainable mining, and Urban Mining.

Key Topics Covered in This Chapter

  • Mineral Policy & MMDR Law: Thematic introduction to the National Mineral Policy 2019, the MMDR Act, and its amendments.
  • DMF, PMKKKY, NMET & KABIL: Institutions and initiatives linked to mining-affected areas, mineral exploration, and securing strategic minerals abroad.
  • Environmental & Health Impacts of Mining: Acid mine drainage, rat-hole mining, dust/silicosis, and impacts on land and water.
  • Sustainable Mining & Urban Mining: Sustainable Development Framework, Star Rating of Mines, MSSS surveillance, and resource recovery through recycling.

Frequently Asked Questions by Students

What is the District Mineral Foundation (DMF)? DMF is a district-level body established in mining-affected districts for the interest and benefit of affected persons and areas; verify the rules of its current schemes from official guidelines.

What is Urban Mining? The process of recovering metals and useful resources from old electronic devices, batteries, and other discarded products is called Urban Mining.
