Waste Hierarchy, Sanitary Landfill, Biomedical Waste, and E-Waste Rules

When preventing, reusing, or recycling waste is not feasible, appropriate treatment and safe disposal become essential. This part covers waste hierarchy, incineration, pyrolysis, gasification, sanitary landfill and leachate, biomedical waste colour coding, E-Waste Management Rules 2022, and the Basel Convention.

Key Topics Covered in This Chapter

  • Waste Hierarchy and Thermal Treatment: Priority order from waste reduction to disposal, and the differences between incineration, pyrolysis, and gasification.
  • Sanitary Landfill and Biomining: Liners, leachate, landfill gas, biomining of legacy waste, and treatment-related questions.
  • Bio-Medical Waste: Colour-coded bags under the Bio-Medical Waste Management Rules 2016 and safe management of segregated waste.
  • E-Waste and International Conventions: E-Waste Management Rules 2022, urban mining, Basel Convention, and control of transboundary movement of hazardous waste.

Frequently Asked Questions by Students

What is prioritized first in the waste hierarchy? In the standard waste hierarchy, preventing waste generation and minimizing its volume hold the highest priority; treatment and disposal are considered only after reuse and recycling.

What is the Basel Convention related to? The Basel Convention is an international treaty governing the control of transboundary movements of hazardous wastes and their environmentally sound management.
