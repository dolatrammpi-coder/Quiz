National Green Tribunal (NGT) Act 2010: Composition and Jurisdiction

The National Green Tribunal (NGT) is a specialized tribunal established for the effective and expeditious disposal of specified cases relating to the environment. In this part, understand the NGT Act 2010, establishment, member composition, Principal and Zonal Benches, jurisdiction, and the environmental laws included in Schedule I.

Key Topics Covered in This Chapter

  • NGT Act 2010 and Background: Establishment of the tribunal, need for environmental justice, and constitutional context.
  • Chairperson and Judicial/Expert Members: Exam-relevant facts on membership structure, qualifications, appointment, and tenure.
  • Benches and Jurisdiction: Principal Bench, Zonal Benches, and types of cases under Sections 14 to 16.
  • Schedule I Laws and Limitations: Environmental laws under NGT's jurisdiction and distinction from laws excluded from the Act.

Common Student Questions

When was the National Green Tribunal established? NGT was established in 2010 under the National Green Tribunal Act, 2010; memorize the establishment date and composition facts provided in this chapter together.

What is the difference between NGT and a Civil Court? NGT is a specialized tribunal for specified environmental matters, whereas a Civil Court has general jurisdiction and a different procedure. Read NGT's jurisdiction alongside the relevant sections of the Act.
