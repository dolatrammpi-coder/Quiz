Benefits of Greenhouse Effect and Climate Change Control: NAPCC, Carbon Sink

The natural greenhouse effect helps maintain a life-sustaining temperature on Earth, but its intensification increases climate risks. This part connects core ideas related to India's National Action Plan on Climate Change (NAPCC), Green India Mission, renewable energy, green/blue/teal carbon, carbon capture, and carbon markets.

Main Topics Covered in This Chapter

  • Natural Greenhouse Effect and Climate Risk: Relationship between Earth's temperature, sea-level rise, and extreme weather.
  • NAPCC and Green India Mission: India's climate policy, mission-based initiatives, and role of renewable energy.
  • Carbon Sinks and Carbon Removal Technologies: Green carbon of forests, blue carbon of coastal ecosystems, teal carbon, biochar, and CCS/BECCS.
  • Carbon Markets and Green Incentives: Carbon Credit Trading Scheme (CCTS), Green Credit, and concepts of mitigation and adaptation.

Frequently Asked Questions by Students

What is a carbon sink? A carbon sink is a natural or technological system that absorbs or removes carbon dioxide from the atmosphere.

What are mitigation and adaptation in climate change? Mitigation focuses on reducing greenhouse gas emissions or removing carbon; adaptation relates to preparing for climate impacts and reducing risks.
