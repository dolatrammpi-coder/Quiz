Noise Pollution Rules 2000: CPCB Standards, Silence Zone and Control

The Noise Pollution (Regulation and Control) Rules, 2000 and related environmental provisions are crucial for controlling noise pollution in India. In this part, study the standards for industrial, commercial, residential, and silence zones, nighttime regulations, green crackers, noise monitoring, and control at the source, path, and receiver levels.

Key Topics Covered in This Chapter

  • Noise Pollution Rules, 2000: Objective of the rules, concept of silence zone, and mechanism for noise-level regulation.
  • Four Zones and CPCB Noise Standards: Comparison of day and night standards for industrial, commercial, residential, and silence zones.
  • Green Crackers and Monitoring: Reference to CSIR-NEERI, vehicle/generator standards, and the National Ambient Noise Monitoring Network (NANMN).
  • Noise Control and Judicial Aspects: Control at source, transmission path, and receiver levels; issues like green muffler and Right to Sleep.

Common Student Questions

What is a silence zone? Under the rules, a silence zone may be declared around specified sensitive locations for noise control; refer to the relevant current rules/notifications for exact boundaries and applicable conditions.

What are the three levels of controlling noise pollution? Controlling noise at the source, controlling the transmission path between the source and the person, and protecting the exposed receiver are the three standard approaches.
