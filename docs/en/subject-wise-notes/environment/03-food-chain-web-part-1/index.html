Food Chain Notes: Trophic Levels, 10 Percent Law and Ecological Pyramids

How energy and nutrients are transferred in a food chain is a foundational topic of ecology. In this part, study the definition of a food chain, the 10 percent law, grazing and detritus food chains, trophic levels, primary productivity, and ecological pyramids in sequence.

What to Study in This Chapter?

  • Food Chain and 10 Percent Law: Energy transfer from producer to consumer and energy loss at each step.
  • Grazing and Detritus Food Chains: Grazing Food Chain (GFC), Detritus Food Chain (DFC), and stages of decomposition.
  • Trophic Levels and Productivity: Trophic levels, primary productivity, and length of a food chain.
  • Aquatic Food Chain and Pyramids: Components of aquatic ecosystems and ecological pyramids of number, biomass, and energy.

Important Points for Competitive Exams

Remember with examples the 10 percent law, producer-consumer-decomposer, trophic levels, and differences among ecological pyramids. These are frequently tested concepts in fundamental ecology and environment questions.
