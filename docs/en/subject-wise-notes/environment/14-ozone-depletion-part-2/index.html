Why does the ozone hole form so much larger and deeper over the Southern Hemisphere (Antarctica) than over the Northern Hemisphere (Arctic)?

After total winter darkness (June–August), what happens to the accumulated Cl₂ gas when the first sunlight hits Antarctica in September (spring)?

Why does the Antarctic ozone hole begin to fill naturally in November–December (early summer)?

Why does a permanent ozone hole like Antarctica's not form over the Arctic (North Pole)?

Montreal Protocol and Kigali Amendment: Ozone Protection Notes

The ozone hole over Antarctica, the Polar Vortex, and Polar Stratospheric Clouds help explain the mechanism of ozone depletion. In this part, revise key points related to the Vienna Convention, Montreal Protocol, World Ozone Day, the Kigali Amendment, HFC phase-down, and India's Cooling Action Plan.

Key Topics Covered in This Chapter

  • Antarctic Ozone Hole and PSCs: Seasonal ozone depletion, the Polar Vortex, and the role of Polar Stratospheric Clouds.
  • Vienna Convention and Montreal Protocol: International cooperation for ozone layer protection, controlled substances, and global compliance.
  • Kigali Amendment and HFCs: Phased control of HFCs, and the link between ozone protection and climate benefits.
  • India's Cooling Strategy and Refrigerants: India Cooling Action Plan, CFC-HCFC-HFC-HFO generations, and the role of natural refrigerants.

Frequently Asked Questions by Students

What is the objective of the Montreal Protocol? The Montreal Protocol is an international agreement designed to phase out the production and consumption of substances that deplete the ozone layer.

What is the Kigali Amendment related to? The Kigali Amendment relates to phasing down HFCs under the Montreal Protocol; HFCs do not directly destroy ozone like many ODS, but they are potent greenhouse gases.
