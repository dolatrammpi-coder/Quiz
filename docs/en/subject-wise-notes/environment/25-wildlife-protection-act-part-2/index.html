Section 1 — Original 6 Schedules vs WPA Amendment 2022 (4 Schedules), Vermin Removal & Protected Plants

How many Schedules were there in the original Wildlife (Protection) Act, 1972 (prior to the 2022 Amendment)?
6 Schedules (Schedule I to Schedule VI)

Animals in which Schedules of the original WPA 1972 enjoyed the highest legal protection and attracted the strictest penalties for hunting?
Schedule I and Part II of Schedule II

What types of wild animals were included in Schedule III and Schedule IV of the original WPA 1972?
Species that were not endangered (hunting was prohibited, though penalties were comparatively lower)

Section 2 — CITES Convention (1973), Appendices I/II/III, Management & Scientific Authorities, Invasive Alien Species & Captive Elephants

What is the full form of CITES?
Convention on International Trade in Endangered Species of Wild Fauna and Flora
Tap to view answer
Because it was signed in which city is CITES also known as the 'Washington Convention'?
Washington, D.C. (signed on 3 March 1973)
Tap to view answer
On which date did the CITES Convention enter into force internationally?
1 July 1975
Tap to view answer
In which year did India become a signatory party to CITES?
1976
Tap to view answer
Which United Nations body administers the CITES Secretariat, and where is its headquarters located?
Administered by UNEP; Headquarters in Geneva (Switzerland)
Tap to view answer
Is the primary objective of CITES to ensure that international trade in wild animals and plants does not threaten their survival?
Yes (regulation of international trade)
Tap to view answer
How many Appendices have been created under CITES based on the degree of threat faced by species?
3 Appendices (Appendix I, Appendix II, and Appendix III)
Tap to view answer
Which species are included in 'Appendix I' of CITES, and what is the rule regarding their commercial international trade?
Species threatened with extinction — commercial international trade is strictly prohibited
Tap to view answer
Which species are included in 'Appendix II' of CITES?
Species not necessarily threatened with extinction now, but may become so unless trade is controlled (regulated trade via export permits)
Tap to view answer
Which species are included in 'Appendix III' of CITES?
Species included at the request of a Party that already regulates trade and needs the cooperation of other countries to prevent unsustainable exploitation
Tap to view answer
Under Section 49E of the WPA Amendment Act, 2022, who is designated by the Central Government as the 'Management Authority' to issue CITES permits?
Director of Wildlife Preservation
Tap to view answer
Which institutions are designated by the Central Government as 'Scientific Authorities' (Section 49F) to advise on the impact of trade on species survival under CITES?
Scientific institutions such as WII, ZSI, BSI, CMFRI, and ICFRE
Tap to view answer
The renowned international wildlife trade monitoring network 'TRAFFIC' (established in 1976) is a joint programme of which two organizations?

Tap to view answer
Under which new Section (Section 62A) did the 2022 WPA Amendment empower the Central Government to regulate the import, trade, and proliferation of 'Invasive Alien Species' in India?

Tap to view answer
What is an 'Invasive Alien Species'?
A species not native to India whose introduction or spread threatens local ecosystems or native species
Tap to view answer
Which are the prominent invasive alien plant species rapidly spreading in Indian forests and destroying grasslands of tigers and herbivores?
Lantana camara, Parthenium (Congress grass), Prosopis juliflora, and Senna spectabilis
Tap to view answer

Section 3 — Revised Penalty Provisions (Section 51), Compounding Fee, Vulture Conservation (Diclofenac Ban), Plan Bee & IBCA

Which Section of the Wildlife (Protection) Act, 1972 provides for penalties and fines for violations of the Act?
Section 51

₹1,00,000 (One lakh rupees)

Along with a fine of up to ₹1 lakh, what is the maximum imprisonment term for general violations?
Imprisonment up to 3 years (or both)

What is the minimum and maximum imprisonment term for offences related to New 'Schedule I' (highest protected animals) or hunting inside a National Park/Sanctuary?
Minimum 3 years to maximum 7 years rigorous imprisonment
Tap to view answer
By the 2022 Amendment, the minimum fine for Schedule I offences was increased from ₹10,000 to what amount?
Minimum ₹25,000 (and minimum ₹1,00,000 fine for a second or subsequent offence)
Tap to view answer
What is the stringent penalty and fine for hunting or committing an offence in the Core Area (Critical Tiger Habitat) of a Tiger Reserve (Section 51(1C))?
First offence: 3 to 7 years jail & ₹50,000 to ₹2 lakh fine; Second offence: 7 years jail & ₹5 lakh to ₹50 lakh fine
Tap to view answer
Under Section 54 of WPA, the maximum compounding fee for minor offences (where minimum imprisonment is not mandatory) has been raised from ₹25,000 to what amount?
Up to ₹5,00,000 (5 lakh rupees)
Tap to view answer
Can serious offences such as the hunting of Schedule I wild animals be compounded?
No, offences carrying a mandatory minimum imprisonment term can never be compounded
Tap to view answer
According to Section 55 of WPA, on whose written complaint can a court take cognizance of a wildlife offence?

Wildlife Protection Amendment 2022: Schedules, CITES & Penalties

The 2022 amendment to the Wildlife (Protection) Act brought major changes to the schedules, CITES implementation, and certain offence and compliance provisions. In this part, study the comparison between the original six schedules and the amended schedule structure, protected plants, CITES Appendices I–III, vermin provisions, penalties, and vulture conservation.

Key Topics Covered in This Chapter

  • 2022 Amendment & Schedules: Matching the old and revised schedule structures, protected animals/plants, and applicable enforcement dates with the statutory text.
  • CITES & International Trade: Purpose of Appendices I, II, and III, and the roles of Management and Scientific Authorities.
  • Vermin & Penalty Provisions: Earlier vermin provisions, Section 51 penalties, and current law applicable to violations.
  • Vulture & Other Conservation Projects: Conservation issues linked to Diclofenac, vulture recovery efforts, and other species-specific programmes.

Frequently Asked Questions by Students

What is the difference between the three appendices of CITES? Appendix I subjects international trade in species threatened with extinction to the strictest controls; Appendix II regulates trade so that survival is not threatened; Appendix III allows any party country to list species for international cooperation.

Why is the Wildlife Protection Amendment 2022 important? The amendment altered several provisions, including the schedule structure and CITES implementation. For examinations, it is essential to distinguish the current provisions in force after the amendment from the original Act.
