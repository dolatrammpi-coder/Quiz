What is National Environment Policy 2006? Objectives and Key Principles

The National Environment Policy (NEP), 2006 provides policy-level direction for environmental protection and sustainable development in India. In this part, study the policy background, earlier environmental policies, seven primary objectives, and inter-generational equity, polluter pays, precautionary principle, and public trust doctrine.

Key Topics Covered in This Chapter

  • Background & Objectives of NEP 2006: Approval of the policy, earlier environmental policies, and priorities of resource conservation.
  • Guiding Principles of the Policy: Meanings of sustainable development, polluter pays, precautionary principle, and public trust doctrine.
  • Environmental Governance & Constitutional Basis: Roles of the government, institutions, and citizens in environmental protection.
  • Green Accounting & Ecosystem Services: Valuation of natural resources, traditional knowledge, and the role of environmental costs in resource decisions.

Frequently Asked Questions by Students

What are the main objectives of the National Environment Policy 2006? This policy emphasizes conservation of critical environmental resources, resource-use efficiency, livelihood security, environmental governance, and integrating environmental considerations into development decisions. Read the seven objectives listed in the policy text in sequence.

What is the Polluter Pays Principle? According to this principle, the cost of preventing pollution and remedying the resulting environmental damage should be borne by the polluting party.
