This part helps revise India's energy policy, the Energy Conservation Act 2001, the Bureau of Energy Efficiency (BEE), star labelling, the PAT scheme, ECBC, and energy security together. Concise facts on Strategic Petroleum Reserve (SPR) locations and energy efficiency are also included.

  • National Energy Policy and Energy Conservation Act: Objectives of energy policy and key provisions of the Energy Conservation Act, 2001.
  • BEE, Star Labelling and PAT: Role of the Bureau of Energy Efficiency, star rating of appliances, and Perform, Achieve and Trade scheme.
