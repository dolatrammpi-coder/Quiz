Environment Protection Act 1986 Notes: Sections and Constitutional Basis

The Environment (Protection) Act, 1986 is the cornerstone of environmental law in India. In this part, systematically revise the background of the Act, its constitutional basis, key definitions, powers of the Central Government, pollution control, inspection, sampling procedures, and Sections 1 to 26.

What to Study in This Chapter?

  • Background and Constitutional Basis of the Act: Official title, commencement date, historical background, and constitutional provisions related to environmental protection.
  • Definitions under Section 2: Identify legal terms such as environment, environmental pollutant, environmental pollution, and hazardous substances.
  • Sections 3 to 6 and Control Powers: Powers of the Central Government, issuing directions, and basis for making rules.
  • Sections 7 to 26 and Enforcement: Emission/discharge control, entry and inspection, sampling procedure, laboratories, and miscellaneous provisions.

Important Points for Competitive Exams

The sections, definitions, and Central Government powers under the Environment (Protection) Act 1986 are important for exams. Distinguish it clearly from the Water (Prevention and Control of Pollution) Act and the Air Act; check the updated official text in case of legal amendments.
