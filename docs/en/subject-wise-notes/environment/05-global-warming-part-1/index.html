Global Warming Notes: Greenhouse Gases, GWP and Emission Sources

To understand the causes of global warming, it is essential to know the role of carbon dioxide, methane, nitrous oxide, and fluorinated gases. In this part, study GWP, water vapour, black carbon, and emissions from fossil fuels, deforestation, agriculture, and transport.

What to Study in This Chapter?

  • Greenhouse Gases and GWP: Role of CO₂, methane, and N₂O and the concept of Global Warming Potential.
  • Water Vapour, F-Gases and Black Carbon: Key points on greenhouse effect, fluorinated gases, and short-lived climate pollutants.
  • Fossil Fuels, Deforestation and Agriculture: Emission sources from fuel combustion, LULUCF, livestock, and agriculture.
  • Transport and Emission Reports: Role of the transport sector and how to read reports on global emissions.

Important Points for Competitive Exams

Understand the difference between the greenhouse effect and global warming, major greenhouse gases, GWP, and emission sources. Verify changing global figures and rankings from a recent official report.

Test yourself: What are the main causes of global warming? Which are the major greenhouse gases? What does GWP stand for?
