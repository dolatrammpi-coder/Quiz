Section 1 — Scientific Methods of Tiger Estimation — Pugmark Technique, Camera Trapping & M-STrIPES System

At what interval is the national-level tiger census (All India Tiger Estimation - AITE) conducted in India?
Every 4 years (started in 2006)
Tap to view answer
With the technical collaboration of which institute do the NTCA and State Forest Departments conduct the All India Tiger Estimation?
Wildlife Institute of India (WII, Dehradun)
Tap to view answer
Which traditional method was used for counting tigers in India prior to 2006?
Pugmark Census Technique
Tap to view answer
Which Indian Forest Service officer developed the technique of counting tigers by tracing their pugmarks?
S. R. Choudhury (Saroj Raj Choudhury)
Tap to view answer
After finding the pugmark method unscientific and prone to overestimation in 2005, which modern methodology was adopted?
Double Sampling with Camera Traps

On the basis of which biometric feature is each individual tiger identified?
Body stripe patterns (which are unique to each tiger like human fingerprints)

Which AI software tools are used to match tiger stripe patterns from millions of camera-trap images?

Which are the three main modules of the M-STrIPES system?
1. Patrol Module, 2. Ecological Module, 3. Conflict & Crime Module

Section 4 — Tiger Ecology, Co-predators, Human-Tiger Conflict & Global Tiger Conservation

Which are the major co-predators sharing Indian forests alongside the tiger?
Leopard, Dhole (Asiatic wild dog — Cuon alpinus), Sloth Bear, and Hyena
Tap to view answer
Which herbivores are the most important and preferred natural primary prey of the tiger in Indian forests?
Chital (Spotted Deer — Axis axis) and Sambar (Rusa unicolor)
Tap to view answer
Due to their large size, which massive wild bovids are adult tigers capable of hunting?

What is the primary natural prey of tigers in the mangrove forests of the Sundarbans?

When two similar-sized predators (such as tiger and leopard) inhabit the same forest, how does the leopard adapt its prey and habitat use (Niche Partitioning) to avoid the tiger?
The leopard hunts smaller prey, hauls kills up trees, and stays along forest edges
Tap to view answer
Which is the only country in the world where both wild Tigers and wild Asiatic Lions are found naturally?

Until the 19th century, tigers, lions, and cheetahs coexisted in Central India; why are tigers and Asiatic lions no longer found in the same forest today?

What adaptive advantage is attributed to the white circular spots (White Ocelli / False Eyes) behind a tiger's ears?
They help cubs follow their mother through dense bushes and deter attacks from behind (creating an illusion of eyes)

Unlike most other cats, do tigers enjoy water and swimming?

How Is the Tiger Census Conducted? M-STrIPES, Tiger Corridors and IBCA

Scientific estimation, camera traps, habitat connectivity, prey availability, and poaching prevention are essential for tiger conservation. This part covers the evolution from the pugmark technique to modern monitoring, M-STrIPES, Management Effectiveness Evaluation, Tiger Corridors, the Special Tiger Protection Force, and the International Big Cats Alliance.

Key Topics Covered in This Chapter

  • Tiger Census Methods: Pugmark technique, camera trapping, unique stripe patterns, and scientific population estimation.
  • M-STrIPES and MEE: Tiger monitoring/patrolling system and the objectives of Management Effectiveness Evaluation.
  • Tiger Corridors and STPF: Measures to connect fragmented habitats, secure landscapes, and curb poaching.
  • IBCA and Human–Tiger Conflict: International cooperation for conserving seven big cats, prey base, and examples of translocation.

Common Student Questions

What is M-STrIPES? M-STrIPES stands for Monitoring System for Tigers—Intensive Protection and Ecological Status. It is used to manage monitoring, patrolling, and ecological data in tiger conservation areas.

Why are Tiger Corridors necessary? Corridors help tigers move between different protected areas and maintain gene flow, reducing the risk of populations becoming isolated.
