Threatened Species of India: IUCN Red List & Legal Protection Notes

In this part, study the conservation context of species such as the Bengal Tiger, Asian Elephant, Gharial, Great Indian Bustard, Gangetic Dolphin, Red Panda, Hawksbill Turtle, vultures, and the One-horned Rhinoceros. It is essential to distinguish between IUCN's global risk category and legal status under India's Wildlife (Protection) Act.

Key Topics Covered in This Chapter

  • Major Indian Species: Conservation context of the Tiger, Elephant, Gharial, Great Indian Bustard, and other listed species.
  • Riverine, Forest & Marine Species: Key facts on the Gangetic Dolphin, Red Panda, Hawksbill Turtle, vultures, and Indian Rhinoceros.
  • IUCN vs. Wildlife Protection Act: Difference between global extinction-risk categories and protection/schedules under Indian law.
