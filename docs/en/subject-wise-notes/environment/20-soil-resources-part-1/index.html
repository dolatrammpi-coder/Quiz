Soils of India: Formation Factors, Soil Profile & ICAR's 8 Types

In this part on soil resources, study the factors of soil formation, horizons of a soil profile, humus, and India's major soil categories. Study the characteristics of Alluvial (Khadar-Bhangar), Black/Regur, Red-Yellow, Laterite, Desert, Mountain, Saline, and Peaty/Marshy soils by linking them to their formation and agricultural uses.

Key Topics Covered in This Chapter

  • Pedology and Soil Formation: Soil science, and soil-forming factors such as climate, organisms, topography, parent material, and time.
  • Soil Profile and Humus: O, A, E, B, C, R horizons and the contribution of organic matter/humus to soil fertility.
  • Major Soil Categories of India: ICAR classification, Khadar-Bhangar in alluvial soil, and characteristics of Black/Regur soil.
  • Red, Laterite, Desert & Other Soils: Color/minerals, leaching, salinity, organic matter, and relationship with agriculture/vegetation.

Frequently Asked Questions by Students

What is the difference between Khadar and Bhangar? Khadar generally refers to new alluvial soil and Bhangar to old alluvial soil; both are compared in terms of their position on the floodplain and sediment deposition.

What is a soil profile? A vertical cut of the soil showing distinct layers or horizons is called a soil profile. Their characteristics help in understanding soil formation and development.
