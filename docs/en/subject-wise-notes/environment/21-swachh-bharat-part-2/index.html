Swachh Bharat Mission Notes: SBM 2.0, ODF Plus & Garbage Free Cities (GFC)

In this chapter of Environment Notes — Lagale, you can revise the rural and urban phases of the Swachh Bharat Mission, sanitation certification, and solid and liquid waste management through exam-oriented Q&As and comparative tables. Using the Reading and Practice options above, students can first read the answers and then practice on their own.

Key Topics Covered in This Chapter

  • Swachh Bharat Mission (Gramin) Phase 2 — SBM-G 2.0: Aspiring, Rising, and Model levels of ODF Plus, village-level sanitation, and the GOBARdhan scheme.
  • Swachh Bharat Mission (Urban) 2.0 — SBM-U 2.0: Source segregation of waste, waste processing, and urban sanitation systems.
  • ODF, ODF+, ODF++ and Water+: Comparison of certification standards related to freedom from open defecation, toilet and faecal sludge management, and water management.
  • GOBARdhan and PWMU: Initiatives related to the utilization and management of organic waste, cattle dung, and other waste.
  • Swachh Survekshan and Garbage Free City (GFC) Star Rating: Urban cleanliness assessment, garbage-free city norms, and key indicators.
  • Solid and Liquid Waste Management (SLWM): Major techniques including source segregation, composting, biomining, greywater, and faecal sludge treatment.

Use for Competitive Examinations

These Swachh Bharat Mission One-Liner GK Notes and revision points can assist in preparing for environment, government schemes, and general knowledge sections of UPSC, UPPSC, UKPSC, and other State Public Service Commissions. Before the exam, always cross-check current scheme guidelines, official data, and revised norms with the respective government sources.

Study Tip: First understand the objectives of SBM-G 2.0 and SBM-U 2.0, then revise the differences between ODF Plus/Water+ certification and GFC Star Rating using the table. Finally, practice with the questions on SLWM technologies and the GOBARdhan scheme.
