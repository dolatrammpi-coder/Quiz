Subject Wise Notes: Structured Study and Rapid Revision Guide for Competitive Exams

To revise the vast syllabus of competitive examinations in minimum time, the Subject Wise Notes section is designed as an interactive digital notebook. Over 500 chapters across General Studies, Languages (Hindi & English), Computer Science, and State GK (UP GK & Uttarakhand GK) are organized into point-wise One-Liner Q&A and Quick Reference Tables.

Key Features & How to Use Subject Wise Notes

  • Reading Mode vs. Practice Mode: Two interactive modes are provided at the top of every chapter. Choose 'Reading Mode' during your first reading so both questions and answers remain visible. Once completed, switch to 'Practice Mode' to mask all answers and test your active recall by tapping each answer cover.
  • Starred Bookmarks (Personal Fact Compilation): While studying any chapter, tap the Star (☆) button next to dates, constitutional articles, scientific names, or figures you find challenging to remember. Later, activate the 'Starred Only' filter for rapid revision of your bookmarked items.
  • Instant Keyword Search & Shuffle: Use the built-in search bar inside each chapter to instantly locate any ruler, constitutional amendment, river, mineral, or literary work. The 'Shuffle All' button randomizes the sequence of items to build genuine conceptual retention rather than rote order memorization.
  • Structured Comparison Tables: Wherever comparative clarity is needed—such as historical battles, constitutional schedules, five-year plans, biosphere reserves, or mineral-producing states—interactive quick-reference tables are integrated at the top of the chapter.

Recommended Study Strategy

For best results, immediately after reading the chapter-wise notes of any subject (such as History, Polity, or Geography), solve the corresponding MCQ Practice Quiz available on our homepage. One-liner notes build a rock-solid factual foundation, while multiple-choice quizzes sharpen your elimination skills for the examination hall.
