Early Satyagrahas
Gandhiji's Early Movements in India — Champaran, Ahmedabad, and Kheda
Comparative and analytical overview of the regional movements of 1917–1918
Movement, Year & Region Main Issue & Leadership Method & Final Outcome
Champaran Satyagraha (April 1917 CE) Northern Bihar Tinkathia system of European planters (indigo on 3/20th of land); Leadership: Gandhiji, Rajkumar Shukla, Rajendra Prasad, Kripalani First Civil Disobedience in India; Gandhiji became a member of the inquiry committee; Tinkathia abolished and 25% illegal exactions refunded; title of 'Mahatma'
Ahmedabad Mill Strike (February–March 1918 CE) Gujarat Dispute over withdrawal of Plague Bonus; workers demanded 50%, owners offered 20%; Leadership: Gandhiji and Anasuya Ben Sarabhai First Hunger Strike in India (3 days); Trusteeship principle; tribunal approved a 35% wage hike for workers
Kheda Satyagraha (March–June 1918 CE) Gujarat Forcible land revenue collection despite crop failure due to drought, violating the Revenue Code (crop yield under 1/4th); Leadership: Gandhiji, Vallabhbhai Patel, Indulal Yagnik First real Non-Cooperation movement in India; peasants' no-tax struggle; secret government order: 'collect revenue only from able peasants'

Historical Chronology
Chronology of Gandhiji's Major Activities from 1915 to 1919
Major events from his return to India to the nationwide anti-Rowlatt movement
Year / Date & Event Place & Associated Colleagues Historical Outcome & Impact
9 January 1915 (Return to India) Bombay (Apollo Bunder); Kasturba Gandhi and C. F. Andrews Returned to India after 21 years; Pravasi Bharatiya Divas; awarded Kaiser-i-Hind medal by the British government
25 May 1915 (Satyagraha Ashram Founded) Kochrab (Ahmedabad); Jivanlal Barrister and Maganlal Ashram founded for Satyagraha, eradication of untouchability, and Khadi; shifted to Sabarmati bank in 1917
February 1916 (BHU Inaugural Speech) Varanasi (BHU Campus); Malaviyaji, Annie Besant, Rajas and Maharajas Gandhiji's first public speech; sharp criticism of feudal ostentation; herald of mass-oriented politics
December 1916 (Lucknow Congress Session) Lucknow (Uttar Pradesh); Historic meeting with Rajkumar Shukla Heard the plight of Champaran indigo peasants; accepted Rajkumar Shukla's request to visit Champaran
April 1917 (Champaran Satyagraha) Motihari and Bettiah (Bihar); Rajendra Prasad, Kripalani, Anugrah Narayan First Civil Disobedience in India; Tinkathia system abolished; 25% illegal exactions refunded; title of 'Mahatma'
February–March 1918 (Ahmedabad Mill Strike) Ahmedabad (Gujarat); Anasuya Ben Sarabhai and Ambalal First Hunger Strike in India; principle of Trusteeship; secured 35% bonus justice for workers
March–June 1918 (Kheda Satyagraha) Kheda (Gujarat); Vallabhbhai Patel, Indulal Yagnik First real Non-Cooperation; refusal to pay revenue on crop failure; agreement to collect only from able peasants
February–April 1919 (Rowlatt Satyagraha) Bombay and all over India; Satyagraha Sabha (Gandhiji as President) First all-India nationwide hartal on 6 April; background to the Jallianwala Bagh massacre was formed
