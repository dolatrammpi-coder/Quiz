Art, Architecture, Literature, and Cultural Contributions of Regional Sultanates
Details of Indo-Islamic provincial architecture and growth of vernacular languages
Regional Style & Center Major Monuments & Architecture Cultural & Literary Features
Sharqi Style (Jaunpur) Atala Masjid, Jhanjhari Masjid, Lal Darwaza, Jama Masjid Complete absence of minarets; massive arched gateways (propylon); creation of Khayal singing and Jaunpuri ragas
Bengal Style (Pandua & Gaur) Adina Masjid, Chhota & Bada Sona Masjid, Eklakhi Mausoleum Use of brick and baked clay (terracotta); curved sloping roofs resembling bamboo huts (Bangla style); Krittivasi Ramayana

Mewar Style (Chittor & Kumbhalgarh) Vijay Stambha (Kirti Stambha), Kumbhalgarh Fort ramparts Traditional Hindu Rajput architecture; 36 km long impregnable rampart; encyclopedia of sculpture; composition of music treatises

Rajput Military Campaigns
Major Political and Military Campaigns of Rana Kumbha and Rana Sanga
Historic struggles of Mewar against Sultanate-era powers
Campaign / Battle & Year Opposing Sides Involved Strategic Outcome & Historical Impact
Battle of Sarangpur (1437 CE) Rana Kumbha vs. Mahmud Khalji I (Malwa) Resounding victory for Kumbha; Khalji imprisoned in Chittor for 6 months; construction of 9-storey Vijay Stambha at Chittor
Resistance to Champaner Alliance (1456–57 CE) Rana Kumbha vs. Combined Malwa & Gujarat armies Kumbha defeated both combined Muslim armies in the Battle of Badnor; sovereignty of Mewar remained intact
Battle of Khatoli (1517 CE) Rana Sanga vs. Ibrahim Lodi (Delhi) Lodi army defeated; Sanga lost an arm and became lame in one leg from an arrow; Lodi influence ended
Battle of Gagron (1519 CE) Rana Sanga vs. Mahmud Khalji II (Malwa) Malwa Sultan captured; Sanga treated him and restored his kingdom; direct Mewar influence established over Malwa
Battle of Bayana (February 1527 CE) Rana Sanga vs. Babur's Mughal vanguard Mughal force badly defeated and routed; Sanga captured Bayana fort; widespread panic spread among the Mughals
Battle of Khanwa (17 March 1527 CE) Rana Sanga (Rajput Confederacy) vs. Babur (Mughal Army) Babur won using artillery and Tulughma tactics; Sanga wounded and fell unconscious; Mughal power consolidated in North India
