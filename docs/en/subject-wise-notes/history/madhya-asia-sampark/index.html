Art Style Comparison
Detailed Comparative Study of Gandhara and Mathura Schools of Art
Differences in origin, materials, religious themes, and sculptural features
Art School & Center Patrons & Materials Used Key Features & Iconography
Gandhara School of Art (North-West India, Taxila) Shaka-Kushana rulers; bluish-grey schist stone and stucco (plaster) Greek influence (Apollo-like Buddha), wavy hair, realistic transparent drapery, serene/solemn posture
Mathura School of Art (Mathura, Central India) Kushana rulers; spotted red sandstone Indigenous origin, Buddhist, Jain, and Hindu sculptures, smiling Buddha with halo, Abhaya Mudra
Amaravati School of Art (Andhra Pradesh, Amaravati) Satavahana and Ikshvaku rulers; white marble Tribhanga posture, narrative scenes from the Buddha's life (Jataka tales), intense dynamism and expressive faces
