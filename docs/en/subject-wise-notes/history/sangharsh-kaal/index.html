Invader & Period Major Indian Campaigns & Regions Historical Impact & Consequences

Muhammad Ghori (1175–1206 CE) Multan, Gujarat, Punjab, Tarain (Haryana), and Chandawar Suppression of Rajput powers; formal establishment of a permanent Turko-Muslim empire (Delhi Sultanate) in India

Chronology of Key Invasions Between 1000 and 1027 CE
Year / Period & Campaign Campaign Target & Site Key Event & Historical Outcome
1001 CE (Battle of Peshawar) Hindu Shahi Kingdom (Peshawar) Ruler: Jayapala Jayapala was captured; released for 2.5 lakh dinars; committed self-immolation on a funeral pyre out of humiliation
1008–09 CE (Battle of Waihind) Hindu Shahi Kingdom (Waihind) Ruler: Anandapala Defeat of the Rajput confederacy; Turkic supremacy over Punjab consolidated; stampede among war elephants

1018 CE (Mathura–Kannauj) Ganga–Yamuna Doab Ruler: Rajyapala Pratihara Destruction of grand temples in Mathura; Rajyapala fled without fighting; decline of Pratihara power sealed
1019–20 CE (Kalinjar Campaign) Chandela Kingdom Ruler: Vidyadhara Chandela Vidyadhara offered stiff resistance to Mahmud; battle remained inconclusive; treaty of friendship and exchange of gifts
1025–26 CE (Somnath Campaign) Somnath Shiva Temple (Kathiawar, Gujarat) Bhima I fled; 50,000 defenders slain; plunder worth 20 lakh dinars; the Jyotirlinga was desecrated
1027 CE (Final Campaign) Sindh–Multan Frontier Target: Khokhars and Jats Fierce naval assault against Jats who had plundered him on return from Somnath; Mahmud's final campaign
