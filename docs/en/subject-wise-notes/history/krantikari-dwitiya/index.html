Major Actions & Events
Kakori, Saunders Assassination, Assembly Bombing & Chittagong Armoury Raid
Comparative overview of four epoch-making revolutionary events of the second phase
Historical Event & Date Place & Key Revolutionaries Sequence of Events, Objective & Immediate Outcome
Kakori Train Action (9 August 1925) Kakori, Lucknow (U.P.) Bismil, Ashfaq, Azad, Lahiri, Roshan Singh Seizure of government treasury from the 8-Down train; 4 executed (Bismil, Ashfaq, Lahiri, Roshan); Azad escaped; setback to HRA
Saunders Assassination (Lahore) (17 December 1928) Lahore Police Headquarters Bhagat Singh, Rajguru, Sukhdev, Azad Avenging Lala Lajpat Rai's death from lathi-charge; Assistant Superintendent of Police Saunders shot dead; vindication of national honour
Central Assembly Bombing (8 April 1929) Central Legislative Assembly, New Delhi Bhagat Singh & Batukeshwar Dutt Threw low-intensity bombs on empty benches to protest Public Safety & Trade Disputes Bills; distributed 'Inquilab Zindabad' leaflets; courted arrest
Chittagong Armoury Raid (18 April 1930) Chittagong (Undivided Bengal) Surya Sen ('Master Da'), Ananta Singh, Ganesh Ghosh Capture of British armouries and telegraph lines; proclamation of provisional revolutionary government; fierce battle at Jalalabad Hill

Trial / Case & Year Key Accused Revolutionaries Court Verdict & Historical Outcome
Kakori Conspiracy Case (1925–1927 CE) Ram Prasad Bismil, Ashfaqulla, Rajendra Lahiri, Roshan Singh, Sanyal Four executed (1927); Sanyal & Bakshi sentenced to life transportation (Kalapani); Manmathnath sentenced to 14 years; Azad remained absconding
