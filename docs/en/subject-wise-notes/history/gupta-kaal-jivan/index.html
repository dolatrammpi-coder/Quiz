Epigraphic Evidence
Major Gupta Inscriptions and Socio-Administrative Information Obtained
Historical sources, rulers, and authentic facts
Inscription & Location Concerned Ruler Social & Administrative Information Obtained
Prayag Prashasti (Prayagraj) Samudragupta Imperial expansion, administrative posts (Mahasandhivigrahika), and provincial policies.
Mehrauli Iron Pillar (Delhi) Chandragupta II Pinnacle of ancient Indian metallurgy and north-western conquests.
Mandsaur Inscription (Madhya Pradesh) Kumaragupta I Social and economic contribution of the silk weavers' guild (Tantuvaya).
Bhitari Pillar Inscription (UP) Skandagupta Wars against the Hunas and Pushyamitras and administrative crisis.
Junagadh Inscription (Gujarat) Skandagupta Repair of Sudarshan Lake, irrigation system, and administration of Chakrapalita.
Eran Inscription (M.P., 510 CE) Bhanugupta First clear epigraphic evidence of the Sati system and commander Goparaja.

Name of Temple Location Architectural Feature & Significance

Field of Art Major Centres / Examples Key Stylistic Features & Contribution
