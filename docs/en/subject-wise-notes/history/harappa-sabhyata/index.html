Phase and Approximate Period Main Regional Characteristic Key Features
Early Harappan — c. 3300–2600 BCE Regional farming settlements and early towns Expansion of permanent settlements, pottery traditions, and early urban organization
Mature Harappan — c. 2600–1900 BCE Extensive Harappan cultural zone Planned cities, standardized bricks, seals, weights and measures, crafts, and long-distance trade
Late / Post-Harappan — after c. 1900 BCE Regional cultural traditions Transformation in urban order, regionalization, and emergence of new local cultures
