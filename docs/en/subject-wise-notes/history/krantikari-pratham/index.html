Revolutionary Conspiracies
Major Revolutionary Conspiracies/Cases — Year, Revolutionaries, and Outcomes
Major political trials and actions of the First Phase (1897–1915)
Conspiracy / Case & Year Location & Associated Key Revolutionaries Sequence of Events, Legal Action, and Outcome
Rand Murder Case (1897 CE) Pune (Maharashtra) Damodar and Balkrishna Chapekar Assassination of Rand in protest against plague atrocities; Chapekar brothers hanged; sedition trial against Tilak
Muzaffarpur Bomb Case (30 April 1908) Muzaffarpur (Bihar) Khudiram Bose and Prafulla Chaki Bomb attack on Kingsford; two women of the Kennedy family killed; Chaki died by suicide; Khudiram Bose hanged
Alipore Conspiracy Case (1908–1909 CE) Calcutta (Bengal) Aurobindo Ghosh, Barindra Ghosh, Ullaskar Maniktala bomb factory unearthed; 34 accused; Aurobindo acquitted by C. R. Das; Barindra sentenced to life transportation (Kala Pani)
Nashik Conspiracy Case (1909–1910 CE) Nashik (Maharashtra) Anant Kanhere, V. D. Savarkar Collector Jackson shot dead; Kanhere hanged; Savarkar sentenced to double life imprisonment (50 years)
Curzon Wyllie Assassination (1 July 1909) London (England) Madan Lal Dhingra Assassination of Wyllie, political aide to the Secretary of State for India; Dhingra hanged at Pentonville Prison in London
Howrah-Sibpur Conspiracy Case (1910 CE) Howrah (Bengal) Jatindranath Mukherjee (Bagha Jatin) Assassination of DSP Shamsul Alam; Bagha Jatin tried; acquitted for lack of evidence; reorganization of Jugantar
Delhi Conspiracy Case (23 December 1912) Chandni Chowk (Delhi) Rash Behari Bose, Basant Biswas Bomb thrown at Viceroy Lord Hardinge; Hardinge injured; Master Amir Chand, Avadh Behari, Balmukund, and Basant hanged; Bose escaped to Japan
