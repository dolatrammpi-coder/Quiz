Soil Structure and Geographical Classification of Uttar Pradesh

Into how many major physiographic regions are the soils of Uttar Pradesh divided based on surface structure and geological composition?
Three major regions: 1. Bhabar and Tarai Region, 2. Great Gangetic Plain, 3. Southern Plateau (Bundelkhand) Region

Which type of soil covers the largest share of the total geographical area of Uttar Pradesh?
Alluvial Soil (in the plains)

Which institutions conducted the first scientific survey and classification of soils in Uttar Pradesh?
Department of Agriculture, Uttar Pradesh and the Indian Council of Agricultural Research (ICAR)
Tap to view answer
Based on origin and formation, under which category do the plain soils of Uttar Pradesh fall?
Transported / Azonal Soil (deposited by rivers)
Tap to view answer
To which category does the soil of the Southern Plateau (Bundelkhand) region of Uttar Pradesh belong?
Residual / Zonal Soil (formed by weathering of parent rocks)
Tap to view answer
Which two major nutrients are generally found to be widely deficient in the soils of Uttar Pradesh?
Nitrogen (N) and Organic Carbon (Humus)
Tap to view answer
Which elements are found in sufficient abundance in most alluvial soils of Uttar Pradesh?
Potash and Lime

In the central and southern Gangetic plains

Between 6.5 and 7.5 (neutral soil)

Soils of the Bhabar and Tarai Region

Which soil belt is found along the northern border of Uttar Pradesh adjoining the Shivalik foothills of the Himalayas?
Bhabar Soil
Tap to view answer
What is the primary physical composition of Bhabar soil?
Porous soil composed of pebbles, stones, gravel, and coarse sand
Tap to view answer
Why does river water disappear from the surface in the Bhabar soil belt?
Due to high porosity caused by large pebbles and boulders
Tap to view answer
Is Bhabar soil suitable for cultivating regular agricultural crops?
No, being highly porous and stony, it is unsuitable for agriculture (only shrubs and forests grow)

What is the fine-grained marshy belt running parallel immediately south of the Bhabar belt called?
Tarai Soil
Tap to view answer
From the deposition of which elements is Tarai soil formed?
Fine silt, alluvium, and clay brought down by rivers
Tap to view answer
Which nutrients are naturally abundant in Tarai soil?
Nitrogen (N) and organic matter (Humus)
Tap to view answer
Which essential nutrient is deficient in Tarai soil?
Phosphate
Tap to view answer
For the bumper production of which two crops is Tarai soil world-renowned in Uttar Pradesh?
Sugarcane and Paddy (Rice)
Tap to view answer
What is the general chemical nature of Tarai soil?
Slightly acidic to neutral (pH 6.0 to 7.2)
Tap to view answer
In which districts of Uttar Pradesh is Tarai soil most extensively found?
Lakhimpur Kheri, Pilibhit, Bahraich, Shravasti, Balrampur, Siddharthnagar, and Maharajganj
Tap to view answer
Due to dense forests and tall grasses in the Tarai region, which element remains at a high level in the soil?
Organic Carbon
Tap to view answer
What measure is essential for a permanent solution to waterlogging in Tarai soil?
Proper drainage system and bunding
Tap to view answer
In which districts are jute and oilseeds successfully cultivated in Tarai soils?
Maharajganj, Kushinagar, and Deoria

Alluvial Soil of the Ganga-Yamuna Plain: Older Alluvium (Bangar)

What is the older alluvial soil found on elevated unflooded terraces of the Ganga-Yamuna plain called?
Bangar Soil (Older Alluvium)

River floodwaters normally do not reach
Tap to view answer
What is the typical colour of Bangar soil?
Light yellow, brown, or muddy brownish-grey
Tap to view answer
What are the hard nodules of calcium carbonate found in mature layers of Bangar soil called?
Kankar (Kankar nodules)
Tap to view answer
How does the presence of Kankar affect the water retention and tillage capacity of Bangar soil?
The land becomes hard and internal drainage is impeded
Tap to view answer
By what local names is Bangar soil known based on particle texture?
Domat (Loam), Matiyar Domat (Clay Loam), Balui Domat (Sandy Loam), and Karail
Tap to view answer
The deep, fertile Bangar soil of the middle Doab with balanced moisture retention is called
Loam Soil (Domat Mitti)
Tap to view answer
Which chemical constituents are deficient in Bangar soil?
Nitrogen, Phosphorus, and Humus
Tap to view answer
Which crops are most extensively cultivated in Bangar soil with adequate irrigation and fertilizer management?
Wheat, paddy, maize, mustard, potato, gram, and sugarcane
Tap to view answer
In which districts of Uttar Pradesh is Bangar soil widely distributed?
Meerut, Aligarh, Bulandshahr, Muzaffarnagar, Etah, Mainpuri, Kanpur, and Lucknow
Tap to view answer
What problem has arisen on Bangar land due to centuries of continuous cultivation and excessive use of chemical fertilizers?
Decline in soil fertility and spread of salinity
Tap to view answer
Within what range does the pH value of Bangar soil generally lie?
7.0 to 8.2 (neutral to slightly alkaline)
Tap to view answer
What are the sandy soil mounds deposited by strong winds along riverbanks in the Bangar region called?
Bhur
Tap to view answer
For the cultivation of which crops is Bhur soil considered suitable?
Bajra, maize, groundnut, and watermelon-muskmelon

Newer Alluvium (Khadar), Kachhari & Bhur Soils

What is the newer alluvial soil deposited annually in river floodplains called?
Khadar Soil (Kachhari Soil)
Tap to view answer
By the annual renewal of which fine sediments is Khadar soil formed?
Fine sand, silt, and clay
Tap to view answer
Between Bangar and Khadar, which soil is naturally more fertile?
Khadar soil (because a fresh fertile layer is deposited every year)
Tap to view answer
What is the typical appearance/colour of Khadar soil?
Light brown, ash-grey, or pale muddy white
Tap to view answer
Are Kankar nodules (calcareous nodules) found in Khadar soil?
No, it is free of Kankar and has a soft texture
Tap to view answer
How does the moisture-holding capacity of Khadar soil compare to that of Bangar soil?
It is higher (requiring less irrigation for crops)
Tap to view answer
What is the Zaid cultivation practised in riverbeds (Kachhar) after floodwaters recede called?
Diyara Farming (Zaid Diara Farming)
Tap to view answer
For the production of which seasonal crops are Khadar and Kachhari soils considered best?
Zaid crops (watermelon, muskmelon, cucumber, kakdi, parwal, and vegetables)
Tap to view answer
In which major river valleys of Uttar Pradesh is Khadar soil primarily distributed?
Floodplains of the Ganga, Yamuna, Ghaghara, Rapti, Ramganga, and Gandak rivers
Tap to view answer
By what other local names is Khadar soil known in eastern Uttar Pradesh?
Balua, Matiyar, or Kachhar
Tap to view answer
For which crops is the Khadar region at the confluence of the Ganga and Ramganga rivers particularly famous?
Potato, maize, and green chilli
Tap to view answer
In which districts of Uttar Pradesh is Bhur soil prominently found along the banks of the Ganga and its tributaries?
Moradabad, Bijnor, Sambhal, Amroha, Budaun, and Shahjahanpur
Tap to view answer
Which chemical elements are present in balanced proportions in Khadar soil?
Lime, magnesium, and potash are adequate
Tap to view answer
What natural benefit does annual flooding provide to the soil in Khadar areas?
Soil fertility is naturally rejuvenated without chemical fertilizers

Black Soils of Bundelkhand: Mar (Mad) & Kabar

By what local names is the black soil found in the Southern Plateau (Bundelkhand) region of Uttar Pradesh known?
Mar (Mad) and Kabar
Tap to view answer
By which scientific names is black soil known nationally and internationally?
Regur Soil / Tropical Black Earth / Vertisols
Tap to view answer
From the weathering of which parent rock was the black soil of Bundelkhand formed?

Tap to view answer
Why is Mar (Mad) soil black in colour?
Due to the presence of titaniferous magnetite and humus
Tap to view answer
What is the most distinctive physical characteristic of Mar soil?
It becomes extremely sticky when wet and develops deep, wide cracks upon drying
Tap to view answer
By what epithet is black soil known due to the automatic formation of cracks upon drying?
'Self-ploughing Soil'
Tap to view answer
What is the percentage of clay in Mar soil?
Approximately 60% or more (very high water-holding capacity)
Tap to view answer
Which are the two major variants of Kabar soil?
Black Kabar (deep black) and Brown Kabar
Tap to view answer
How does Kabar soil compare to Mar soil?
It is less sticky than Mar and contains a mixture of sand
Tap to view answer
For cultivating which crops are the black soils (Mar and Kabar) of Bundelkhand considered best?
Gram, wheat, oilseeds (linseed/sesame), and cotton
Tap to view answer
In which districts of Uttar Pradesh is the concentration of Mar and Kabar soils found?
Jhansi, Jalaun, Hamirpur, and Mahoba
Tap to view answer
Which chemical constituents are abundant in black soil?
Iron, lime, calcium, aluminium, and magnesium carbonate
Tap to view answer
Which elements are deficient in black soil?
Nitrogen, phosphorus, and organic carbon
Tap to view answer
Why does ploughing become extremely difficult in Mar soil during the rainy season?
Because it becomes extremely sticky, causing ploughs and wheels to sink into it
