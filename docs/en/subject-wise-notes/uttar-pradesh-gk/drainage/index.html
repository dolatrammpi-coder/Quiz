Nature and Classification of the Drainage System of Uttar Pradesh

In which general direction do most of the rivers of Uttar Pradesh flow?
From north-west to south-east
Tap to view answer
Into how many main categories are the rivers of Uttar Pradesh divided based on their sources of origin?
Three categories: rivers originating from the Himalayas, rivers originating from the plains, and rivers originating from the southern plateau
Tap to view answer
What is the main water-flow nature of Himalayan rivers?
Perennial — receiving water from both snowmelt and monsoon rainfall
Tap to view answer
What is the water-flow nature of rivers originating from the plains (lakes and marshes) of Uttar Pradesh?
Non-perennial / rain-fed — water flow reduces drastically in summer
Tap to view answer
What is the key characteristic of rivers coming from the southern plateau region (Peninsular India)?
Generally drying up in summer and flowing swiftly during the rainy season
