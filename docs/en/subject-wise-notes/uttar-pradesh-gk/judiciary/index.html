Constitutional Status, Appointment & Powers of High Court Judges

Which Article of the Indian Constitution provides for a High Court for each State?
Article 214

Under which Article is a High Court accorded the status of a Court of Record?
Article 215

Who appoints the Chief Justice and other Judges of a High Court?
By the President in consultation with the Chief Justice of India and the Governor of the concerned State (Article 217)
Tap to view answer
Who administers the oath of office and secrecy to the Judges of a High Court?
The Governor of the concerned State (Article 219)
Tap to view answer
What is the retirement age limit for a Judge of a High Court?
62 years (increased from 60 to 62 by the 15th Constitutional Amendment Act, 1963)

On the Consolidated Fund of India

Under which Article does a High Court issue writs for the enforcement of Fundamental Rights and other legal rights?

How many types of writs can a High Court issue under Article 226?
Total 5 types of writs (Habeas Corpus, Mandamus, Prohibition, Certiorari, Quo Warranto)
Tap to view answer
Under which Article does a High Court have the power of superintendence over all subordinate courts and tribunals?
Article 227
Tap to view answer
The process of removing a High Court Judge from office is similar to that of whom?

At least 10 years (or an advocate of a High Court for 10 years)

The President in consultation with the Chief Justice of India (CJI) (Article 222)
