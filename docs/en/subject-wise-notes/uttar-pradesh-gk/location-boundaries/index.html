Physiographic Division: Bhabar & Tarai Region

On the basis of geographical and topographical structure, into how many major physical divisions is Uttar Pradesh divided?
Three major physical divisions: 1. Bhabar and Tarai Region 2. Great Gangetic Plain 3. Southern Plateau Region
Tap to view answer
What is the narrow belt composed of pebbles, stones, and gravel at the foothills of the Shivaliks called?
Bhabar Region
Tap to view answer
What is the main hydrological characteristic of the Bhabar region?
Due to the high permeability of pebbles and stones, rivers disappear beneath the surface
Tap to view answer
In which direction is the Bhabar belt wider and in which direction does it become narrower in Uttar Pradesh?
Wider in the west (Saharanpur, ~34 km) and becomes narrower towards the east
Tap to view answer
The belt of fine particles, marshy, and damp land located immediately south of the Bhabar region is called:
Tarai Region
Tap to view answer
In which region do the rivers that disappeared in the Bhabar reappear on the surface?

What are the main climatic and geographical characteristics of the Tarai region?
Heavy rainfall (150-200 cm), high humidity, marshy soil, and dense forests
Tap to view answer
Which two crops are cultivated on the largest scale in the Tarai region?
Paddy (rice) and sugarcane
Tap to view answer
