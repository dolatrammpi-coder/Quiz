Taj Mahal: UNESCO Criteria, Architecture & Conservation

In which year was the Taj Mahal included in the UNESCO World Heritage List?
In 1983 (under Cultural Criterion i: 'a masterpiece of human creative genius')
Tap to view answer
In whose memory did Mughal Emperor Shah Jahan build the Taj Mahal?
His beloved wife Mumtaz Mahal (birth name: Arjumand Banu Begum)
Tap to view answer
What was the main construction period of the Taj Mahal?
From 1631 to 1648 (full completion including surrounding structures: 1653 CE)
Tap to view answer
Who were the chief architects of the Taj Mahal?
Ustad Ahmad Lahori (whom Shah Jahan conferred the title 'Nadir-ul-Asar') and Ustad Isa Khan
Tap to view answer
Which special marble was used in the construction of the Taj Mahal?
High-grade white marble from Makrana in Nagaur district, Rajasthan
Tap to view answer
What is the art of inlaying precious stones into the white marble walls of the Taj Mahal called?
Pietra Dura (Parchinkari)
Tap to view answer
On which traditional Islamic garden style is the garden layout of the Taj Mahal based?
Charbagh Style — in which four canals symbolize the four rivers of paradise
Tap to view answer
What is the height of the Taj Mahal's main dome and the structure of its plinth?
Dome height is about 73 metres (240 feet), with four slightly tilted minarets (40 metres) at the four corners of the plinth
Tap to view answer
Why were the four minarets of the Taj Mahal built with a slight outward tilt?
So that in the event of an earthquake, the minarets would fall outward rather than onto the main mausoleum
Tap to view answer
What are the mosque inside the Taj Mahal complex and the identical building built opposite it for symmetry called?
Mosque in the west and its symmetrical counterpart 'Jawab' (guest house) in the east
Tap to view answer
What is the 10,400 sq km zone declared to protect the Taj Mahal from air pollution (acid rain / marble cancer)?
Taj Trapezium Zone (TTZ) — by Supreme Court order in 1996
Tap to view answer
Which natural paste is used by the ASI to remove yellowing and restore the lustre of the Taj Mahal's marble?
Multani Mitti clay pack treatment (Mud Pack Therapy)
Tap to view answer
In which year was the Taj Mahal chosen among the New 7 Wonders of the World?
In 2007 (through a global poll)
Tap to view answer
Which are the two main entry gates for tourists at the Taj Mahal complex?
Western Gate and Eastern Gate (Southern Gate was earlier used for the caravanserai)

In 1571 (remained the Mughal capital from 1571 to 1585)

In honour of the renowned Chishti Sufi saint Sheikh Salim Chishti

Akbar's victory over Gujarat (1572–73 CE)

Approximately 176 feet (53.6 metres) — 134 feet from the platform and 176 feet including the steps

Jodha Bai's Palace (Mariam-uz-Zamani Palace) — Gujarati and Rajput architectural influence
