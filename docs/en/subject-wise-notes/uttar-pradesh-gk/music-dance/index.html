Kathak Dance: Origin, Form, Elements, and Traditions

Which is the only classical dance of Uttar Pradesh?
Kathak
Tap to view answer
From which ancient proverb is the etymology of the word 'Kathak' derived?
'Katha kahe so Kathak kahave' (one who narrates a story through dance-acting is called a Kathak)
Tap to view answer
In the ancient tradition of Kathak dance, episodes from which texts were primarily staged by storytellers?

Tap to view answer
Which are the oldest texts providing a classical foundation to Kathak dance?
'Natyashastra' by Bharata Muni and 'Abhinaya Darpana' by Nandikeshvara
Tap to view answer
What are the main sequential elements of a Kathak dance performance?
Aamad, Thaat, Toda, Tukda, Paran, Tihai, Gat-Bhav, Tatkar, and Padhant

Which are the major traditional musical instruments accompanying Kathak dance?
Tabla, pakhawaj (mridang), sarangi, harmonium, and manjeera

Where did Kathak dance evolve after moving out of temple courtyards during the Mughal period?
In royal courts (Mughal and Awadh courts), where courtly attire and grace (nazakat) were added
