Gram Sabha & Gram Panchayat: Organization, Pradhan & Tenure

What is the definition of 'Gram Sabha' under Article 243(b) of the Constitution?
A body consisting of persons registered in the electoral rolls relating to a village comprised within the area of a Panchayat at the village level
Tap to view answer
What is the minimum number of general meetings of the Gram Sabha mandatory in a year in Uttar Pradesh?
Minimum two meetings in a year (after Kharif harvest and after Rabi harvest)
Tap to view answer
Who presides over the meetings of the Gram Sabha?
Gram Pradhan (Up-Pradhan in their absence)
Tap to view answer
What is the minimum population norm generally required to constitute a Gram Panchayat in Uttar Pradesh?
Population of 1,000
Tap to view answer
How many members (ward members) are there in a Gram Panchayat based on population?
9 to 15 members (up to 1,000: 9; up to 2,000: 11; up to 3,000: 13; above 3,000: 15 members)
Tap to view answer
By what method are the Gram Pradhan and Gram Panchayat members elected?
Direct voting (adult suffrage) by the voters of the Gram Sabha
Tap to view answer
What is the minimum age limit for a candidate to contest Gram Panchayat elections?
Minimum 21 years
Tap to view answer
What is the normal tenure of a Gram Panchayat?
5 years from the date appointed for its first meeting (Article 243-E)
Tap to view answer
If a Gram Panchayat is dissolved before completing 5 years, within what period must fresh elections be held?
Within 6 months from the date of dissolution
Tap to view answer
For how long does a newly elected Gram Panchayat function after mid-term dissolution?
Only for the remainder of the period (Remaining Period)
Tap to view answer
After how many years of tenure can a no-confidence motion be brought to remove a Gram Pradhan?
After completion of the first 2 years of tenure
Tap to view answer
How many days' prior written notice is mandatory to bring a no-confidence motion against a Gram Pradhan?
At least 15 days in advance (signed by 1/3rd of the Gram Sabha members)
Tap to view answer
If a no-confidence motion fails, for how long can another no-confidence motion not be brought?
For the next 1 year
Tap to view answer

One Kshetra Panchayat territorial constituency (ward) for approximately 2,000 population

How are the members of the Kshetra Panchayat (BDC members) elected?
Directly by the adult voters of the area

How is the head of the Kshetra Panchayat (Block Pramukh) elected?
Indirectly by the elected members of the Kshetra Panchayat (BDC)

At least once every 2 months

After at least 1 year (amended in 2018)

Reservation System in Local Bodies (Women, SC/ST, OBC)

Under which Article of the Constitution is a minimum 1/3rd (33%) reservation for women mandatory in Panchayats?
Article 243-D(3)
Tap to view answer
In which Article is the constitutional provision for reservation for women in urban local bodies?
Article 243-T(3)
Tap to view answer
What percentage of reservation is provided for women in Panchayats and urban local bodies in Uttar Pradesh?
Minimum 33.33% (one-third) of total seats
Tap to view answer
What is the basis of reservation for Scheduled Castes (SC) and Scheduled Tribes (ST) in Panchayats in Uttar Pradesh?
In proportion to their population in the respective area (Article 243-D)
Tap to view answer
What is the maximum percentage of reservation provided for Other Backward Classes (OBC) in local bodies in Uttar Pradesh?
Maximum 27% reservation (subject to the 50% total reservation ceiling)
Tap to view answer
Which commission was constituted to determine OBC reservation in urban local body elections in Uttar Pradesh?
Justice Ram Avtar Singh Commission (December 2022, 5-member dedicated commission)
Tap to view answer
What is the 'Triple Test' prescribed by the Supreme Court for OBC reservation?
1. Setting up a dedicated commission 2. Collecting empirical data on backwardness 3. Not exceeding the 50% total reservation ceiling
Tap to view answer
Does reservation also apply to the posts of Gram Pradhan and Zila Panchayat Adhyaksha in Panchayats?
Yes, reservation is applied on a rotational basis
Tap to view answer
On the basis of which rule is reservation determined in Uttar Pradesh?
By the rotational system (roster system)
Tap to view answer
Are seats for SC, ST, and OBC women included within the 33% seats reserved for women?
Yes, 1/3rd of the seats of the respective category are reserved for women of that category
Tap to view answer
Do women have 50% reservation in Panchayats in Uttar Pradesh like in some other Indian states?
No, the current legal reservation in Uttar Pradesh is 33.33% (1/3rd)
Tap to view answer
Where does the final judicial resolution of any dispute regarding reservation take place?
In the High Court or the Supreme Court
Tap to view answer
Who issues the reservation of seats in the local body election notification in Uttar Pradesh?
Panchayati Raj Department and Urban Development Department
