Differences Among Major Literary Genres (Autobiography, Biography, Memoir)
Books Authors
GenreDefinitionFamous Example
AutobiographyA detailed, chronological account of one's own life written by the person themselves.My Experiments with Truth (Mahatma Gandhi)
BiographyA factual and researched account of another person's life.The Man Who Knew Infinity (on Ramanujan by Robert Kanigel)
MemoirAn emotional account of a specific event, period, or memory from the author's life.The Diary of a Young Girl (Anne Frank)
