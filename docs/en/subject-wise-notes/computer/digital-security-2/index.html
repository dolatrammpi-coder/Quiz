One-Liner Questions

What is Cybercrime?
Any illegal act committed using a computer, digital device, or network as either the tool or the target.

What is the basic definition of Digital/Cyber Fraud?
Deceptive activity carried out using the internet and digital devices to obtain financial or personal gain.

What is the main objective of a Denial of Service (DoS) Attack?
Sending massive amounts of fake traffic to a server or network to make its services unavailable to legitimate users.

How does a Distributed Denial of Service (DDoS) Attack differ from DoS?
Instead of using just one computer, the attacker simultaneously uses a network of many infected devices called a Botnet.

What is the defining feature of a Ransomware cyber attack?
It encrypts the victim's system or files and demands a ransom in exchange for the decryption key.

What is Cryptojacking?
Unauthorized use of a user's computer or mobile processing power for cryptocurrency mining without the user's permission.

What is meant by Cyber Espionage?
The process of obtaining confidential data, intellectual property, or strategic information from rival companies or adversary nations without authorization.
Tap to view answer
What does Cyber Stalking mean?
Continuously monitoring, intimidating, or mentally harassing an individual via the internet, social media, or email.

Sharing messages and photos on digital platforms (such as social media and messaging apps) to humiliate, threaten, or demean someone.

The criminal deceives the telecom provider to obtain a duplicate SIM card for the victim's mobile number, causing all 2FA OTPs to be routed to the criminal.

Malware downloading in the background without any clicks, triggered simply by visiting a malicious or compromised website.

What does 'Social Engineering' mean in cyber security?

What are the five major phases of Social Engineering?
Information Gathering, Developing Relationship, Exploitation, Execution, and Exit.
Tap to view answer
What is the standard definition of Phishing?
Masquerading as a trustworthy entity to obtain sensitive credentials (passwords, OTPs) via email or messages.
Tap to view answer
How does Spear Phishing differ from ordinary phishing?
It is a targeted attack crafted specifically with a particular individual or organization in mind.
Tap to view answer
Whom does a Whaling Attack target?
Top-level executives of an organization such as the CEO, CFO, or senior government officials.
Tap to view answer
What is CEO Fraud (Business Email Compromise - BEC)?
Creating a spoofed email of a company's top executive to instruct employees to transfer funds immediately.
Tap to view answer
What is meant by the Pretexting social engineering technique?
Creating a fabricated scenario or false role (pretext) to win the victim's trust and obtain sensitive data.
Tap to view answer
What does Baiting mean?
Luring a victim with an enticement (such as free software or leaving an infected USB drive) to trick them into loading malware.
Tap to view answer
On what principle does a Quid Pro Quo attack operate?
Demanding passwords or system access from a user in exchange for a service or assistance (such as fake technical support).
Tap to view answer
What type of security breach is Tailgating (Piggybacking)?
Physically entering a restricted area without authentication by closely following an authorized person.
Tap to view answer
What is Dumpster Diving in cyber security?
Searching through a company's or individual's trash/dumpster for discarded sensitive documents, password notes, or hardware.
Tap to view answer
How is protection against Shoulder Surfing possible?
Covering the keypad when entering confidential information and using privacy screen filters.
Tap to view answer
What is Identity Theft?
Using another person's personal identification credentials (such as Aadhaar, PAN, or name) without their consent for financial or criminal gain.
Tap to view answer
What is Synthetic Identity Theft?
Combining real and fabricated information to construct a brand-new fake identity.

Automatically trying all possible combinations until the correct password is discovered.

What is an Evil Twin Wi-Fi attack?

Copying the content of a previously received legitimate email, replacing only the link or attachment with a malicious one, and resending it.

What does 'Confidentiality' mean in the CIA Triad?
Ensuring that data remains accessible only to authorized personnel.
Tap to view answer
What does 'Integrity' represent in the CIA Triad?
Keeping data accurate and protected from any unauthorized modification, alteration, or destruction.
Tap to view answer
What does 'Availability' mean in the CIA Triad?
Ensuring that data and systems remain readily available whenever authorized users need them.
Tap to view answer
What is the importance of the 'Non-repudiation' principle in cyber security?
