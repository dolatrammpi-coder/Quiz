Dialect / Sub-DialectMajor Regions of PrevalenceLinguistic Group & Key Feature
KhasparjiaAlmora (Barahmandal Pargana)Central Pahari; standard and literary dialect of Kumaoni
SrinagariSrinagar, Pauri, and central Alaknanda ValleyCentral Pahari; standard and refined dialect of Garhwali
KumaiyaChampawat and Kali Kumaon regionKumaoni dialect; partial influence of Nepali and Awadhi
SoriyaliPithoragarh (Sor Valley)Kumaoni dialect; pure pronunciation, simple vocabulary
SalaniPauri Garhwal (Salan region)Garhwali sub-dialect; widely spoken in southern Garhwal
JaunsariChakrata, Tyuni, Kalsi (Dehradun)Western Pahari group; close resemblance to Sirmauri and Himachali
RawaltiUttarkashi (Rawain) and Tehri (Jaunpur)Transitional dialect intermediate between Garhwali and Jaunsari
RangDharchula (Vyas, Darma, Chaudas Valleys)Tibeto-Burman language family; identity of Bhotiya culture
