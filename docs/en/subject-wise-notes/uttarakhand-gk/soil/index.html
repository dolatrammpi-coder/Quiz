Soil Conservation Methods, Hill Measures, and Applications
Soils
Soil Conservation MethodImplementation Technique / MethodKey Environmental & Agricultural Benefits
Contour PlowingPlowing along equal-elevation lines perpendicular to hill slopesReduces rainwater runoff velocity, prevents topsoil erosion
Terraced Step FarmingCutting steep slopes into flat steps with bundingFoundation of hill agriculture, water harvesting, retaining wall stabilization
Strip CroppingDense grass and pulse strips between main cropsChecks wind and water erosion, binds soil through root networks
Check Dam & Retaining Wall ConstructionStone/wire-mesh barriers across streams and gadherasControls gully erosion, traps silt, recharges groundwater
AfforestationDense plantation of Banj, Utis, and Bhimal on barren slopesStabilizes slopes via deep root systems, builds humus
MulchingCovering exposed field soil with grass and leavesConserves moisture, protects against raindrop impact, increases organic manure
