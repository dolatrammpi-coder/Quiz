Uttarakhand MSME Policy: Category-Wise Classification & Financial Incentives
Industries and MSMEs
MSME CategoryCovered Geographical Areas / DistrictsCapital Subsidy & Incentives
Category 'A' (Extremely Remote)Pithoragarh, Uttarkashi, Chamoli, Rudraprayag, Champawat50% subsidy on capital investment (up to Rs 40 lakh), 5% interest subsidy
Category 'B' (Remote Hilly)Almora, Pauri, Tehri, Bageshwar, hilly parts of Nainital and Dehradun40% subsidy on capital investment (up to Rs 35 lakh), 5% interest subsidy
Category 'C' (Moderately Sloping)Sub-montane areas of Dehradun and Nainital (Ramnagar, Kotdwar periphery)30% subsidy on capital investment (up to Rs 30 lakh), 3% interest subsidy
Category 'D' (Plains)Haridwar, Udham Singh Nagar, plain areas of Dehradun and NainitalStandard industrial benefits, minimal capital subsidy
Women / SC-ST EntrepreneursEntire state of Uttarakhand5% additional financial subsidy over the standard limit
