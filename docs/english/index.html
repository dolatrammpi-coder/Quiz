General English MCQ Quiz: Complete Grammar, Vocabulary & Comprehension Practice

General English is a core scoring pillar across national and state competitive examinations including SSC (CGL, CHSL, CPO, MTS, Stenographer), Banking (IBPS PO/Clerk, SBI PO/Clerk, RBI Assistant), Defence (NDA, CDS, AFCAT), Teaching Eligibility Tests (CTET, UPTET, DSSSB, KVS) and Civil Services CSAT. Mastering English requires a balanced combination of grammatical rule accuracy, contextual vocabulary retention, and fast reading comprehension skills.

Structured 22-Chapter English Curriculum on Quiz Study

  • Core Grammar Foundations: Parts of Speech, Tenses & Time Sequence, Subject-Verb Agreement (Syntax), Articles (Definite & Indefinite usage and omissions), Prepositions & Fixed Prepositional Phrases, Conjunctions & Connectors, Modals & Auxiliaries, and Conditional Sentences (Zero, First, Second, Third, and Mixed Conditionals).
  • Sentence Transformation & Usage: Active and Passive Voice transformation rules across all tenses, Direct and Indirect Speech (Narration), Error Spotting (identifying grammatical flaws in segmented sentences), Sentence Correction / Phrase Replacement, and Fill in the Blanks.
  • High-Frequency Vocabulary: Synonyms, Antonyms, One Word Substitution (covering governance, sciences, phobias, and human traits), Idioms & Phrases, and Spelling Test rules.
  • Reading & Discourse Skills: Reading Comprehension passages, Cloze Test (contextual gap-filling), Sentence Rearrangement (Para Jumbles / PQRS), and Paragraph Completion.
