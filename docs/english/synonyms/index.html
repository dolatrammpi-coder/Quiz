What are Synonyms?

Synonyms are words which have same or similar meaning - different word, same meaning. Example: Big = Large, Small = Little, Happy = Joyful, Sad = Unhappy, Fast = Quick, Slow = Sluggish. For SSC CGL, CHSL, CPO, Railway, Banking, CTET synonyms are very important because direct synonym questions asked - Choose synonym of Abandon, Honest, Brave, Diligent. One word given, four options given, you have to choose same meaning word. Synonym does not mean exactly same 100% but similar meaning in context. Example: Answer and Reply are synonyms both mean jawab, but Answer is more general, Reply is answer to question. Begin and Start are synonyms both mean shuru karna. End and Finish are synonyms both mean khatam. Buy and Purchase are synonyms both mean kharidna, Purchase more formal. Synonyms help to avoid repetition in writing - instead of using same word again and again, use synonym for variety - He is happy, He is joyful, He is cheerful all same happy meaning but different words for variety.

Important Rule: Contextual Synonyms - Synonym depends on context sentence - word may have different meaning in different context. Example: Hard has two meanings - Hard = Difficult (Hard work = Difficult work) synonym Difficult, Hard = Solid (Hard stone = Solid stone) synonym Solid. So same word Hard has two synonyms based on context Difficult or Solid. Light has two meanings - Light = Not heavy (Light bag) synonym Not heavy, Light = Bright (Light colour) synonym Bright. So always check context sentence for contextual synonym. Exam trick: If word given alone without sentence, choose most common meaning synonym. If sentence given, choose synonym according to sentence context. One-word synonym questions - just word given, choose most common synonym.

These are easy common synonyms asked in basic exams. Exam-Oriented Synonyms: SSC CGL CHSL high level vocabulary synonyms - Abandon-Leave, Diligent-Hardworking, Brave-Courageous, Honest-Truthful, Generous-Kind, Arrogant-Proud, Humble-Modest, Punctual-Timely, Diligent-Industrious, Meticulous-Careful, Ambiguous-Unclear, Benevolent-Kind, Cordial-Friendly, Ephemeral-Short-lived. These are tough formal words for SSC high level. One-Word Synonym Questions: Only one word given without sentence - Find synonym of Abandon? Options: Leave, Come, Stay, Remain - Answer Leave because Abandon means Leave. Find synonym of Honest? Truthful. This is direct one-word synonym. Contextual Synonyms: Word with sentence context - He is hardworking? Hardworking context? Or sentence: The light was bright - Light synonym Bright in bright context. The bag is light - Light synonym Not heavy in weight context. So contextual synonym depends on sentence meaning.

Tips for Synonym Questions in SSC

Most important SSC tricks: 1) Learn 500 most frequent SSC synonyms list by heart - Abandon-Leave, Abundant-Plentiful, Brave-Courageous, Diligent-Hardworking, Honest-Truthful, Generous-Kind, etc. 2) Context matters - if sentence given, read sentence meaning then choose synonym according to sentence context, not just word alone. Example: Light in weight context synonym Not heavy, Light in brightness context synonym Bright. 3) Part of speech same - Synonym must be same part of speech - Noun synonym noun, Verb synonym verb, Adjective synonym adjective. Example: Honest is adjective, Truthful is adjective same part adjective adjective, not Truth is noun wrong part. 4) Elimination - If you don't know meaning, eliminate opposite meaning options - if word is positive, eliminate negative options. 5) Root words - Learn Latin Greek roots - Bene = Good Benevolent = Good kind, Mal = Bad Malicious = Bad. 6) Use in sentence - If confused, make sentence with word and options - which fits same meaning? He is diligent student = He is hardworking student fits same meaning diligent = hardworking.

Exam Tip: SSC most asked synonyms repeat every year - Learn these 20 by heart: Abandon-Leave/Forsake, Abundant-Plentiful/Ample, Brave-Courageous/Bold, Benevolent-Kind/Generous, Candid-Frank/Straightforward, Cordial-Friendly/Warm, Diligent-Hardworking/Industrious, Ephemeral-Short-lived/Transient, Frank-Candid, Generous-Kind/Liberal, Honest-Truthful/Sincere, Humble-Modest/Meek, Industrious-Hardworking, Meticulous-Careful/Detailed, Modest-Humble, Punctual-Timely/Regular, Truthful-Honest, Wealthy-Rich/Affluent, Diligent-Industrious, Ample-Plentiful. For contextual synonyms, always read full sentence - synonym must fit sentence meaning, not just word meaning alone.
