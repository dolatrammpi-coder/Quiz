Major Tense-Wise Active and Passive Structures
Structure/Case Transformation Rule Example
Simple Present
उत्तर देखें
Object + is/am/are + V3 (+ by subject)
उत्तर देखें
A letter is written by him.
Present Continuous
उत्तर देखें
Object + is/am/are + being + V3 (+ by subject)
उत्तर देखें
A letter is being written.
Present Perfect
उत्तर देखें
Object + has/have + been + V3 (+ by subject)
उत्तर देखें
A letter has been written.
Simple Past
उत्तर देखें
Object + was/were + V3 (+ by subject)
उत्तर देखें
A letter was written by him.
Past Continuous
उत्तर देखें
Object + was/were + being + V3 (+ by subject)
उत्तर देखें
A letter was being written.
Past Perfect
उत्तर देखें
Object + had + been + V3 (+ by subject)
उत्तर देखें
A letter had been written.
Simple Future
उत्तर देखें
Object + will/shall + be + V3 (+ by subject)
उत्तर देखें
A letter will be written.
Future Perfect
उत्तर देखें
Object + will/shall + have been + V3
उत्तर देखें
A letter will have been written.
Special Passive Constructions (Modals, Imperatives & Interrogatives)
Structure/Case Transformation Rule Example
Modal Auxiliaries
उत्तर देखें
Object + modal + be + V3
उत्तर देखें
The puzzle can be solved.
Modal Perfect
उत्तर देखें
Object + modal + have been + V3
उत्तर देखें
It should have been completed.
Imperative (Order)
उत्तर देखें
Let + object + be + V3
उत्तर देखें
Let the door be shut.
Imperative (Moral Advice)
उत्तर देखें
Object + should + be + V3
उत्तर देखें
The poor should be helped.
Imperative (Request)
उत्तर देखें
You are requested + to + V1
उत्तर देखें
You are requested to wait.
Imperative (Prohibition)
उत्तर देखें
You are forbidden + to + V1
उत्तर देखें
You are forbidden to enter.
Interrogative (Yes/No)
उत्तर देखें
Auxiliary + object + (being/been) + V3?
उत्तर देखें
Was the report submitted by you?
Interrogative (Who)
उत्तर देखें
By whom + auxiliary + object + V3?
उत्तर देखें
By whom was this book written?
Interrogative (Whom)
उत्तर देखें
Who + auxiliary + V3 (+ by subject)?
उत्तर देखें
Who was invited by you?
Two Objects (Ditransitive)
उत्तर देखें
Indirect personal object preferred as passive subject
उत्तर देखें
I was given a pen.
To-Infinitive Passive
उत्तर देखें
Subject + verb + to be + V3
उत्तर देखें
The project is to be submitted.
Reporting Clause Passive
उत्तर देखें
It is said/believed that + clause
उत्तर देखें
It is said that he is honest.

Voice Fundamentals, Transitivity & Object Identification

Definition of grammatical voice
उत्तर देखें
Verb form indicating agent or patient focus.
Definition of active voice
उत्तर देखें
Subject performs the verbal action.
Definition of passive voice
उत्तर देखें
Subject receives the verbal action.
Essential grammatical requirement for passive voice transformation
उत्तर देखें
Presence of a transitive verb.
Why intransitive verbs cannot form passive voice
उत्तर देखें
Lack direct object to become subject.
Examples of purely intransitive verbs
उत्तर देखें
Sleep, die, laugh, fall, arrive.
How to identify direct object in active sentence
उत्तर देखें
Ask 'what' or 'whom' after verb.
How to identify indirect object in active sentence
उत्तर देखें
Ask 'to whom' or 'for whom'.
Case taken by active subject in by-agent phrase
उत्तर देखें
Objective case (e.g. by him).
Case taken by active object becoming passive subject
उत्तर देखें
Subjective/nominative case (e.g. He).
Mandatory participle form across all passive constructions
उत्तर देखें
Past participle (V3).
Auxiliary verb family foundational to all passive structures
उत्तर देखें
Be-verb family (is, was, been, being).
When is the by-agent omitted in passive voice?
उत्तर देखें
Agent is vague, unknown, or obvious.
Examples of indefinite subjects typically dropped in passive
उत्तर देखें
Someone, somebody, people, they, one.
Active to passive: 'Someone stole my watch'
उत्तर देखें
My watch was stolen.
Active to passive: 'People speak English all over world'
उत्तर देखें
English is spoken worldwide.
Active to passive: 'Police arrested the thief'
उत्तर देखें
The thief was arrested.
Communicative purpose of using passive voice
उत्तर देखें
Focus on recipient or completed action.
What happens to fixed preposition of a phrasal verb in passive?
उत्तर देखें
Remains attached immediately to verb.
Active to passive: 'They laughed at the beggar'
उत्तर देखें
Beggar was laughed at by them.
Active to passive: 'She looks after the child'
उत्तर देखें
The child is looked after.
Can intransitive verb with preposition (prepositional verb) become passive?
उत्तर देखें
Yes, prepositional object becomes passive subject.
Primary rule regarding sentence tense during voice transformation
उत्तर देखें
Tense remains unchanged.
Primary difference between voice change and narration change
उत्तर देखें
Voice keeps tense; narration shifts tense.
Core test for identifying active versus passive construction
उत्तर देखें
Determine if grammatical subject acts or receives.

Tense-Wise Active to Passive Transformations

Passive formula for simple present tense
उत्तर देखें
Is/am/are + past participle (V3).
Passive formula for present continuous tense
उत्तर देखें
Is/am/are + being + V3.
Passive formula for present perfect tense
उत्तर देखें
Has/have + been + V3.
Passive formula for simple past tense
उत्तर देखें
Was/were + past participle (V3).
Passive formula for past continuous tense
उत्तर देखें
Was/were + being + V3.
Passive formula for past perfect tense
उत्तर देखें
Had + been + past participle (V3).
Passive formula for simple future tense
उत्तर देखें
Will/shall + be + V3.
Passive formula for future perfect tense
उत्तर देखें
Will/shall + have been + V3.
Total number of tenses lacking standard passive voice
उत्तर देखें
Four tenses.
The four tenses lacking standard passive voice
उत्तर देखें
Three perfect continuous tenses plus future continuous.
Why perfect continuous tenses avoid passive voice
उत्तर देखें
Creates clumsy double-be construction (been being).
Active to passive: 'She sings a melodious song'
उत्तर देखें
A melodious song is sung.
Active to passive: 'They are composing a new anthem'
उत्तर देखें
A new anthem is being composed.
Active to passive: 'He has painted the exterior wall'
उत्तर देखें
Exterior wall has been painted.
Active to passive: 'The hunter shot the ferocious tiger'
उत्तर देखें
The tiger was shot by hunter.
Active to passive: 'Workers were repairing the damaged bridge'
उत्तर देखें
Damaged bridge was being repaired.
Active to passive: 'She had finished the scientific thesis'
उत्तर देखें
Thesis had been finished.
Active to passive: 'The company will launch a new product'
उत्तर देखें
New product will be launched.
Active to passive: 'They will have concluded the investigation'
उत्तर देखें
Investigation will have been concluded.
Negative passive structure for simple present tense
उत्तर देखें
Is/am/are + not + V3.
Active to passive: 'He does not know the answer'
उत्तर देखें
Answer is not known to him.
Negative passive structure for simple past tense
उत्तर देखें
Was/were + not + V3.
Active to passive: 'She did not accept the offer'
उत्तर देखें
Offer was not accepted.
Role of subject-verb agreement after passive transformation
उत्तर देखें
Be-verb agrees with new grammatical subject.
Active to passive: 'He bought five books'
उत्तर देखें
Five books were bought by him.

Modals, Infinitives, Gerunds & Causative Verbs in Passive

Passive formula for modal auxiliary verbs
उत्तर देखें
Modal + be + past participle (V3).
Passive formula for modal perfect forms
उत्तर देखें
Modal + have been + V3.
Active to passive: 'We can conquer this challenge'
उत्तर देखें
This challenge can be conquered.
Active to passive: 'Citizens must obey traffic regulations'
उत्तर देखें
Traffic regulations must be obeyed.
Active to passive: 'You should have informed the authority'
उत्तर देखें
Authority should have been informed.
Active to passive: 'They might have completed the project'
उत्तर देखें
Project might have been completed.
Passive formula for to-infinitive (to + V1)
उत्तर देखें
To be + past participle (V3).
Active to passive: 'I have to finish this report'
उत्तर देखें
This report has to be finished.
Active to passive: 'She wants to invite you'
उत्तर देखें
You want to be invited.
Active to passive: 'It is time to take tea'
उत्तर देखें
It is time for tea to be taken.
Passive formula for 'It is time + to-infinitive'
उत्तर देखें
It is time for + object + to be V3.
Passive formula for gerund (V-ing)
उत्तर देखें
Being + past participle (V3).
Active to passive: 'She remembers her father taking her there'
उत्तर देखें
She remembers being taken there.
Active to passive: 'He dislikes people flattering him'
उत्तर देखें
He dislikes being flattered.
Transformation rule for causative verb 'make' in passive
उत्तर देखें
Takes to-infinitive in passive voice.
Active to passive: 'They made him sign the confession'
उत्तर देखें
He was made to sign confession.
Transformation rule for verbs of perception (hear, see) in passive
उत्तर देखें
Bare infinitive converts to to-infinitive.
Active to passive: 'I saw him enter the hall'
उत्तर देखें
He was seen to enter hall.
Active to passive: 'We heard her sing melodiously'
उत्तर देखें
She was heard to sing melodiously.
Transformation rule for verb 'let' expressing permission in passive
उत्तर देखें
Replaced by 'was/were allowed to'.
Active to passive: 'He let me leave the room'
उत्तर देखें
I was allowed to leave room.
Passive of bare infinitive after 'bid' / 'help'
उत्तर देखें
Requires to-infinitive in passive voice.
Active to passive: 'I helped him carry the luggage'
उत्तर देखें
He was helped to carry luggage.
Can causative 'have' and 'get' take passive participles in active sentences?
उत्तर देखें
Yes (e.g. I had my car washed).
Grammatical name for 'I had my haircut'
उत्तर देखें
Causative passive structure.

Imperative Sentences, Orders, Advice & Requests

Passive formula for imperative command or order with object
उत्तर देखें
Let + object + be + V3.
Active to passive: 'Shut the front door'
उत्तर देखें
Let the front door be shut.
Active to passive: 'Bring a cup of warm tea'
उत्तर देखें
Let a cup of tea be brought.
Passive formula for negative imperative command
उत्तर देखें
Let + object + not + be + V3.
Active to passive: 'Do not pluck the flowers'
उत्तर देखें
Let the flowers not be plucked.
Passive formula for moral advice or suggestion with object
उत्तर देखें
Object + should + be + V3.
Active to passive: 'Help the impoverished people'
उत्तर देखें
Impoverished people should be helped.
Active to passive: 'Respect your teachers and elders'
उत्तर देखें
Teachers and elders should be respected.
Active to passive: 'Love your motherland'
उत्तर देखें
Motherland should be loved.
Passive formula for polite requests starting with 'Please' or 'Kindly'
उत्तर देखें
You are requested + to + V1.
Active to passive: 'Please grant me leave'
उत्तर देखें
You are requested to grant leave.
Active to passive: 'Kindly forward this email'
उत्तर देखें
You are requested to forward email.
Passive formula for intransitive imperative advice (no object)
उत्तर देखें
You are advised + to + V1.
Active to passive: 'Work diligently for success'
उत्तर देखें
You are advised to work diligently.
Passive formula for intransitive imperative order (no object)
उत्तर देखें
You are ordered + to + V1.
Active to passive: 'Stand up on the bench'
उत्तर देखें
You are ordered to stand up.
Passive formula for negative prohibition without object
उत्तर देखें
You are forbidden + to + V1.
Active to passive: 'Do not smoke in the office'
उत्तर देखें
You are forbidden to smoke here.
Can negative word 'not' follow 'forbidden' in passive?
उत्तर देखें
No, forbidden inherently contains negation.
Passive formula for imperative beginning with 'Let' + pronoun
उत्तर देखें
Let + object + be + V3 + by.
Active to passive: 'Let him complete the assignment'
उत्तर देखें
Let assignment be completed by him.
Passive formula for 'Let us' expressing proposal or suggestion
उत्तर देखें
It is suggested that we should.
Active to passive: 'Let us celebrate the victory'
उत्तर देखें
It is suggested that we celebrate.
Alternative moral passive for: 'Do not insult the poor'
उत्तर देखें
The poor should not be insulted.
Criteria for choosing between 'Let' and 'Should' in imperative passive
उत्तर देखें
Commands use Let; moral advice uses Should.

Interrogative Sentences & Dual Object Constructions

Passive formula for Yes/No question in simple present
उत्तर देखें
Is/am/are + object + V3 (+ by)?
Active to passive: 'Do you play cricket?'
उत्तर देखें
Is cricket played by you?
Passive formula for Yes/No question in simple past
उत्तर देखें
Was/were + object + V3 (+ by)?
Active to passive: 'Did he complete the homework?'
उत्तर देखें
Was homework completed by him?
Passive formula for Yes/No question in present perfect
उत्तर देखें
Has/have + object + been + V3?
Active to passive: 'Have they delivered the package?'
उत्तर देखें
Has the package been delivered?
Passive formula for questions beginning with 'Who'
उत्तर देखें
By whom + auxiliary + object + V3?
Active to passive: 'Who broke this beautiful mirror?'
उत्तर देखें
By whom was mirror broken?
Alternative modern passive formula for 'Who'
उत्तर देखें
Who was mirror broken by?
Passive formula for questions beginning with 'Whom'
उत्तर देखें
Who + auxiliary + V3 (+ by)?
Active to passive: 'Whom did the committee select?'
उत्तर देखें
Who was selected by committee?
Passive formula for questions beginning with 'What'
उत्तर देखें
What + auxiliary + V3 (+ by)?
Active to passive: 'What did you discover?'
उत्तर देखें
What was discovered by you?
Passive formula for questions beginning with 'Which' + noun
उत्तर देखें
Which + noun + auxiliary + V3?
Active to passive: 'Which author wrote this novel?'
उत्तर देखें
By which author was novel written?
The two object types found in ditransitive clauses
उत्तर देखें
Direct object and indirect object.
Which object is traditionally preferred as passive subject?
उत्तर देखें
Indirect personal object.
Active to passive (preferred): 'He gifted me a watch'
उत्तर देखें
I was gifted a watch.
Active to passive (secondary): 'He gifted me a watch'
उत्तर देखें
A watch was gifted to me.
Preposition required when direct object becomes subject
उत्तर देखें
Preposition 'to' or 'for'.
Active to passive: 'She bought me an expensive dress'
उत्तर देखें
Dress was bought for me.
Active to passive: 'The teacher taught us grammar'
उत्तर देखें
We were taught grammar by teacher.
Punctuation closing an interrogative sentence in passive voice
उत्तर देखें
Question mark (?).
Word order retained in interrogative passive sentences
उत्तर देखें
Auxiliary verb precedes grammatical subject.
Why 'Who was the thief caught' is incorrect
उत्तर देखें
Omission of by-agent or wrong pronoun.

Quasi-Passive, Prepositions Other Than 'By' & Common Traps

Definition of quasi-passive verbs
उत्तर देखें
Active in form, passive in meaning.
Passive formula for quasi-passive perception verbs
उत्तर देखें
Subject + be + adjective + when V3.
Active to passive: 'Quinine tastes bitter'
उत्तर देखें
Quinine is bitter when it is tasted.
Active to passive: 'The rose smells sweet'
उत्तर देखें
Rose is sweet when it is smelt.
Active to passive: 'This velvet cloth feels smooth'
उत्तर देखें
Cloth is smooth when it is felt.
Passive formula for reporting verbs with that-clause
उत्तर देखें
It is said/believed that + clause.
Active to passive: 'People say that he is a genius'
उत्तर देखें
It is said that he is genius.
Alternative infinitive passive for: 'People say he is a genius'
उत्तर देखें
He is said to be a genius.
Active to passive: 'They believe that honesty pays'
उत्तर देखें
It is believed that honesty pays.
Fixed preposition following passive verb 'know'
उत्तर देखें
Preposition 'to' (never 'by').
Active to passive: 'I know Dr. Sharma thoroughly'
उत्तर देखें
Dr. Sharma is known to me.
Fixed preposition following passive verb 'marry'
उत्तर देखें
Preposition 'to' (never 'with').
Active to passive: 'Ram married Sita'
उत्तर देखें
Sita was married to Ram.
Fixed preposition following passive verb 'surprise'
उत्तर देखें
Preposition 'at'.
Active to passive: 'His unexpected conduct surprised me'
उत्तर देखें
I was surprised at his conduct.
Fixed preposition following passive verb 'please'
उत्तर देखें
Preposition 'with'.
Active to passive: 'Her dedication pleased the committee'
उत्तर देखें
Committee was pleased with her dedication.
Fixed preposition following passive verb 'contain'
उत्तर देखें
Preposition 'in'.
Active to passive: 'This glass bottle contains pure honey'
उत्तर देखें
Pure honey is contained in bottle.
Fixed preposition following passive verb 'fill'
उत्तर देखें
Preposition 'with'.
Active to passive: 'Dense smoke filled the auditorium'
उत्तर देखें
Auditorium was filled with smoke.
Why stative verb 'have' (possession) lacks passive form
उत्तर देखें
Expresses state of ownership, not action.
Why 'A luxurious mansion is had by him' is erroneous
उत्तर देखें
Possessive verb 'have' resists passive conversion.
Stative verbs resisting passive transformation
उत्तर देखें
Resemble, suit, lack, fit, hold.
Ultimate rule for solving voice questions in competitive exams
उत्तर देखें
Preserve tense, determine subject-verb agreement.
