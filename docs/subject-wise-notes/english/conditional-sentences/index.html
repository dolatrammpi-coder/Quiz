Fundamentals, Clauses & The Zero Conditional

Definition of a conditional sentence
Sentence expressing a condition and its outcome.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Two core components of a conditional sentence
Conditional clause and main clause.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Alternative name for the conditional clause
If-clause or protasis.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Alternative name for the main result clause
Apodosis or result clause.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Punctuation rule when if-clause precedes main clause
Comma separates the clauses.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Punctuation rule when main clause precedes if-clause
No comma is required.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Primary function of the zero conditional
Expresses universal truths and scientific facts.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Verb tense in the if-clause of zero conditional
Simple present tense.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Verb tense in the main clause of zero conditional
Simple present tense.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Can 'when' replace 'if' in zero conditional sentences?
Yes, without altering the meaning.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Scientific example of zero conditional
If you heat ice, it melts.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Habitual factual example of zero conditional
If I drink coffee, I stay awake.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Are modal auxiliaries (will/shall) used in zero conditional?
No, both clauses use present tense.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Distinction between real and unreal conditionals
Real are possible; unreal are imaginary.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Which conditionals fall under real conditions?
Zero and first conditionals.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Which conditionals fall under unreal conditions?
Second and third conditionals.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Can zero conditional govern imperative instructions?
Yes, if-clause plus imperative verb.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Example of zero conditional with imperative outcome
If you have questions, ask now.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Use of simple present for general rules in conditionals
Describes fixed consequences of recurring actions.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Nature of certainty in zero conditional statements
Absolute certainty or guaranteed result.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Can zero conditional apply to geographical facts?
Yes, expressing constant geographical truths.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Example of geographical zero conditional
If rivers freeze, navigation stops.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Relationship between cause and effect in zero conditional
Direct, unchanging, and automatic linkage.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Is the condition in zero conditional time-bound?
No, it holds true anytime.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
What happens if 'will' is introduced into zero conditional?
It converts into a first conditional.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें

The First Conditional: Real and Probable Conditions

Primary function of the first conditional
Expresses realistic, probable future outcomes.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Verb tense in the if-clause of first conditional
Simple present tense.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Verb structure in main clause of first conditional
Will, shall, can, may + V1.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Exam rule regarding 'will' inside the if-clause
Never use will in conditional clause.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Standard example of first conditional
If you work hard, you will succeed.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Can present continuous appear in if-clause of first conditional?
Yes, expressing actions currently underway.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Example of present continuous in if-clause
If you are waiting, please sit down.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Can present perfect appear in if-clause of first conditional?
Yes, expressing a completed prerequisite action.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Example of present perfect in if-clause
If you have finished, you may leave.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Modal verb used in main clause to denote possibility
May or might.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Modal verb used in main clause to denote capability
Can.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Modal verb used in main clause to denote advice
Should or ought to.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Can an imperative clause function as the main clause?
Yes, giving direct commands or requests.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Example of first conditional with imperative main clause
If it rains, take an umbrella.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Why 'If it will rain, we will cancel' is incorrect
Double will violates conditional grammar rules.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Corrected form of 'If it will rain, we will cancel'
If it rains, we will cancel.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Rare exception allowing 'will' in if-clause
Expressing polite willingness or obstinate persistence.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Example of 'will' expressing willingness in if-clause
If you will wait, I will call him.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Time reference of the first conditional
Refers to present condition and future result.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Level of likelihood in first conditional scenarios
High probability of actual occurrence.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Can 'going to' replace 'will' in first conditional?
Yes, expressing predetermined future plans.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Function of 'should' in if-clause of first conditional
Indicates lower probability or chance occurrence.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Example with 'should' in first conditional if-clause
If you should see him, inform me.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Subject-verb agreement rule in first conditional if-clause
Singular subjects take third-person singular verbs.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Common error in subject-verb agreement in if-clause
Omitting -s/-es on third-person verbs.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें

The Second Conditional & Subjunctive 'Were'

Primary function of the second conditional
Expresses hypothetical, unreal, or unlikely present/future situations.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Verb tense in the if-clause of second conditional
Simple past tense (or subjunctive were).
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Verb structure in main clause of second conditional
Would, could, or might + V1.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Subjunctive verb rule for 'to be' across all persons
Always use were, never was.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Why 'If I was a bird' is considered erroneous in exams
Hypothetical condition requires subjunctive 'were'.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Corrected standard form for hypothetical bird sentence
If I were a bird, I would fly.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Standard formula for offering advice using second conditional
If I were you, I would...
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Example of giving advice with second conditional
If I were you, I would apologize.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Can 'would' be used inside the second conditional if-clause?
No, never use would in if-clause.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Difference between 'would', 'could', and 'might' in main clause
Would is definite; could/might denote capability/possibility.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Time reference of the second conditional
Present or future imaginary scenarios.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Can second conditional express purely impossible physical conditions?
Yes, purely counterfactual or imaginary states.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Example of counterfactual physical condition
If dogs had wings, they would fly.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Is simple past in second conditional a past time marker?
No, it signals hypothetical psychological distance.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Structure using 'were to + V1' in second conditional
If + subject + were to + V1.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Semantic effect of 'were to' in if-clause
Makes hypothetical scenario even more improbable.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Example sentence featuring 'were to'
If he were to resign, chaos would follow.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Can past continuous be used in second conditional if-clause?
Yes, denoting hypothetical ongoing activity.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Example of past continuous in second conditional
If it were raining, we would cancel.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Negative contraction form commonly tested in second conditional
Were not (weren't).
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Common error involving 'would have' in second conditional
Confusing second conditional with third conditional.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Contrast between first and second conditional meaning
First is probable; second is improbable.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Example comparing first versus second conditional probability
If I win (probable) vs If I won (unlikely).
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Why 'If he would come, I would go' is wrong
Redundant 'would' in conditional clause.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Corrected form of 'If he would come, I would go'
If he came, I would go.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें

The Third Conditional & Mixed Conditionals

Primary function of the third conditional
Expresses counterfactual, unalterable past situations and regrets.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Verb tense in the if-clause of third conditional
Past perfect tense (had + V3).
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Verb structure in main clause of third conditional
Would have, could have, might have + V3.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Can 'would have' appear in the if-clause of third conditional?
Never, always use had + V3.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Standard textbook example of third conditional
If he had studied, he would have passed.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Time reference of the third conditional
Completely in the unchangeable past.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Psychological mood frequently expressed by third conditional
Regret, relief, or historical speculation.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Function of 'could have + V3' in main clause
Indicates unfulfilled past ability or opportunity.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Function of 'might have + V3' in main clause
Indicates unfulfilled past possibility.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Definition of a mixed conditional sentence
Combines clauses from different conditional types.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Most common mixed conditional pattern in exams
Past condition producing present consequence.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Structure for past action affecting present state
If + had + V3, would + V1.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Example of past condition with present result
If I had slept earlier, I would feel fresh now.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Structure for permanent trait affecting past outcome
If + simple past, would have + V3.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Example of permanent trait affecting past outcome
If she were brave, she would have spoken.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Identification clue for mixed conditionals
Presence of time adverbs like 'now' or 'yesterday'.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Why 'If he had had patience' is grammatically correct
Auxiliary 'had' plus main verb V3 'had'.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Can passive voice appear in third conditional?
Yes, had been + V3.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Example of third conditional in passive voice
If warned, he would have been saved.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Common error regarding 'had' in third conditional
Omitting auxiliary 'had' before main verb.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Why 'If he would have worked hard, he had passed' is incorrect
Swaps had + V3 and would have.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Corrected version of the hard work sentence
If he had worked hard, he would have passed.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Contracted form of 'I had' and 'I would'
Both contract to I'd.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
How to differentiate 'I'd' in third conditional if-clause
Followed by V3 indicates past perfect.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
How to differentiate 'I'd' in main clause
Followed by 'have + V3' indicates would have.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें

Conditional Connectors: Unless, Until, In Case & Others

Definition and fundamental meaning of 'unless'
Means 'if not' or 'except if'.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Strict grammatical rule regarding 'not' inside an unless-clause
Never use not in unless-clause.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Why 'Unless you do not run' is a serious exam error
Creates an ungrammatical double negative.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Corrected form of 'Unless you do not run'
Unless you run, you will miss it.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Can 'will' or 'shall' appear inside an unless-clause?
No, simple present tense is required.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Difference between 'unless' and 'until'
Unless denotes condition; until denotes time.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Example demonstrating proper use of 'until'
Wait here until I return.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Can 'not' be used inside an until-clause?
No, until inherently expresses negation.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Definition and function of 'provided that'
Introduces an indispensable, strict condition.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Tense governed by 'provided that' referring to future
Simple present tense.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Example sentence using 'provided that'
You may enter provided that you have a pass.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Is 'providing that' interchangeable with 'provided that'?
Yes, both function as conditional connectors.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Definition and function of 'as long as'
Expresses continuing condition or duration.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Example sentence using 'as long as'
You are safe as long as you stay here.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Critical difference between 'if' and 'in case'
If is reactive; in case is precautionary.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Why 'I will buy food in case you will come' is incorrect
In case never takes future modal will.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Corrected sentence with 'in case'
I will buy food in case you come.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Function of 'supposing' as a conditional marker
Introduces a speculative, imaginative condition.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Why 'Supposing if' used together is grammatically incorrect
Redundant pairing of two identical connectors.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Meaning and function of prepositional phrase 'but for'
Means 'were it not for' or 'without'.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Grammatical structure immediately following 'but for'
Followed by noun or noun phrase.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Third conditional equivalent of 'But for his help, I would have drowned'
If he had not helped me.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Meaning of 'on condition that'
Strict contractual or procedural requirement.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Can 'even if' express an emphatic condition?
Yes, outcome unchanged despite extreme condition.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Example sentence featuring 'even if'
Even if it rains, the match continues.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें

Conditional Inversion, Transformations & Common Traps

Definition of inversion in conditional sentences
Omitting 'if' and placing auxiliary before subject.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Inversion formula for first conditional using 'should'
Should + subject + base verb (V1).
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Inverted form of 'If anyone calls, take a message'
Should anyone call, take a message.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Inversion formula for second conditional with 'were'
Were + subject + complement / to-V1.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Inverted form of 'If I were the prime minister'
Were I the prime minister.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Inverted form of 'If he knew the truth'
Were he to know the truth.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Inversion formula for third conditional with 'had'
Had + subject + past participle (V3).
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Inverted form of 'If you had informed me earlier'
Had you informed me earlier.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Position of 'not' in negative inverted conditionals
Placed directly after the subject.
