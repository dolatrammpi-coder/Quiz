Common Sentence Correction Rules and Patterns
16. Sentence Correction
Error PatternRuleCorrect Form
The quality of mangoes were poor
उत्तर देखें
Subject 'quality' is singular; ignores prepositional modifier
उत्तर देखें
The quality of mangoes was poor.
He as well as they are guilty
उत्तर देखें
Connector 'as well as' governs verb with first subject
उत्तर देखें
He as well as they is guilty.
Neither he nor his friends was present
उत्तर देखें
Nearest subject determines verb number in either/neither pairs
उत्तर देखें
Neither he nor his friends were present.
If he will come, I will help
उत्तर देखें
Conditional if-clauses cannot take modal auxiliary will
उत्तर देखें
If he comes, I will help.
Had I known, I would inform you
उत्तर देखें
Past unreal third conditional requires would have + V3
उत्तर देखें
Had I known, I would have informed.
She is married with a doctor
उत्तर देखें
Passive voice of marry governs preposition to
उत्तर देखें
She is married to a doctor.
He is senior than me
उत्तर देखें
Latin comparative adjectives govern preposition to, never than
उत्तर देखें
He is senior to me.

उत्तर देखें
Correlative connector scarcely strictly pairs with when
उत्तर देखें
Scarcely had he left when it rained.

उत्तर देखें
Correlative connector no sooner strictly pairs with than
उत्तर देखें
No sooner did he arrive than bells rang.

उत्तर देखें
Advice is uncountable mass noun; cannot take plural -s
उत्तर देखें
He gave me pieces of advice.
A five-hundred-rupees note was found
उत्तर देखें
Hyphenated compound modifying adjective must remain singular
उत्तर देखें
A five-hundred-rupee note was found.
Being a cold day, I stayed home
उत्तर देखें
Dangling participle requires explicit dummy subject 'It'
उत्तर देखें
It being a cold day, I stayed.

Subject-Verb Agreement & Noun-Pronoun Harmony

Subject-verb agreement rule for intervening prepositional phrases
Verb agrees with primary subject noun.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Subject-verb agreement rule for subjects joined by 'as well as'
Verb agrees with the first subject.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Subject-verb agreement rule for 'together with' and 'along with'
Verb agrees with the first subject.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Subject-verb agreement rule for 'either...or' and 'neither...nor'
Verb agrees with the nearest subject.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Subject-verb agreement rule for 'not only...but also'
Verb agrees with the nearest subject.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Verb form governed by distributive pronouns (each, every, neither)
Always takes a singular verb.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Verb form governed by 'one of the + plural noun'
Singular verb.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Verb form when relative pronoun follows 'one of the + plural noun'
Plural verb.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Verb form when 'the only one of the + plural noun' precedes relative pronoun
Singular verb.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Verb form governed by 'a number of + plural noun'
Plural verb.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Verb form governed by 'the number of + plural noun'
Singular verb.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Verb form for two nouns expressing a single unified concept
Singular verb.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Example of unified pair taking singular verb
Bread and butter is wholesome.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Verb form for collective noun acting in complete unison
Singular verb.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Verb form for collective noun with divided individual members
Plural verb.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Verb form for academic subjects ending in -s (physics, mathematics)
Singular verb.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Verb form for academic subjects modified by possessive adjectives
Plural verb.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Plural nouns denoting singular measurement, time, or money unit
Take a singular verb.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Correction for: 'Ten miles are a long distance to walk'
Ten miles is a long distance.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Correction for: 'Neither of the boys were present'
Neither of the boys was present.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Correction for: 'Each of the participants were given medals'
Each of the participants was given.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Correction for: 'The police has arrested the criminal'
The police have arrested the criminal.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Correction for: 'The cattle is grazing in the valley'
The cattle are grazing in valley.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Correction for: 'One must do his duty honestly'
One must do one's duty honestly.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Order of personal pronouns for pleasant or polite events
Second, third, first person (231).
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें

Tense Consistency, Time Sequence & Conditional Clauses

Sequence of tenses rule when principal clause is in past tense
Subordinate clause must be in past.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Exception to sequence of tenses rule for past principal clause
Universal truths retain simple present tense.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Correction for: 'He said that the earth moved round the sun'
Said that the earth moves.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Tense required when action is accompanied by past time adverbial (yesterday, ago)
Simple past tense.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Correction for: 'I have seen him yesterday evening'
I saw him yesterday evening.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Tense structure following conjunction 'since' denoting point of time
Takes simple past clause.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Tense structure in principal clause preceding 'since'
Requires present perfect tense.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Correction for: 'Two years passed since he has left'
Two years have passed since he left.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Rule for earlier of two completed past actions
Takes past perfect tense (had + V3).
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Correction for: 'The train departed before I reached station'
The train had departed before I reached.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Can modal auxiliary 'will' appear inside a conditional if-clause?
Never in standard conditional statements.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Correction for: 'If it will rain, the match will stop'
If it rains, the match will stop.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Verb form in if-clause of hypothetical second conditional
Simple past subjunctive 'were'.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Correction for: 'If I was a bird, I would fly'
If I were a bird, I would fly.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Main clause structure in past unreal third conditional
Would have + past participle (V3).
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Correction for: 'If he had worked hard, he would pass'
Would have passed.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Correction for: 'Had he invited me, I would attend'
Would have attended.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Tense structure governed by 'It is high time'
Takes simple past tense verb.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Correction for: 'It is high time we start studying'
It is high time we started studying.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Can stative verbs (know, believe, understand) take continuous form?
No, avoid progressive continuous tenses.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Correction for: 'I am knowing the answer thoroughly'
I know the answer thoroughly.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Tense used for habitual actions occurring continuously
Simple present tense.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Correction for: 'He is going for morning walk daily'
He goes for a morning walk daily.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Tense used with present perfect progressive marker 'for five years'
Has been / have been living.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Correction for: 'I am living in Delhi for five years'
Have been living in Delhi.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें

Articles, Prepositions & Conjunctions Correction

Article required before singular countable noun beginning with vowel sound
Indefinite article 'an'.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Correction for: 'He is a honest civil servant'
He is an honest civil servant.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Correction for: 'She is an European citizen'
She is a European citizen.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Article required before superlative degree adjectives
Definite article 'the'.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Article required before parallel comparative clauses
The (e.g. the higher, the cooler).
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Article omission rule before abstract and material nouns in general sense
Zero article.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Correction for: 'The gold is a precious metal'
Gold is a precious metal.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Article required when material noun is particularized with 'of'
Takes 'the' (the gold of Kolar).
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Correction for: 'He went to the hospital to undergo surgery'
Went to hospital for primary purpose.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Fixed preposition governed by 'abstain' and 'refrain'
From.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Correction for: 'He abstained to vote in elections'
Abstained from voting in elections.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Fixed preposition governed by 'accused' and 'acquitted'
Of.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Correction for: 'He was accused for committing murder'
Accused of committing murder.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Correction for: 'He could not cope up with the workload'
Cope with the workload.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Latin comparative adjectives requiring preposition 'to'
Senior, junior, superior, inferior, prior.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Correction for: 'This model is superior than that one'
Superior to that one.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Correlative conjunction partner required for 'scarcely' and 'hardly'
When.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Correction for: 'Hardly had he arrived than it started raining'
Hardly had he arrived when it started.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Correlative conjunction partner required for 'no sooner'
Than.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Correction for: 'No sooner did he leave when the bell rang'
No sooner did he leave than.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Modal auxiliary strictly governed by negative conjunction 'lest'
Should.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Can negative word 'not' follow 'lest' in a clause?
Never, lest inherently expresses negation.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Correction for: 'Walk carefully lest you should not stumble'
Walk carefully lest you should stumble.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Correction for: 'Between 10 AM to 5 PM'
Between 10 AM and 5 PM.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Correction for: 'From 10 AM and 5 PM'
From 10 AM to 5 PM.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें

Modifiers, Parallelism & Dangling Participles

Definition of a dangling modifier in sentence structure
Participial phrase lacking clear logical subject.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Correction for: 'Walking in the garden, a snake bit him'
While he was walking, a snake bit him.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Correction for: 'Being a stormy night, we remained indoors'
It being a stormy night.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Rule of grammatical parallelism across coordinating conjunctions
Connected items must share identical grammatical form.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Correction for: 'She likes swimming, dancing, and to sing'
Swimming, dancing, and singing.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Placement rule for correlative pair 'not only...but also'
Must precede identical parts of speech.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Correction for: 'He not only lost his coat but also his wallet'
Lost not only his coat but also.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Placement rule for limiting adverb 'only'
Place immediately before the word it modifies.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Meaning change in: 'Only he died' vs 'He only died'
Alters who died versus what happened.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Correction for: 'The climate of Shimla is cooler than Delhi'
Cooler than that of Delhi.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Correction for: 'The streets of Mumbai are wider than Pune'
Wider than those of Pune.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Rule for comparative degree when comparing within the same group
Use 'any other' to exclude the subject.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Correction for: 'Iron is more useful than any metal'
More useful than any other metal.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Can double comparative forms (more taller) be used together?
No, double comparatives are strictly ungrammatical.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Correction for: 'He is more cleverer than his brother'
He is cleverer than his brother.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Can double superlative forms (most greatest) be used together?
No, double superlatives are strictly ungrammatical.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Correction for: 'She is the most best student in class'
She is the best student.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Adjective versus adverb rule with action verbs
Action verbs are modified by adverbs.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Correction for: 'He ran very quick to catch the bus'
He ran very quickly.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Adjective versus adverb rule with verbs of sensation (smell, taste, feel)
Modified by adjectives, not adverbs.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Correction for: 'The food tastes deliciously'
The food tastes delicious.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Correction for: 'The rose smells sweetly'
The rose smells sweet.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Inversion rule when sentence begins with negative adverb
Auxiliary verb precedes grammatical subject.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Correction for: 'Seldom I have seen such magnificent art'
Seldom have I seen such magnificent art.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Correction for: 'Never he had experienced such warmth'
Never had he experienced such warmth.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें

Non-Finite Verbs: Infinitives, Gerunds & Participles

Definition of a bare infinitive
Base verb form without preceding 'to'.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Verb form governed by causative verb 'make' in active voice
Bare infinitive.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Correction for: 'The officer made him to sign the document'
Made him sign the document.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Verb form governed by causative verb 'make' in passive voice
To-infinitive.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Correction for: 'He was made sign the confession'
He was made to sign the confession.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Verb form governed by causative verb 'let'
Bare infinitive in active and passive.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Correction for: 'Let him to enter the examination hall'
Let him enter the examination hall.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Verb form governed by idioms 'had better' and 'would rather'
Bare base infinitive (V1).
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Correction for: 'You had better to leave immediately'
You had better leave immediately.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Case of noun or pronoun modifying a gerund
Requires possessive case.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Correction for: 'I disliked him coming late to class'
I disliked his coming late.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Correction for: 'She was pleased at me winning the contest'
Pleased at my winning the contest.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Verb form governed by 'look forward to'
Gerund (V-ing).
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Correction for: 'We look forward to meet our new mentor'
Look forward to meeting our new mentor.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Verb form governed by 'used to' expressing past habit
Base verb form (V1).
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Verb form governed by 'be used to' meaning accustomed
Gerund (V-ing).
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Correction for: 'He is used to wake up at dawn'
Used to waking up at dawn.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Verb form governed by 'with a view to'
Gerund (V-ing).
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Correction for: 'He studied hard with a view to pass'
With a view to passing.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Verb form governed by 'would you mind'
Gerund (V-ing).
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Correction for: 'Would you mind to close the window?'
Would you mind closing the window?
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
