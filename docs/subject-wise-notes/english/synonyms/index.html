Definitions, Concepts & Principles of Synonymy

Definition of a synonym in linguistic science
उत्तर देखें
Word having identical or similar meaning.
Etymological origin of the term synonym
उत्तर देखें
Greek syn (together) and onyma (name).
Definition of an absolute (exact) synonym
उत्तर देखें
Words mutually interchangeable in all contexts.
Are absolute synonyms common in English?
उत्तर देखें
Extremely rare in natural languages.
Definition of near-synonyms (plesionyms)
उत्तर देखें
Words sharing core meaning with nuances.
What factors create differences among near-synonyms?
उत्तर देखें
Connotation, register, dialect, and collocation.
Definition of contextual synonym
उत्तर देखें
Synonym applicable only in specific usage.
Example of contextual synonymy for 'firm'
उत्तर देखें
Solid (physical) versus company (business).
Role of register in choosing correct synonyms
उत्तर देखें
Distinguishes formal, informal, and technical diction.
Formal synonym for the verb 'buy'
उत्तर देखें
Purchase.
Formal synonym for the noun 'house'
उत्तर देखें
Residence or domicile.
Definition of positive connotation in synonyms
उत्तर देखें
Favorable emotional association of a word.
Definition of negative connotation in synonyms
उत्तर देखें
Unfavorable or critical emotional association.
Positive synonym for spending little money
उत्तर देखें
Thrifty or frugal.
Negative synonym for spending little money
उत्तर देखें
Miserly or stingy.
Role of collocations in synonym selection
उत्तर देखें
Dictates natural habit of word pairings.
Why 'powerful car' works while 'strong car' fails
उत्तर देखें
Collocational restriction governs natural usage.
Part of speech requirement for true synonyms
उत्तर देखें
Must share identical grammatical part.
Synonym of an adjective must always be
उत्तर देखें
An adjective.
Synonym of an abstract noun must be
उत्तर देखें
An abstract noun.
Concept of polysemy affecting synonymy
उत्तर देखें
Multiple word senses demand different synonyms.
Primary pitfall when answering synonym questions
उत्तर देखें
Selecting an antonym by misreading.
Best technique for discovering context-based synonyms
उत्तर देखें
Substitute candidate words into sentence.
Role of etymological root analysis in vocabulary tests
उत्तर देखें
Decodes unfamiliar vocabulary through prefixes/roots.
Ultimate goal of studying synonyms for competitive exams
उत्तर देखें
Master precise shades of meaning.
