Basic Principles, Number Harmony & Compound Subjects

Definition of subject-verb agreement (concord)
Harmony in number and person between them.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Verb ending for third-person singular present tense
Takes suffix -s or -es.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Does a plural noun take suffix -s while plural verb does not?
Yes, opposite inflection patterns.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
General rule for two subjects joined by 'and'
Governs a plural verb.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Example of compound subject with 'and'
Gold and silver are precious metals.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Verb rule when two nouns joined by 'and' express single idea
Governs a singular verb.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Verb in: 'Bread and butter ___ wholesome food'
Is (single unified dish).
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Verb in: 'Slow and steady ___ the race'
Wins (unified proverb idea).
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Verb in: 'Rice and curry ___ his favorite meal'
Is (unified meal concept).
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Verb rule when two titles joined by 'and' refer to one person
Governs a singular verb.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Identification clue for two titles referring to one person
Article used before first title only.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Verb in: 'The director and producer ___ arrived'
Has arrived (one single person).
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Verb rule when two titles joined by 'and' refer to two persons
Governs a plural verb.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Identification clue for two distinct persons holding titles
Article repeated before both titles.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Verb in: 'The director and the producer ___ arrived'
Have arrived (two distinct persons).
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Verb rule when two singular nouns joined by 'and' are preceded by 'every'
Governs a singular verb.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Verb in: 'Every boy and girl ___ present'
Was present.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Verb rule when two singular nouns joined by 'and' are preceded by 'each'
Governs a singular verb.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Verb in: 'Each officer and clerk ___ signed'
Has signed.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Does grammatical person affect auxiliary verb form?
Yes, determines am/is/are and have/has.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Subject-verb agreement rule for dummy subject 'It'
Always takes a singular verb.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Verb in: 'It ___ the children who made noise'
Was (governed by It).
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Primary rule to isolate true grammatical subject
Ignore non-essential descriptive modifier phrases.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Verb in: 'The quality of these mangoes ___ not good'
Is (agrees with quality).
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Why 'The quality of these mangoes are' is erroneous
Mistakes prepositional object mangoes for subject.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें

Disjunctive Connectors, Proximity & Parenthetical Phrases

Subject-verb agreement rule for subjects joined by 'as well as'
Verb agrees with the first subject.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Verb in: 'The teacher as well as students ___ present'
Was present.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Verb in: 'The students as well as teacher ___ present'
Were present.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Connectors following first-subject agreement rule
Along with, together with, with, accompanied by.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Additional connectors following first-subject agreement rule
In addition to, like, unlike, besides.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Verb in: 'The captain along with players ___ training'
Is training.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Verb in: 'He, rather than his associates, ___ culpable'
Is culpable.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Subject-verb agreement rule for 'either...or'
Verb agrees with the nearest subject.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Subject-verb agreement rule for 'neither...nor'
Verb agrees with the nearest subject.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Subject-verb agreement rule for 'not only...but also'
Verb agrees with the nearest subject.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Verb in: 'Either Ram or his brothers ___ done this'
Have done this.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Verb in: 'Neither the players nor the coach ___ happy'
Was happy.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Verb in: 'Not only he but also his friends ___ coming'
Are coming.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Subject-verb agreement rule for subjects joined simply by 'or'
Verb agrees with the nearest subject.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Verb in: 'Is Ram or his friends attending the seminar?'
Is (agrees with nearest subject Ram).
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Verb in: 'Are his friends or Ram attending the seminar?'
Are (agrees with nearest friends).
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Pronoun agreement rule when subjects differ in number or gender
Pronoun matches the nearest subject.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Pronoun in: 'Neither the manager nor subordinates did ___ duty'
Their duty.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Pronoun in: 'Neither subordinates nor the manager did ___ duty'
His duty.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Rule of proximity definition in syntax
Verb agrees with adjacent nominal element.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Connectors governed strictly by rule of proximity
Or, nor, either...or, neither...nor, but also.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Can parenthetical commas alter subject-verb agreement?
No, parenthetical elements are disregarded.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Verb in: 'The king, with his councilors, ___ executed'
Was executed.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Verb in: 'My dog, unlike cats, ___ swimming'
Loves swimming.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Why intervening prepositional phrases create exam errors
They disguise the true sentence subject.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें

Indefinite Pronouns, Distributives & Quantifiers

Verb agreement governed by distributive pronoun 'each'
Always takes a singular verb.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Verb in: 'Each of the participants ___ a certificate'

Governs a plural verb.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Verb in: 'The students each ___ submitted their papers'

उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें

Traditionally singular, plural with countables.

उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें

Always take plural verbs.
