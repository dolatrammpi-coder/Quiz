Fundamental Spelling Rules: Vowels, Stems & Consonants

The classic 'i before e' spelling rule
उत्तर देखें
I before e except after c.
Example of 'i before e' rule
उत्तर देखें
Believe, achieve, and thief.
Example of 'ei' following the letter 'c'
उत्तर देखें
Receive, ceiling, and deceit.
Exception to 'i before e' rule sounding like 'ay'
उत्तर देखें
Neighbor and weigh.
Notable exception to 'i before e' rule
उत्तर देखें
Weird, seize, and leisure.
Rule for doubling final consonant of a monosyllabic word
उत्तर देखें
Doubled before vowel suffix (run -> running).
Rule for doubling consonant in two-syllable words
उत्तर देखें
Doubled if second syllable is stressed.
Example of doubling consonant due to stress
उत्तर देखें
Occur becomes occurred.
Example of not doubling consonant due to unstressed syllable
उत्तर देखें
Benefit becomes benefited.
British English rule for final 'l' before vowel suffixes
उत्तर देखें
Always doubled (travel -> travelled).
American English rule for final 'l' in travel
उत्तर देखें
Single 'l' (traveled).
Rule for dropping final silent 'e' before vowel suffixes
उत्तर देखें
Silent 'e' is dropped.
Example of dropping silent 'e'
उत्तर देखें
Hope becomes hoping.
Rule for retaining silent 'e' before consonant suffixes
उत्तर देखें
Silent 'e' is retained.
Example of retaining silent 'e'
उत्तर देखें
Hope becomes hopeful.
Exception where final silent 'e' is retained after 'c' or 'g'
उत्तर देखें
Retained before 'a' or 'o'.
Example of retaining 'e' to preserve soft 'c' sound
उत्तर देखें
Noticeable.
Example of retaining 'e' to preserve soft 'g' sound
उत्तर देखें
Courageous.
Exception where silent 'e' is dropped before consonant suffix
उत्तर देखें
Truly, argument, and awful.
Rule for words ending in consonant + 'y' before suffixes
उत्तर देखें
'Y' changes to 'i'.
Example of 'y' changing to 'i'
उत्तर देखें
Beauty becomes beautiful.
Exception to 'y' changing to 'i' when suffix begins with 'i'
उत्तर देखें
'Y' is kept (hurry -> hurrying).
Rule for words ending in vowel + 'y'
उत्तर देखें
'Y' is retained (play -> played).
Exception to vowel + 'y' rule
उत्तर देखें
Day becomes daily.
Rule for words ending in 'ie' before suffix '-ing'
उत्तर देखें
'Ie' changes to 'y' (die -> dying).
