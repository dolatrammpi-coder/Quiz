Commonly Tested Grammar-Based Blank Patterns
Blank Category Rule/Clue Example
Causative Verb Blank
उत्तर देखें
Make takes bare infinitive in active voice
उत्तर देखें
He made me laugh
Subjunctive Were Blank
उत्तर देखें
Hypothetical unreal wish uses were across all persons
उत्तर देखें
I wish I were rich
Inverted Third Conditional
उत्तर देखें
Had opening conditional takes would have + V3 in main
उत्तर देखें
Had he known, he would have come
Infinitive vs Gerund Blank
उत्तर देखें
Prepositional phrases like look forward to take V-ing
उत्तर देखें
Look forward to meeting you
Adjective vs Adverb Blank
उत्तर देखें
Verbs of sensation take predicate adjectives
उत्तर देखें
The food smells delicious
Parenthetical Subject Blank
उत्तर देखें
As well as links back to first subject
उत्तर देखें
He as well as they was present
Negative Conjunction Blank
उत्तर देखें
Lest governs should without negative word
उत्तर देखें
Run lest you should fail
Double Comparative Prevention
उत्तर देखें
Avoid combining more with comparative suffix -er
उत्तर देखें
Much better, not more better
Stative Verb Restriction
उत्तर देखें
Stative verbs avoid progressive continuous forms
उत्तर देखें
She understands the concept
One of the Plural Blank
उत्तर देखें
One of the takes plural noun and singular verb
उत्तर देखें
One of my friends is coming

Concept, Core Strategy & Elimination Techniques

Primary objective of fill-in-the-blank questions
उत्तर देखें
Evaluate grammar, vocabulary, and contextual logic.
First step before choosing an answer for a blank
उत्तर देखें
Read the entire sentence completely.
Role of identifying the sentence tone
उत्तर देखें
Determines positive or negative polarity.
Elimination technique rule for parts of speech
उत्तर देखें
Discard words matching wrong syntactic slot.
How to handle blanks with multiple similar synonyms
उत्तर देखें
Examine governing prepositions and collocations.
Significance of the word immediately following the blank
उत्तर देखें
Reveals required preposition or noun form.
Significance of the word immediately preceding the blank
उत्तर देखें
Reveals required part of speech.
Role of transition words like however or although
उत्तर देखें
Signals contrasting meaning in blank.
Role of transition words like therefore or thus
उत्तर देखें
Signals cause-effect consequence in blank.
Role of transition words like furthermore or moreover
उत्तर देखें
Signals additive supportive information.
Strategy for double-blank questions in exams
उत्तर देखें
Test easier blank first to eliminate options.
Risk of selecting an option based purely on sound
उत्तर देखें
Colloquial speech often hides grammatical errors.
Why verifying subject-verb agreement is essential
उत्तर देखें
Prevents selecting mismatched singular/plural verbs.
Final step after selecting the option
उत्तर देखें
Reread sentence to verify natural flow.
Definition of contextual clue in fill-in-the-blanks
उत्तर देखें
Surrounding words hinting at missing idea.
Role of root words and affixes in vocabulary blanks
उत्तर देखें
Deduces meanings of unfamiliar options.
Handling options with differing grammatical inflections
उत्तर देखें
Identify target tense or participle form.
Significance of punctuation marks around a blank
उत्तर देखें
Marks clause boundaries and parenthetical phrases.
Trap of attractive but out-of-context vocabulary
उत्तर देखें
Impressive words may clash with sentence meaning.
Ultimate goal when solving fill-in-the-blank questions
उत्तर देखें
Achieve grammatically sound and logically coherent sentence.

Grammar-Based Blanks: Tenses, Modals & Agreement

Verb form required in blank after has, have, had
उत्तर देखें
Past participle (V3).
Verb form required in blank after modal auxiliaries
उत्तर देखें
Base form of verb (V1).
Verb required in blank: Each of the candidates ___ registered
उत्तर देखें
Has (singular verb).
Verb required in blank: Neither of the two plans ___ viable
उत्तर देखें
Is or was (singular verb).
Verb required in blank: Bread and butter ___ wholesome food
उत्तर देखें
Is (singular unified concept).
Verb required in blank: Time and tide ___ for none
उत्तर देखें
Wait (traditional plural usage).
Verb required in blank: The leader as well as his aides ___ arrived
उत्तर देखें
Has arrived (agrees with leader).
Verb required in blank: Either he or his friends ___ mistaken
उत्तर देखें
Are or were (nearest subject).
Verb required in blank: A number of students ___ passed
उत्तर देखें
Have (takes plural verb).
Verb required in blank: The number of applicants ___ fifty
उत्तर देखें
Is or was (takes singular verb).
Verb tense in blank: He ___ here since 2018
उत्तर देखें
Has lived or has been living.
Verb tense in blank: I ___ the letter yesterday
उत्तर देखें
Posted or sent (simple past).
Verb form in blank: The train ___ before we reached the station
उत्तर देखें
Had departed (past perfect).
Verb form in blank: If he ___ hard, he will succeed
उत्तर देखें
Works (simple present in if-clause).
Verb form in blank: If I ___ a king, I would help everyone
उत्तर देखें
Were (subjunctive were).
Verb form in blank: Had he informed me, I ___ him
उत्तर देखें
Would have helped.
Verb form in blank: The teacher made the student ___ the exercise
उत्तर देखें
Repeat (bare infinitive after make).
Verb form in blank: He let me ___ the room
उत्तर देखें
Leave (bare infinitive after let).
Verb form in blank: She is looking forward to ___ you
उत्तर देखें
Meeting (gerund after look forward to).
Verb form in blank: He is addicted to ___ online games
उत्तर देखें
Playing (gerund after addicted to).
Verb form in blank: You had better ___ a doctor immediately
उत्तर देखें
Consult (bare infinitive after had better).
Modal verb in blank: Walk fast lest you ___ miss the bus
उत्तर देखें
Should (lest governs should).
Modal verb in blank: We ___ to respect our national flag
उत्तर देखें
Ought (paired with to).
Verb form in blank: It is high time we ___ for home
उत्तर देखें
Left (simple past after it is high time).
Verb form in blank: It is time to ___
उत्तर देखें
Leave (base verb with to-infinitive).

Articles, Pronouns & Quantifier Blanks

Article required in blank: He is ___ honest civil servant
उत्तर देखें
An (silent h, vowel sound).
Article required in blank: Copper is ___ useful metal
उत्तर देखें
A (initial /juː/ consonant sound).
Article required in blank: She graduated from ___ European university
उत्तर देखें
A (consonant sound /juː/).
Article required in blank: He is ___ MBA graduate
उत्तर देखें
An (initial /em/ vowel sound).
Article required in blank: Mount Everest is ___ highest peak
उत्तर देखें
The (superlative degree).
Article required in blank: The higher you climb, ___ cooler it gets
उत्तर देखें
The (parallel comparative structure).
Article required in blank: He is ___ taller of the two boys
उत्तर देखें
The (comparative with of the two).
Article required in blank: He plays ___ flute melodiously
उत्तर देखें
The (musical instrument).
Quantifier in blank: I have ___ hope of his recovery (almost none)
उत्तर देखें
Little (negative meaning, uncountable).
Quantifier in blank: A ___ knowledge is a dangerous thing
उत्तर देखें
Little (positive small amount).
Quantifier in blank: ___ friends he had deserted him in trouble
उत्तर देखें
The few (all that were available).
Quantifier in blank: There are ___ books on the table (a small number)
उत्तर देखें
A few (countable positive).
Quantifier in blank: How ___ sugar do you need?
उत्तर देखें
Much (uncountable noun).
Quantifier in blank: How ___ candidates attended the interview?
उत्तर देखें
Many (plural countable noun).
Pronoun required in blank: One must do ___ duty sincerely
उत्तर देखें
One's (not his).
Pronoun required in blank: The man ___ stole the purse was arrested
उत्तर देखें
Who (subject relative pronoun).
Pronoun required in blank: This is the woman ___ we met yesterday
उत्तर देखें
Whom (object relative pronoun).
Pronoun required in blank: All that glitters ___ not gold
उत्तर देखें
Is (proverbial relative construction).
Pronoun required in blank: This is the same book ___ I bought
उत्तर देखें
That (used after the same).
Pronoun order in blank: ___ must share the prize (you, he, I)
उत्तर देखें
You, he, and I (order 2-3-1).
Pronoun order in blank: ___ are responsible for the blunder
उत्तर देखें
I, you, and he (order 1-2-3 for guilt).
Case of pronoun in blank: There is no dispute between you and ___
उत्तर देखें
Me (objective case after preposition).
Case of pronoun in blank: Let you and ___ solve this issue
उत्तर देखें
Me (objective case after let).
Pronoun in blank: The two brothers helped ___ in business
उत्तर देखें
Each other (two entities).
Pronoun in blank: The three partners supported ___
उत्तर देखें
One another (more than two entities).

Conjunctions, Correlatives & Common Exam Traps

उत्तर देखें
Hardly or Scarcely.
Conjunction in blank: Hardly had the train stopped ___ crowds rushed in
उत्तर देखें
When (not than).
Conjunction in blank: No sooner did the bell ring ___ the students ran
उत्तर देखें
Than (not then/when).
Conjunction in blank: No sooner had he finished ___ he departed
उत्तर देखें
Than.
Conjunction in blank: He is not only knowledgeable ___ extremely humble
उत्तर देखें
But also.
Conjunction in blank: ___ you apologize, you will face disciplinary action
उत्तर देखें
Unless (meaning if not).
Conjunction in blank: Wait in the lounge ___ the doctor calls your name
उत्तर देखें
Until (time limit).
Conjunction in blank: Although he is impoverished, ___ he is deeply honest
उत्तर देखें
Yet (or comma, never but).
Conjunction in blank: The reason why he resigned is ___ he was ill
उत्तर देखें
That (not because).
Conjunction in blank: He had no other choice ___ to surrender unconditionally
उत्तर देखें
Than (paired with other).
Conjunction in blank: It was nothing else ___ pure foolishness
उत्तर देखें
But (paired with else).
Conjunction in blank: He cannot run fast ___ he is injured
उत्तर देखें
Because or as (causality).
Conjunction in blank: She worked tirelessly ___ her brother relaxed
उत्तर देखें
While or whereas (contrast).
