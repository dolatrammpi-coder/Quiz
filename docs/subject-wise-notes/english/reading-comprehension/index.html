Definition of an inference in reading comprehension
Unstated logical deduction derived from stated premises.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें

No, it must be deduced logically.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Key difference between fact and inference
Fact is stated; inference is derived.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Why a restatement of a passage sentence is a bad inference
Direct restatement is fact, not inference.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Definition of an implication in comprehension
Meaning suggested indirectly by the author.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Boundary limit for valid inferences in exams
Must not exceed logical scope of text.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Danger of far-fetched speculation in inference questions
Requires unsupported leaps beyond passage facts.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
How to validate an inference choice

उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
