Narration

🔍
Major Direct-to-Indirect Transformation Rules across Sentence Types
Sentence Type Transformation Rule Example
Assertive Sentences
उत्तर देखें
Said to becomes told; conjunction that connects clauses
उत्तर देखें
He said that he was busy.
Interrogative (Yes/No)
उत्तर देखें
Reporting verb asked/inquired; if/whether conjunction; assertive word order
उत्तर देखें
She asked if I knew him.
Interrogative (Wh-)
उत्तर देखें
Reporting verb asked; Wh-word conjunction; assertive word order
उत्तर देखें
He asked where I lived.
Imperative (Command)
उत्तर देखें
Reporting verb ordered/commanded; to-infinitive connects verb
उत्तर देखें
The officer ordered soldiers to march.
Imperative (Request)
उत्तर देखें
Reporting verb requested/pleaded; please omitted; to-infinitive used
उत्तर देखें
He requested me to help.
Imperative (Prohibition)
उत्तर देखें
Reporting verb forbade; not omitted; to-infinitive used
उत्तर देखें
Mother forbade him to go.
Imperative (Proposal)
उत्तर देखें
Let's converted to suggested/proposed; should-clause used
उत्तर देखें
He suggested that we should walk.
Exclamatory Sentences
उत्तर देखें
Exclaimed with joy/sorrow; conjunction that; assertive order
उत्तर देखें
She exclaimed that it was beautiful.
Optative Sentences
उत्तर देखें
Reporting verb wished/prayed; might + V1 construction
उत्तर देखें
They prayed that God might bless.
Universal Truths
उत्तर देखें
Reporting verb in past; reported clause tense remains unchanged
उत्तर देखें
He said the sun rises east.
Reporting Verbs and Corresponding Clause Structures
Sentence Type Transformation Rule Example
Tell / Told
उत्तर देखें
Mandatory direct object without preposition to
उत्तर देखें
She told me the truth.
Say / Said
उत्तर देखें
Requires preposition to when taking personal object
उत्तर देखें
He said to me.
Inquire / Demand
उत्तर देखें
Requires preposition of before personal object
उत्तर देखें
He inquired of the clerk.
Propose / Suggest
उत्तर देखें
Requires to before personal object and that-should clause
उत्तर देखें
Suggested to us that we go.
Forbid / Forbade
उत्तर देखें
Takes object and to-infinitive; strictly excludes not
उत्तर देखें
The teacher forbade him to talk.
Applaud
उत्तर देखें
Applauded + object + saying that / for V-ing
उत्तर देखें
Applauded him saying he played well.
Warn
उत्तर देखें
Warned + object + against V-ing or not to-V1
उत्तर देखें
Warned them not to enter.
Congratulate
उत्तर देखें
Congratulated + object + on V-ing
उत्तर देखें
Congratulated him on his success.
Admit / Confess
उत्तर देखें
Admitted/confessed + that-clause or to V-ing
उत्तर देखें
He confessed that he stole.
Threaten
उत्तर देखें
Threatened + to-infinitive or that-clause
उत्तर देखें
Threatened to shoot the hostage.

Fundamentals, Terminology & General Tense Shift Rules

Definition of direct speech
उत्तर देखें
Quoting speaker's actual exact words.
Definition of indirect speech (reported speech)
उत्तर देखें
Reporting substance of speech without quoting.
Definition of reporting verb
उत्तर देखें
Verb introducing the spoken statement.
Definition of reported speech
उत्तर देखें
Clause enclosed within quotation marks.
Condition when reported clause tense does not change
उत्तर देखें
Reporting verb in present or future.
Rule when reporting verb is in the past tense
उत्तर देखें
Reported speech shifts to corresponding past.
Tense change for simple present in reported speech
उत्तर देखें
Converts to simple past tense.
Tense change for present continuous in reported speech
उत्तर देखें
Converts to past continuous tense.
Tense change for present perfect in reported speech
उत्तर देखें
Converts to past perfect tense.
Tense change for present perfect continuous
उत्तर देखें
Converts to past perfect continuous.
Tense change for simple past in reported speech
उत्तर देखें
Converts to past perfect tense.
Tense change for past continuous in reported speech
उत्तर देखें
Converts to past perfect continuous.
Tense change for past perfect in reported speech
उत्तर देखें
Remains past perfect (no change).
Tense change for past perfect continuous
उत्तर देखें
Remains unchanged.
Modal shift of can in indirect speech
उत्तर देखें
Converts to could.
Modal shift of may in indirect speech
उत्तर देखें
Converts to might.
Modal shift of will in indirect speech
उत्तर देखें
Converts to would.
Modal shift of shall in indirect speech
उत्तर देखें
Converts to should or would.
Do could, might, should, and would shift in narration?
उत्तर देखें
No, they remain unchanged.
Modal shift of must expressing immediate past obligation
उत्तर देखें
Converts to had to.
When does must remain unchanged in indirect speech?
उत्तर देखें
Expressing universal moral duty or rule.
Are quotation marks retained in indirect speech?
उत्तर देखें
No, quotation marks are removed.
Punctuation mark closing all indirect speech sentences
उत्तर देखें
Full stop (period).
Rule when two simultaneous past actions are connected by while
उत्तर देखें
Tenses remain in past continuous.
Example of unchanged tenses with while
उत्तर देखें
While I was singing, he danced.

Pronoun Transformation & Time/Place Adverbial Shifts

Rule for changing first-person pronouns in reported speech
उत्तर देखें
Change according to reporting subject (SON).
Rule for changing second-person pronouns in reported speech
उत्तर देखें
Change according to reporting object (SON).
Rule for changing third-person pronouns in reported speech
उत्तर देखें
No change occurs (SON rule).
Mnemonic SON formula in narration rules
उत्तर देखें
Subject, Object, No change.
Shift of adverbial this in indirect speech
उत्तर देखें
Converts to that.
Shift of plural demonstrative these in indirect speech
उत्तर देखें
Converts to those.
Shift of place adverbial here in indirect speech
उत्तर देखें
Converts to there.
Shift of time adverbial now in indirect speech
उत्तर देखें
Converts to then.
Shift of time adverbial today in indirect speech
उत्तर देखें
Converts to that day.
Shift of time adverbial tonight in indirect speech
उत्तर देखें
Converts to that night.
Shift of time adverbial yesterday in indirect speech
उत्तर देखें
The previous day / day before.
Shift of time adverbial tomorrow in indirect speech
उत्तर देखें
The next day / following day.
Shift of phrase last night in indirect speech
उत्तर देखें
The previous night.
Shift of phrase next week in indirect speech
उत्तर देखें
The following week.
Shift of time adverbial ago in indirect speech
उत्तर देखें
Converts to before.
Shift of adverb thus in indirect speech
उत्तर देखें
Converts to so.
Shift of archaic adverb hither in indirect speech
उत्तर देखें
Converts to thither.
Shift of archaic adverb hence in indirect speech
उत्तर देखें
Converts to thence.
Condition when this or here does not shift to that or there
उत्तर देखें
Object or place present before speaker.
Condition when today does not shift to that day
उत्तर देखें
Speech reported on the same day.
Indirect form: He said, 'I live here'
उत्तर देखें
He said that he lived there.
Indirect form: She said, 'I bought this yesterday'
उत्तर देखें
Bought that the previous day.
Case retained by pronouns during transformation
उत्तर देखें
Grammatical case remains consistent.
Possessive pronoun shift: my becomes
उत्तर देखें
His or her.
Reflexive pronoun shift: myself becomes
उत्तर देखें
Himself or herself.

Assertive Sentences & Interrogative Transformations

Transformation of said to in assertive sentences
उत्तर देखें
Converts to told.
Can the verb told take preposition to before its object?
उत्तर देखें
Never, told takes direct object.
Conjunction connecting assertive clauses in indirect speech
उत्तर देखें
That.
Indirect form: Ram said to Sham, 'You are honest'
उत्तर देखें
Ram told Sham that he was honest.
Reporting verbs replacing said to in interrogative sentences
उत्तर देखें
Asked, inquired of, questioned, demanded.
Conjunction used for Yes/No questions in indirect speech
उत्तर देखें
If or whether.
Conjunction preferred when question offers an alternative or choice
उत्तर देखें
Whether (whether...or).
Conjunction used for Wh-questions in indirect speech
उत्तर देखें
Wh-word itself acts as conjunction.
Can conjunction that precede wh-words in indirect questions?
उत्तर देखें
Never, that is strictly prohibited.
Can that precede if or whether in indirect questions?
उत्तर देखें
Never, that is strictly prohibited.
Sentence order in indirect interrogative clauses
उत्तर देखें
Converts to assertive subject-verb order.
Why 'He asked me where was I going' is incorrect
उत्तर देखें
Uses inverted interrogative word order.
Corrected form of 'He asked me where was I going'
उत्तर देखें
Asked me where I was going.
Punctuation replacing question mark in indirect questions
उत्तर देखें
Replaced by full stop (period).
Indirect form: He said to me, 'Are you coming?'
उत्तर देखें
He asked me if I was coming.
Indirect form: She said, 'What do you want?'
उत्तर देखें
She asked what I wanted.
Auxiliary do/does behavior in affirmative indirect past tense
उत्तर देखें
Omitted; main verb changes to V2.
Indirect form: He said, 'Where do you live?'
उत्तर देखें
He asked where I lived.
Auxiliary did behavior in negative indirect questions
उत्तर देखें
Converts to had not + V3.
Preposition required after inquire when personal object follows
उत्तर देखें
Of (inquired of me).
Indirect form: 'Shall I open the window?' asked Mohan
उत्तर देखें
Mohan asked if he should open.
Indirect form of questions asking for advice
उत्तर देखें
Shall converts to should.
Handling vocative address (names) in direct questions
उत्तर देखें
Becomes object of the reporting verb.
Indirect form: He said, 'Sita, do you know Ram?'
उत्तर देखें
He asked Sita if she knew Ram.
Can question tags be retained in indirect speech?
उत्तर देखें
No, question tags are dropped.

Imperative, Suggestive & Negative Commands

Reporting verbs replacing said to in imperative sentences
उत्तर देखें
Ordered, requested, advised, commanded, warned.
Grammatical connector used in imperative indirect speech
उत्तर देखें
To-infinitive (to + V1).
Handling words of politeness (please, kindly) in indirect speech
उत्तर देखें
Omitted; verb becomes requested.
Transformation of negative imperative (Do not / Don't)
उत्तर देखें
Not to + V1.
Indirect form: The doctor said, 'Do not smoke'
उत्तर देखें
Advised him not to smoke.
Reporting verb that eliminates the negative word not
उत्तर देखें
Forbade (past of forbid).
Why 'He forbade me not to enter' is a serious exam error
उत्तर देखें
Forbade inherently contains negative meaning.
Corrected form of the forbade sentence
उत्तर देखें
He forbade me to enter.
Prepositional construction alternative for prohibit in indirect speech
उत्तर देखें
Prohibited from + gerund (V-ing).
Transformation of direct sentence starting with 'Let us' (proposal)
उत्तर देखें
Suggested/proposed that we/they should.
Preposition required when suggest or propose takes personal object
उत्तर देखें
To (suggested to me).
Indirect form: He said, 'Let us go for a walk'
उत्तर देखें
Suggested that they should walk.
Transformation of 'Let' expressing permission
उत्तर देखें
Requested to let / might be allowed.
Indirect form: The boy said, 'Let me come in'
उत्तर देखें
Requested that he might enter.
Transformation of 'Let' expressing indifference or supposition
उत्तर देखें
Converted using might or should.
Indirect form: The commander said, 'Fire!'
उत्तर देखें
The commander commanded to fire.
Indirect form: Teacher said, 'Sit down, boys'
उत्तर देखें
Teacher ordered boys to sit.
Indirect form: Mother said, 'Never tell a lie'
उत्तर देखें
Mother advised never to lie.
Position of adverb never in negative imperative infinitive
उत्तर देखें
Placed before to-infinitive (never to).
Transformation of warning imperative: 'Beware of pickpockets'
उत्तर देखें
Warned to beware of pickpockets.
Reporting verb used when urging or encouraging someone
उत्तर देखें
Urged or encouraged.
Indirect form: He said, 'Work hard, my friend'
उत्तर देखें
Urged his friend to work hard.
Is that conjunction used with imperative to-infinitives?
उत्तर देखें
No, that is not used.
Can imperative sentences retain helping verb do in indirect speech?
उत्तर देखें
No, helping verb do is omitted.
Indirect form of emphatic command: 'Do visit us tomorrow'
उत्तर देखें
Warmly requested to visit next day.

Exclamatory & Optative Transformations

Reporting verbs replacing said in exclamatory sentences
उत्तर देखें
Exclaimed with joy, sorrow, surprise, anger.
Handling interjections (Alas!, Hurrah!, Oh!) in indirect speech
उत्तर देखें
Omitted; mood absorbed by reporting verb.
Transformation of interjection 'Alas!'
उत्तर देखें
Exclaimed with sorrow or grief.
Transformation of interjection 'Hurrah!'
उत्तर देखें
Exclaimed with joy or delight.
Transformation of interjection 'Bravo!'
उत्तर देखें
Applauded saying that.
Transformation of interjection 'Pooh!' or 'Fie!'
उत्तर देखें
Exclaimed with contempt or disgust.
Conjunction connecting clauses in indirect exclamatory speech
उत्तर देखें
That.
Sentence structure in indirect exclamatory clause
उत्तर देखें
Converts to assertive statement order.
Transformation of 'What a / How' modifying an adjective
उत्तर देखें
Replaced by very (e.g. very beautiful).
Transformation of 'What a' modifying a noun
उत्तर देखें
Replaced by great, fine, or wonderful.
Indirect form: He said, 'What a beautiful flower!'
उत्तर देखें
Exclaimed that it was very beautiful.
Indirect form: She said, 'What a fool I am!'
उत्तर देखें
Exclaimed that she was a great fool.
Indirect form: 'Alas! I am ruined,' cried the trader
उत्तर देखें
Exclaimed with sorrow that he was ruined.
Punctuation replacing exclamation mark in indirect speech
उत्तर देखें
Replaced by full stop (period).
Reporting verbs replacing said in optative sentences
उत्तर देखें
Wished, prayed, blessed, or cursed.
Modal verb structure in indirect optative clauses
उत्तर देखें
Subject + might + base verb (V1).
Indirect form: The hermit said, 'May you live long!'
उत्तर देखें
The hermit blessed that he might live.
Indirect form: People said, 'May God save the king!'
उत्तर देखें
Prayed that God might save king.
Transformation of greeting 'Good morning' in indirect speech
उत्तर देखें
Reporting verb wished (wished good morning).
Transformation of farewell 'Good night' or 'Goodbye'
उत्तर देखें
Reporting verb bade (bade goodbye).
Indirect form: He said to me, 'Good morning'
उत्तर देखें
He wished me good morning.
Indirect form: She said to her friends, 'Goodbye'
उत्तर देखें
She bade her friends goodbye.
Can optative indirect speech retain direct word order 'might God'?
उत्तर देखें
No, inverted to 'God might'.
Handling curse statements like 'May you go to ruin'
उत्तर देखें
Reporting verb cursed (cursed that...).
Is conjunction that used with simple greetings like wished good morning?
उत्तर देखें
No conjunction is needed.

Universal Truths, Habitual Facts & Common Exam Traps

Tense rule when reported speech contains a universal truth
उत्तर देखें
Tense remains unchanging simple present.
Tense rule when reported speech contains a scientific law
उत्तर देखें
Tense remains unchanging simple present.
Tense rule when reported speech expresses a perpetual habit
उत्तर देखें
Tense remains unchanging simple present.
Tense rule when reported speech quotes a classical proverb
उत्तर देखें
Tense remains unchanged.
Tense rule when reported speech states a historical fact
उत्तर देखें
Tense remains in simple past.
Indirect form: Teacher said, 'The earth moves round the sun'
उत्तर देखें
Teacher said the earth moves round sun.
Why 'Teacher said the earth moved' is an exam error
उत्तर देखें
Universal truths cannot shift to past.
Indirect form: He said, 'Honesty is the best policy'
उत्तर देखें
Said that honesty is best policy.
Indirect form: Historian said, 'India became free in 1947'
उत्तर देखें
Said that India became free in 1947.
Why historical dates do not shift to past perfect
उत्तर देखें
Fixed chronological historical events retain simple past.
Error in: He told to me that he was tired
उत्तर देखें
Delete redundant preposition to after told.
Error in: He asked me that what my name was
उत्तर देखें
Delete redundant conjunction that before what.
Error in: She asked that if I was ready
उत्तर देखें
Delete redundant conjunction that before if.
Error in: The teacher forbade the students not to shout
उत्तर देखें
Delete negative word not after forbade.
Error in: He asked me where was the railway station
उत्तर देखें
Invert word order: where station was.
Error in: She suggested me to visit the library
उत्तर देखें
Insert preposition to: suggested to me.
Handling dialogical answers 'Yes' in indirect speech
उत्तर देखें
Answered/replied in the affirmative.
Handling dialogical answers 'No' in indirect speech
उत्तर देखें
Answered/replied in the negative.
Indirect form: Ram said, 'Yes'
उत्तर देखें
Ram replied in the affirmative.
Indirect form: Sita said, 'No'
उत्तर देखें
Sita replied in the negative.
Transformation of conditional sentences: 'If I were rich, I would help'
उत्तर देखें
Hypothetical unreal condition tenses remain unchanged.
Transformation of past continuous when time clause specifies duration
उत्तर देखें
Often retains past continuous tense.
Difference between said and told regarding objects
उत्तर देखें
Said takes to-object; told takes direct object.
First task when converting direct speech in exams
उत्तर देखें
Check tense of the reporting verb.
Ultimate rule for solving narration questions accurately
उत्तर देखें
Maintain tense logic, word order, and pronouns.
