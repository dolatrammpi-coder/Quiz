Fundamentals, Definitions & The Simple Present Tense

Definition of tense in English grammar
Verb form indicating action's time.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Difference between natural time and grammatical tense
Time is universal; tense is grammatical.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Can present tense express a scheduled future event?
Yes, for fixed scheduled timetables.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Example of present tense expressing future event
The train departs at 5 PM tomorrow.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Three primary grammatical time divisions
Present, past, and future.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Four grammatical aspects for each tense division
Simple, continuous, perfect, perfect continuous.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Total number of standard active tense forms
Twelve distinct tense forms.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Basic affirmative structure of simple present tense
Subject + base verb (V1/V1-s/es).
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
When is suffix -s or -es added to base verb?
With third-person singular subjects.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Primary usage of simple present tense
Universal truths, habits, and facts.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Example of universal truth in simple present
The sun rises in the east.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Example of habitual routine in simple present
He exercises every morning regularly.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Tense used for live sports broadcasting and running commentary
Simple present tense.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Example of sports commentary in simple present
Kohli drives the ball to boundary.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Tense used for dramatic historic present in vivid narratives
Simple present tense.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Example of historic present in narrative
Akbar now marches towards the fortress.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Tense used in exclamatory sentences starting with Here or There
Simple present tense.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Example of Here/There exclamatory sentence
Here comes the chief guest!
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Negative structure of simple present for third-person singular
Subject + does not + V1.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Why 'He does not knows' is an exam error
Auxiliary does requires base verb V1.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Corrected form of 'He does not knows'
He does not know.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Interrogative structure of simple present tense
Do/Does + subject + V1?
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Adverbs of frequency commonly signaling simple present
Always, often, usually, seldom, rarely.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Tense required in time clauses introduced by when or as soon as
Simple present, not simple future.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Example of time clause in simple present
As soon as he arrives, call me.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें

Present Continuous, Present Perfect & Continuous Aspects

Active voice structure of present continuous tense
Subject + is/am/are + V-ing.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Primary usage of present continuous tense
Action happening at time of speaking.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Example of present continuous tense
She is reading an editorial now.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Can present continuous indicate near planned future?
Yes, for fixed personal arrangements.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Example of present continuous for planned future
I am flying to London tomorrow.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Time adverbs commonly signaling present continuous
Now, currently, at present, right now.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Why stative verbs avoid continuous aspect
Express enduring states, not active processes.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Four major semantic categories of stative verbs
Perception, emotion, cognition, and possession.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Correction for: 'I am owning two luxury cars'
I own two luxury cars.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Correction for: 'She is loving classical music'
She loves classical music.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Context where verb 'see' can take continuous aspect
Meaning meeting or interviewing someone.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Example of 'see' used dynamically in continuous tense
The doctor is seeing patients now.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Context where verb 'think' can take continuous aspect
Describing active deliberation or reflection.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Example of 'think' used in continuous tense
I am thinking about your proposal.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Active voice structure of present perfect tense
Subject + has/have + V3.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Primary usage of present perfect tense
Past action having present consequence.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Can specific past time adverbs (yesterday) accompany present perfect?
Never, strictly ungrammatical in standard English.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Why 'I have met him yesterday' is incorrect
Past adverb yesterday requires simple past.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Corrected form of 'I have met him yesterday'
I met him yesterday.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Adverbs typically signaling present perfect tense
Just, already, yet, recently, so far.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Standard sentence position for adverb 'already'
Between auxiliary and main verb.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Sentence types where adverb 'yet' is standardly used
Negative and interrogative sentences.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Active voice structure of present perfect continuous tense
Subject + has/have been + V-ing.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Primary usage of present perfect continuous tense
Action begun past continuing into present.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Difference between since and for in perfect continuous
Since indicates point; for indicates duration.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें

Past Tenses: Simple Past, Continuous, Perfect & Perfect Continuous

Active voice structure of simple past tense
Subject + past form (V2).
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Primary usage of simple past tense
Action completed at definite past time.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Time adverbials strictly requiring simple past tense
Yesterday, ago, last night, in 1947.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Negative structure of simple past tense
Subject + did not + V1.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Why 'He did not went' is a serious exam error
Auxiliary did requires base verb V1.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Corrected form of 'He did not went'
He did not go.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Interrogative structure of simple past tense
Did + subject + V1?
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Active voice structure of past continuous tense
Subject + was/were + V-ing.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Primary usage of past continuous tense
Action in progress at past moment.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Example of past continuous tense
He was reading when I arrived.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Tense used for an interrupted longer past background action
Past continuous tense.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Tense used for shorter interrupting past event
Simple past tense.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Active voice structure of past perfect tense
Subject + had + V3.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Primary usage of past perfect tense
Action completed before another past action.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Tense assignment when two past actions are linked by 'before'
Earlier takes had+V3; later takes V2.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Classic exam example of past perfect with 'before'
The patient had died before doctor arrived.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Tense assignment when two past actions are linked by 'after'
Action after 'after' takes past perfect.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Example of past perfect with 'after'
He went home after work had finished.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Can past perfect be used for isolated single past event?
No, requires simple past tense instead.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Active voice structure of past perfect continuous tense
Subject + had been + V-ing.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Primary usage of past perfect continuous tense
Ongoing past action preceding another past event.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Example of past perfect continuous tense
He had been waiting for two hours.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Tense structure governed by idiom 'It is high time'
Simple past tense (subjunctive).
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Example of sentence using 'It is high time'
It is high time we started studying.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Difference between 'used to' and 'would' for past habits
Used to expresses states; would expresses actions.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें

Future Tenses: Simple, Continuous, Perfect & Perfect Continuous

Active voice structure of simple future tense
Subject + will/shall + V1.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Traditional first-person modal for pure future assertion
Shall (I shall, We shall).
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Modern informal usage trend for future modal auxiliary
Will is widely used across all persons.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Meaning when 'shall' is used with second/third person
Command, promise, threat, or legal regulation.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Meaning when 'will' is used with first-person subjects
Determination, promise, threat, or strong resolve.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Active voice structure of future continuous tense
Subject + will/shall be + V-ing.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Primary usage of future continuous tense
Action in progress at specific future time.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Example of future continuous tense
At 10 AM tomorrow, I will be taking exam.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Can future continuous express routine expected future events?
Yes, planned normal future happenings.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Active voice structure of future perfect tense
Subject + will/shall have + V3.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Primary usage of future perfect tense
Action completed by specific future point.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Preposition characteristically signaling future perfect tense
By (e.g. by tomorrow, by 2030).
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Tense required in time clause accompanying future perfect
Simple present tense.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Example of future perfect with time clause
He will have finished by the time you arrive.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Can modal 'will' appear inside future time clause?
Never, simple present tense is required.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Correction for: 'He will have left by the time you will arrive'
Replace will arrive with arrive.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Active voice structure of future perfect continuous tense
Subject + will have been + V-ing.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Primary usage of future perfect continuous tense
Action progressing up to specific future milestone.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Example of future perfect continuous tense
By 2028, she will have been teaching ten years.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Usage of 'be going to' structure for future time
Premeditated intention or obvious impending certainty.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Example of 'going to' expressing impending certainty
Look at clouds; it is going to rain.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Meaning expressed by 'be about to + base verb'
Imminent action on the verge of occurring.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Tense used for official institutional timetables in future
Simple present tense.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Can future modals appear inside conditional if-clauses?
Never in standard conditional statements.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Correction for: 'If it will rain, we will cancel'
If it rains, we will cancel.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें

Time Adverbials, Clues & Sequence of Tenses

Definition of sequence of tenses rule
Subordinate clause tense harmonizes with principal clause.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
General rule when principal clause is in past tense
Subordinate clause must be in past.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Example of past sequence of tenses
He told me that he was unwell.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
First exception to sequence of tenses rule
Subordinate clause expressing universal truth.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Example of universal truth exception to sequence of tenses
Copernicus proved that the earth revolves.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Second exception to sequence of tenses rule
Subordinate clause introduced by comparative 'than'.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Example of comparison exception to sequence of tenses
He loved me more than he loves his dog.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Modal auxiliary governed by negative conjunction 'lest'
Should + bare base verb.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Preposition indicating precise starting point in perfect tenses
Since (e.g. since 2010, since Monday).
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Preposition indicating total duration in perfect tenses
For (e.g. for five years, for two hours).
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Can 'since' indicate future starting point?
No, use 'from' for future time.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Example of future starting point using 'from'
The examination starts from next Monday.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Meaning conveyed by adverb 'already' in tense usage
Action completed earlier than anticipated.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Meaning conveyed by adverb 'yet' in tense usage
Action expected but unfulfilled so far.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Meaning conveyed by adverb 'just' in tense usage
Action completed a very short time ago.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Tense strictly governed by phrase 'just now'
Simple past tense.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Difference between 'just' and 'just now' in tense selection
Just takes perfect; just now takes simple past.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Example of simple past with 'just now'
The postman arrived just now.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Tense governed by 'while' for simultaneous past actions
Past continuous in both clauses.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Example of simultaneous past actions with 'while'
While mother cooked, father was reading.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Tense governed by 'as if' for hypothetical present unreality
Simple past subjunctive (were).
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Example of hypothetical present with 'as if'
He talks as if he were a king.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Tense governed by 'as if' for past counterfactual statement
Past perfect tense (had been).
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Tense following 'wish' for present unfulfilled desire
Simple past (I wish I knew).
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Tense following 'wish' for past unfulfilled regret
Past perfect (I wish I had attended).
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें

Common Tense Errors, Conditionals & Exam Traps

Error in: 'I have seen the Taj Mahal last year'
Replace have seen with saw.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Why 'last year' rejects present perfect tense
Definite past time demands simple past.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Error in: 'He is having a large family'
Stative possession requires 'He has'.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Why 'have' for possession rejects continuous tense
Stative verb expressing ownership.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
When can 'have' take continuous tense?
Describing active eating, drinking, or experiencing.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Example of 'have' in continuous tense
