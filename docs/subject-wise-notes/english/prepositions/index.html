14. Prepositions
PrepositionUsage/RuleExample
In vs At (Place)
उत्तर देखें
In for large areas; at for specific points
उत्तर देखें
Lives at Rohini in Delhi.
In vs Into
उत्तर देखें
In denotes rest inside; into denotes entering motion
उत्तर देखें
Sat in room; jumped into pool.
On vs Onto
उत्तर देखें
On denotes surface contact; onto denotes upward movement
उत्तर देखें
Book on table; cat jumped onto bed.
Between vs Among
उत्तर देखें
Between for two entities; among for three or more
उत्तर देखें
Between Ram and Sham; among students.
Beside vs Besides
उत्तर देखें
Beside means at the side of; besides means in addition to
उत्तर देखें
Sat beside me; knows French besides English.
By vs With
उत्तर देखें
By denotes human agent; with denotes tool/instrument
उत्तर देखें
Tiger killed by hunter with gun.
Since vs For
उत्तर देखें
Since denotes point of time; for denotes duration
उत्तर देखें
Since 2015; for five years.
Across vs Through
उत्तर देखें
Across means flat surface; through means enclosed space
उत्तर देखें
Swam across river; walked through forest.
Above vs Over
उत्तर देखें
Above means higher level; over implies vertical cover
उत्तर देखें
Ceiling above us; umbrella over head.
Below vs Under
उत्तर देखें
Below indicates lower rank/level; under indicates directly beneath
उत्तर देखें
Below average marks; dog under table.
Beneath vs Underneath
उत्तर देखें
Beneath implies directly covered or inferior status
उत्तर देखें
Buried beneath earth; beneath his dignity.
During vs For
उत्तर देखें
During specifies time frame; for denotes measured duration
उत्तर देखें
During the summer; stayed for two weeks.

Abstain / Refrain
उत्तर देखें
Takes preposition from before gerund or noun
उत्तर देखें
Abstain from alcohol completely.
Accused / Acquitted
उत्तर देखें
Governs preposition of for crimes and charges
उत्तर देखें
Accused of theft; acquitted of murder.
Adhere / Comply
उत्तर देखें
Adhere takes to; comply takes with
उत्तर देखें
Adhere to rules; comply with regulations.
Senior / Junior / Prior
उत्तर देखें
Latin comparative adjectives take to, never than
उत्तर देखें
He is senior to me.
Congratulate / Insist
उत्तर देखें
Both govern preposition on before noun/gerund
उत्तर देखें
Congratulated on victory; insisted on going.
Deprive / Rob
उत्तर देखें
Governs preposition of for stolen/withheld items
उत्तर देखें
Deprived of rights; robbed of jewelry.
Cope with
उत्तर देखें
Standard idiom; never use redundant up
उत्तर देखें
Can cope with academic pressure.
Eligible / Long for
उत्तर देखें
Governs preposition for expressing qualification/desire
उत्तर देखें
Eligible for post; long for peace.
Good at / Excel in
उत्तर देखें
Good governs at; excel governs in
उत्तर देखें
Good at math; excels in singing.
Die of vs Die from
उत्तर देखें
Die of disease; die from indirect causes
उत्तर देखें
Died of cancer; died from thirst.
Agree with vs Agree to
उत्तर देखें
Agree with person; agree to proposal/terms
उत्तर देखें
Agree with you; agree to plan.
Married to
उत्तर देखें
Passive voice governs to, never with
उत्तर देखें
She is married to a doctor.
