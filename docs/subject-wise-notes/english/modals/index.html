Modals

🔍
Major Modal Verbs and Their Principal Uses
Modal Main Use Example
Can
उत्तर देखें
Present ability, informal permission, or possibility
उत्तर देखें
She can speak five languages fluently.
Could
उत्तर देखें
Past ability, polite request, or remote possibility
उत्तर देखें
Could you pass the salt, please?
May
उत्तर देखें
Formal permission, factual possibility, or wish
उत्तर देखें
May I leave the room now?
Might
उत्तर देखें
Weak or remote possibility and polite hesitation
उत्तर देखें
It might rain later this evening.
Shall
उत्तर देखें
First-person offers, suggestions, or official regulations
उत्तर देखें
Shall we begin the meeting now?
Should
उत्तर देखें
Moral obligation, sensible advice, or logical expectation
उत्तर देखें
You should consult a physician immediately.
Will
उत्तर देखें
Future certainty, determination, or willing intention
उत्तर देखें
I will achieve my target anyhow.
Would
उत्तर देखें
Past routine habit, preference, or polite request
उत्तर देखें
Would you like some warm tea?
Must
उत्तर देखें
Strong compulsion, urgent necessity, or deduction
उत्तर देखें
Candidates must bring their hall tickets.
Ought to
उत्तर देखें
Moral obligation or social duty
उत्तर देखें
We ought to respect our elders.
Need
उत्तर देखें
Absence of obligation in negative/interrogative
उत्तर देखें
You need not worry about results.
Dare
उत्तर देखें
Expressing courage in negative/interrogative sentences
उत्तर देखें
How dare you enter without permission?
Used to
उत्तर देखें
Discontinued past habit or former state
उत्तर देखें
He used to swim every morning.
Comparative Analysis of Confusing Modal Pairs
Modal Main Use Example
Can vs Could
उत्तर देखें
Can expresses present ability; could expresses past ability
उत्तर देखें
He can run now; could run faster.
May vs Might
उत्तर देखें
May denotes strong possibility; might denotes weak possibility
उत्तर देखें
He may come (likely); might come (doubtful).
May vs Can (Permission)
उत्तर देखें
May is formal polite; can is casual informal
उत्तर देखें
May I speak vs Can I take?
Shall vs Will (First Person)
उत्तर देखें
Shall denotes plain future; will expresses firm resolve
उत्तर देखें
I shall visit vs I will succeed.
Should vs Ought to
उत्तर देखें
Should offers general advice; ought to conveys duty
उत्तर देखें
Should sleep early; ought to help.
Must vs Have to
उत्तर देखें
Must conveys internal resolve; have to external compulsion
उत्तर देखें
I must study vs I have to.
Must not vs Need not
उत्तर देखें
Must not means prohibited; need not means optional
उत्तर देखें
Must not park vs Need not pay.
Would rather vs Prefer
उत्तर देखें
Would rather takes bare infinitive; prefer takes gerund
उत्तर देखें
Would rather stay vs Prefer reading.

Nature, Definition & General Rules of Modal Auxiliaries

Definition of a modal auxiliary verb
उत्तर देखें
Verb expressing mood, attitude, or modality.
Form of the principal verb immediately following a modal
उत्तर देखें
Bare base infinitive (V1).
Do modal auxiliary verbs inflect for third-person singular (-s/-es)?
उत्तर देखें
No, they never take inflectional endings.
Can modal verbs take primary participle suffixes (-ing or -ed)?
उत्तर देखें
No, modals have no participle forms.
Can two modal verbs be placed consecutively without a conjunction?
उत्तर देखें
No, double modals are strictly ungrammatical.
How is a negative statement formed with a modal verb?
उत्तर देखें
Add 'not' immediately following the modal.
How is an interrogative statement formed with a modal verb?
उत्तर देखें
Invert modal before the grammatical subject.
Which modal verbs are classified as semi-modals or marginal modals?
उत्तर देखें
Need, dare, and used to.
Why is 'used to' categorized as a marginal modal?
उत्तर देखें
Combines with to-infinitive and auxiliary 'did'.
Why are 'need' and 'dare' termed semi-modals?
उत्तर देखें
Function as main verbs and auxiliaries.
Can 'can' be followed by a to-infinitive?
उत्तर देखें
Never, takes bare infinitive only.
What core linguistic notions do modals primarily express?
उत्तर देखें
Ability, permission, necessity, obligation, and possibility.
What is the contracted negative form of 'shall'?
उत्तर देखें
Shan't.
What is the contracted negative form of 'will'?
उत्तर देखें
Won't.
What is the contracted negative form of 'must'?
उत्तर देखें
Mustn't.
What is the contracted negative form of 'ought to'?
उत्तर देखें
Oughtn't to.
Are modal verbs capable of standing alone as complete predicates?
उत्तर देखें
Only in elliptical short responses.
Example of an elliptical response with a modal
उत्तर देखें
Yes, I can.
Role of modal auxiliaries in question tags
उत्तर देखें
The modal repeats in the tag.
Question tag for: 'She can drive well'
उत्तर देखें
Can't she?

Can and Could: Ability, Possibility & Requests

Primary function of the modal 'can' regarding skills
उत्तर देखें
Expresses present general ability or capacity.
Primary function of 'could' regarding past capability
उत्तर देखें
Expresses past general ability.
Difference between 'could' and 'was/were able to' for specific past events
उत्तर देखें
'Was able to' denotes single success.
Tone conveyed by 'could' in present requests
उत्तर देखें
Polite and tentative request.
Degree of possibility expressed by 'can'
उत्तर देखें
General theoretical possibility.
Degree of possibility expressed by 'could'
उत्तर देखें
Specific, conditional, or remote possibility.
Can 'can' be used to express official formal permission?
उत्तर देखें
No, 'may' is preferred formally.
Meaning of negative modal 'cannot' (can't) regarding deductions
उत्तर देखें
Logical impossibility.
Example of logical impossibility with 'cannot'
उत्तर देखें
That cannot be true.
Is 'cannot' written as one word or two words?
उत्तर देखें
Standardly written as one word.
Meaning of 'could have + V3'
उत्तर देखें
Unfulfilled past capability or missed opportunity.
Example of unfulfilled past capability
उत्तर देखें
He could have won the race.
Use of 'can' with verbs of perception (see, hear, smell)
उत्तर देखें
Replaces continuous tense for perception.
Example of 'can' modifying a verb of perception
उत्तर देखें
I can hear music playing.
Can 'could' express present or future time reference?
उत्तर देखें
Yes, in polite requests and conditionals.
Meaning of idiom 'cannot help + V-ing'
उत्तर देखें
Unable to stop oneself doing something.
Meaning of idiom 'cannot but + base verb'
उत्तर देखें
Have no choice except doing something.
Which expression takes a gerund: 'cannot help' or 'cannot but'?
उत्तर देखें
'Cannot help' takes a gerund.
Which expression takes a bare infinitive: 'cannot but' or 'cannot help'?
उत्तर देखें
'Cannot but' takes bare infinitive.
Why 'I could not be able to come' is an exam error
उत्तर देखें
Redundant pairing of 'could' and 'able'.

May and Might: Permission, Probability & Optative Wishes

Primary function of 'may' in administrative or polite contexts
उत्तर देखें
Seeking or granting formal permission.
Degree of factual possibility expressed by 'may'
उत्तर देखें
Good, realistic, or 50% probability.
Degree of possibility expressed by 'might'
उत्तर देखें
Weak, doubtful, or remote possibility.
Function of 'may' in optative sentences expressing wishes or prayers
उत्तर देखें
Expresses blessings, wishes, or curses.
Word order in optative sentences introduced by 'may'
उत्तर देखें
May + subject + base verb.
Example of optative sentence using 'may'
उत्तर देखें
May you achieve grand success!
Modal used in purpose clauses with 'so that' in the present tense
उत्तर देखें
May.
Modal used in purpose clauses with 'so that' in the past tense
उत्तर देखें
Might.
Example of 'so that' governing 'may'
उत्तर देखें
We eat that we may live.
Example of 'so that' governing 'might'
उत्तर देखें
He worked hard that he might succeed.
Meaning of 'may have + V3'
उत्तर देखें
Past possibility that possibly occurred.
Meaning of 'might have + V3'
उत्तर देखें
Past possibility that did not occur.
Can 'might' be used in polite inquiries with extreme hesitation?
उत्तर देखें
Yes, expresses very tentative requests.
Example of hesitant polite inquiry with 'might'
उत्तर देखें
Might I borrow your pen?
Can 'may' be used to express past general ability?
उत्तर देखें
Never, 'could' expresses past ability.
Difference between 'may be' and 'maybe'
उत्तर देखें
'May be' is verb phrase; 'maybe' adverb.
Part of speech of 'maybe' written as a single word
उत्तर देखें
Adverb meaning perhaps.
Why 'He may likely come' is considered erroneous
उत्तर देखें
Redundant combination of 'may' and 'likely'.
Corrected version of 'He may likely come'
उत्तर देखें
He may come (or is likely).
Tone conveyed by 'might as well'
उत्तर देखें
Accepting choice with mild resignation.

Shall and Should: Rules, Inversion & Conjunction Pairing

Traditional rule for 'shall' in simple future statements
उत्तर देखें
Used with first person (I, We).
Meaning when 'shall' is used with second or third person subjects
उत्तर देखें
Expresses command, threat, promise, or determination.
Example of 'shall' expressing a legal requirement or command
उत्तर देखें
You shall not steal.
Function of 'shall' in first-person interrogative questions
उत्तर देखें
Offers assistance or makes suggestions.
Example of 'shall' offering assistance
उत्तर देखें
Shall I carry your bag?
Primary semantic function of 'should'
उत्तर देखें
Expresses advice, duty, or logical expectation.
Fixed modal verb governed by the negative conjunction 'lest'
उत्तर देखें
Should.
Can 'not' follow 'should' in a clause introduced by 'lest'?
उत्तर देखें
Never, 'lest' inherently contains negation.
Function of 'should' in inverted conditional clauses
उत्तर देखें
Expresses unlikely or hypothetical condition.
Inverted conditional structure using 'should'
उत्तर देखें
Should + subject + base verb (V1).
Meaning of 'should have + V3'
उत्तर देखें
Unfulfilled past moral duty or obligation.
Example of unfulfilled past duty with 'should have'
उत्तर देखें
You should have spoken the truth.
Meaning of 'should not have + V3'
उत्तर देखें
Criticism of an improper past action.
Example of criticism with 'should not have'
उत्तर देखें
You should not have insulted him.
Question tag for sentences beginning with imperative 'Let's'
उत्तर देखें
Shall we?
Question tag for: 'Let us help the needy'
उत्तर देखें
Shall we?
Use of 'should' following verbs of demand or recommendation (subjunctive)
उत्तर देखें
Mandative should in noun clauses.
Example of mandative 'should'
उत्तर देखें
I demand that he should resign.
Can 'should' denote mathematical or logical deduction?
उत्तर देखें
Yes, expresses high expected probability.
Example of logical deduction with 'should'
उत्तर देखें
The train should arrive any minute.

Will, Would & Habitual, Preference & Polite Structures

Traditional rule for 'will' in simple future assertions
उत्तर देखें
Used with second and third persons.
Meaning when 'will' is paired with first person subjects (I, We)
उत्तर देखें
Expresses promise, threat, resolution, or willingness.
Use of 'will' to denote characteristic or habitual present behavior
उत्तर देखें
Describes persistent natural habits.
Example of habitual present behavior with 'will'
उत्तर देखें
A drowning man will catch straws.
Use of 'would' to denote discontinued past habitual actions
उत्तर देखें
Replaces 'used to' for repeated habits.
Example of past repeated habit with 'would'
उत्तर देखें
He would sit for hours meditating.
Primary difference between 'would' and 'used to' for past states
उत्तर देखें
'Used to' expresses states; 'would' actions.
Why 'He would live in London' is ungrammatical for past state
उत्तर देखें
Stative verbs require 'used to'.
Tone conveyed by 'would' in present polite requests
उत्तर देखें
Highly polite and courteous inquiry.
Fixed verb form following phrase 'would you mind'
उत्तर देखें
Gerund (V-ing).
Example of polite request with 'would you mind'
उत्तर देखें
Would you mind closing the door?
Fixed verb form following 'would rather'
उत्तर देखें
Bare base infinitive (V1).
Conjunction paired with 'would rather' to show preference
उत्तर देखें
Than.
Example sentence using 'would rather...than'
उत्तर देखें
I would rather starve than beg.
Tense governed by 'would rather + subject' referring to present/future
उत्तर देखें
Simple past subjunctive.
Example of 'would rather' with a secondary subject
उत्तर देखें
I would rather you left now.
Meaning of 'would have + V3' in conditionals
उत्तर देखें
Unreal hypothetical result of past condition.
Use of 'would' to express unreal wishes with 'I wish'
उत्तर देखें
Expresses desire for someone to change.
Example of 'would' expressing frustrated wish
उत्तर देखें
I wish it would stop raining.
Can 'will' appear inside a conditional if-clause?
उत्तर देखें
Never in standard conditional statements.

Must, Ought to, Need, Dare & Used to

Primary meaning of 'must' in imperative or regulatory context
उत्तर देखें
Absolute compulsion or urgent necessity.
Primary meaning of 'must' in epistemic logical reasoning
उत्तर देखें
Strong positive logical deduction.
Example of logical deduction with 'must'
उत्तर देखें
He has a mansion; he must be rich.
What is the past tense form of 'must' when expressing compulsion?
उत्तर देखें
Had to.
Meaning of 'must have + V3'
उत्तर देखें
Certain logical deduction about past event.
Example of past logical deduction with 'must have'
उत्तर देखें
He must have reached by now.
Difference between 'must not' and 'need not'
उत्तर देखें
'Must not' prohibits; 'need not' relieves.
Fixed preposition paired with the modal 'ought'
उत्तर देखें
To.
Core semantic difference between 'should' and 'ought to'
उत्तर देखें
'Ought to' emphasizes objective moral duty.
Meaning of 'ought to have + V3'
उत्तर देखें
Neglected past moral duty.
Syntactic behavior of 'need' when acting as a modal auxiliary
उत्तर देखें
Takes bare infinitive without third-person -s.
In which sentence types does 'need' primarily function as a modal?
उत्तर देखें
Negative and interrogative sentences.
Example of 'need' functioning as a modal auxiliary
उत्तर देखें
He need not come today.
Why 'He needs not come' is a serious exam error
उत्तर देखें
Modal 'need' never takes suffix -s.
Meaning of 'need not have + V3'
उत्तर देखें
Action performed unnecessarily in the past.
Difference between 'did not need to' and 'need not have + V3'
उत्तर देखें
'Did not need' avoided; 'needn't have' did.
Syntactic behavior of 'dare' as a modal auxiliary
उत्तर देखें
Takes bare infinitive without auxiliary do/did.
Example of 'dare' functioning as a modal auxiliary
उत्तर देखें
He dare not oppose his father.
Verb form following 'used to' expressing a past habit
उत्तर देखें
Base form of verb (V1).
Verb form following 'be / get used to' meaning accustomed
उत्तर देखें
Gerund (V-ing).

Modal Perfect Forms, Common Traps & Exam Errors

Formula for constructing modal perfect forms
उत्तर देखें
Modal + have + past participle (V3).
Can the auxiliary 'has' or 'had' ever follow a modal verb directly?
उत्तर देखें
Never, always use base form 'have'.
Why 'He must has reached' is grammatically incorrect
उत्तर देखें
Modals must take base form 'have'.
Corrected form of 'He must has reached'
उत्तर देखें
He must have reached.
Meaning conveyed by 'could have + V3'
उत्तर देखें
Unfulfilled past capability.
Meaning conveyed by 'should have + V3'
उत्तर देखें
Unfulfilled past moral duty.
Meaning conveyed by 'must have + V3'
उत्तर देखें
Definite past logical deduction.
Meaning conveyed by 'might have + V3'
उत्तर देखें
Weak unverified past possibility.
Meaning conveyed by 'need not have + V3'
उत्तर देखें
Unnecessary past action that occurred.
Error identification in: 'You ought respect your parents'
उत्तर देखें
Insert missing preposition 'to' after ought.
Error identification in: 'He could not able to pass'
उत्तर देखें
Delete 'able to' or replace could.
Error identification in: 'He does not dare to speak' versus modal dare
उत्तर देखें
Both correct: main verb versus modal.
Why 'We shall to help them' is wrong
उत्तर देखें
Modal 'shall' takes bare infinitive.
Why 'I am used to wake up early' is incorrect
उत्तर देखें
Change 'wake' to gerund 'waking'.
Corrected version of 'I am used to wake up early'
उत्तर देखें
I am used to waking up early.
Error identification in: 'Walk carefully lest you will stumble'
उत्तर देखें
Replace 'will' with 'should'.
Error in: 'If he will arrive on time, we will begin'
उत्तर देखें
Delete 'will' from conditional if-clause.
Error in: 'Would you mind to lend me your book?'
उत्तर देखें
Replace 'to lend' with 'lending'.
Error in: 'I would rather to die than tell a lie'
उत्तर देखें
Delete 'to' after 'would rather'.
Core rule for solving modal questions in competitive exams
उत्तर देखें
Verify base verbs, bare infinitives, and contexts.
