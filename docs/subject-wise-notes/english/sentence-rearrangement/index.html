Concept, Definition & Identifying Opening Sentences

Primary objective of sentence rearrangement (para jumbles)
उत्तर देखें
Reconstruct logically coherent paragraph from jumbled sentences.
First step in solving sentence rearrangement questions
उत्तर देखें
Identify the independent opening sentence.
Definition of an opening sentence in a paragraph
उत्तर देखें
Independent sentence introducing central theme.
Can a sentence starting with 'However' be the opening sentence?
उत्तर देखें
Rarely, as it implies prior contrast.
Can a sentence starting with 'Therefore' open a paragraph?
उत्तर देखें
No, it signals a prior deduction.
Can personal pronouns (he, she, they) begin an opening sentence?
उत्तर देखें
Rarely, unless antecedent is introduced simultaneously.
Role of proper nouns in identifying opening sentences
उत्तर देखें
Proper nouns typically introduce main subject.
Rule regarding full names versus surnames in sequence
उत्तर देखें
Full name precedes surname or abbreviation.
Rule regarding full institutional names versus acronyms
उत्तर देखें
Expanded acronym precedes abbreviated initials.
Example of acronym progression
उत्तर देखें
World Health Organization precedes WHO.
Article progression rule for sentence sequence
उत्तर देखें
Indefinite article precedes definite article.
Why 'A dog barked' precedes 'The dog ran'
उत्तर देखें
Indefinite article introduces, definite specifies.
Characteristic tone of an opening sentence
उत्तर देखें
Broad, introductory, and self-contained.
Can demonstrative pronouns (this, that, these) begin a passage?
उत्तर देखें
Rarely, they require clear prior reference.
Effect of starting with a rhetorical question
उत्तर देखें
Often opens paragraph to spark discussion.
Definition of an independent sentence
उत्तर देखें
Sentence making complete sense without context.
Role of universal definitions in opening sentences
उत्तर देखें
Definitions often serve as opening statements.
Can transition words like 'moreover' open a paragraph?
उत्तर देखें
No, they indicate additive prior points.
Can 'on the contrary' begin an opening sentence?
उत्तर देखें
No, requires preceding statement to refute.
What to look for among options when identifying opening sentence
उत्तर देखें
Check initial letters in answer choices.
Benefit of scanning option first letters
उत्तर देखें
Narrows candidate opening sentences to two.
Role of historical background in opening sentences
उत्तर देखें
Sets chronological stage for narrative progression.
Can an opening sentence begin with 'In fact'?
उत्तर देखें
Rarely, usually emphasizes an earlier claim.
Identifying opening sentence in problem-solution texts
उत्तर देखें
Sentence defining the problem comes first.
Identifying opening sentence in biographical passages
उत्तर देखें
Birth or early life introduction opens.

Identifying Concluding Sentences & Paragraph Closures

Definition of a concluding sentence in paragraph sequence
उत्तर देखें
Sentence delivering final synthesis or resolution.
Major connectors signaling a concluding sentence
उत्तर देखें
Therefore, thus, hence, consequently, ultimately.
Can a concluding sentence raise an unresolved new premise?
उत्तर देखें
No, it resolves preceding premises.
Function of 'Ultimately' in sentence rearrangement
उत्तर देखें
Signals final long-term outcome.
Function of 'In conclusion' in sequence
उत्तर देखें
Formally marks the terminal sentence.
Can a forward-looking recommendation conclude a paragraph?
उत्तर देखें
Yes, recommendations often form final sentences.
Can a call-to-action sentence serve as conclusion?
उत्तर देखें
Yes, common in persuasive passages.
Role of summary statements in concluding sentences
उत्तर देखें
Encapsulate core message of preceding sentences.
Why a sentence introducing 'First of all' cannot conclude
उत्तर देखें
Signals beginning of an enumerative list.
Significance of 'Finally' in sequential processes
उत्तर देखें
Marks last step in chronological order.
Relationship between penultimate and final sentence
उत्तर देखें
Final sentence naturally resolves penultimate tension.
Can a rhetorical question conclude a paragraph?
उत्तर देखें
Yes, leaves reader with reflective takeaway.
Identifying conclusion in scientific experiment passages
उत्तर देखें
Sentence stating final verified result concludes.
Identifying conclusion in policy debate passages
उत्तर देखें
Sentence assessing future viability concludes.
Why sentences with open-ended pronouns cannot easily conclude
उत्तर देखें
Pronouns without closure leave thoughts dangling.
Function of 'As a result' at sentence start
उत्तर देखें
Concludes cause-and-effect relationship.
Can a sentence starting with 'For example' be the conclusion?
उत्तर देखें
Rarely, examples typically precede conclusions.
How cyclic paragraphs conclude
उत्तर देखें
Echo opening sentence with deeper resolution.
How linear narrative passages conclude
उत्तर देखें
Conclude with final chronological milestone.
Identifying conclusion when no overt transition word exists
उत्तर देखें
Look for broadest, final synthesis sentence.
Can a sentence starting with 'Such measures' conclude?
उत्तर देखें
Yes, summarizing previously detailed actions.
Role of moral or lesson in fable passages
उत्तर देखें
Forms the mandatory terminal sentence.
Checking option end-letters for fast elimination
उत्तर देखें
Verify whether last letter makes sense.
Danger of picking an abrupt sentence as conclusion
उत्तर देखें
Leaves narrative unfinished and disjointed.
Benchmark test for an accurate concluding sentence
उत्तर देखें
Leaves no logical expectation unanswered.

Mandatory Pairs & Structural Linkages

Definition of a mandatory pair in sentence rearrangement
उत्तर देखें
Two sentences that must follow consecutively.
Primary method to establish mandatory pairs
उत्तर देखें
Trace noun-pronoun, cause-effect, or correlative links.
Correlative conjunction pair rule across sentences
उत्तर देखें
'Not only' sentence precedes 'but also'.
Comparison pair rule: 'On one hand' and 'On the other hand'
उत्तर देखें
'On one hand' precedes 'on other'.
Sequence of question and answer in paragraphs
उत्तर देखें
Question sentence precedes its explanatory answer.
Sequence of problem and solution in paragraphs
उत्तर देखें
Problem statement precedes its proposed solution.
Sequence of general statement and concrete example
उत्तर देखें
General statement precedes specific illustrative example.
Sequence of statement and elaboration
उत्तर देखें
Assertion precedes sentence starting with 'Specifically'.
Mandatory pair formed by 'former' and 'latter'
उत्तर देखें
Sentence introducing two items precedes 'former/latter'.
Mandatory pair formed by cause and effect
उत्तर देखें
Cause sentence precedes 'Consequently' sentence.
Mandatory pair formed by action and reaction
उत्तर देखें
Stimulus sentence precedes response sentence.
Mandatory pair formed by hypothesis and experimental test
उत्तर देखें
Theory sentence precedes testing sentence.
Mandatory pair formed by claim and counter-claim
उत्तर देखें
Claim precedes sentence refuting it with 'However'.
How to use mandatory pairs with options elimination
उत्तर देखें
Eliminate options where pair is separated.
If AB is a confirmed mandatory pair, what to check?
उत्तर देखें
Discard options with BA or separated AB.
Significance of colon at end of a sentence
उत्तर देखें
Next sentence must explain or list.
Significance of semicolon linking two independent clauses
उत्तर देखें
Indicates extremely close logical partnership.
Sequence of quotation and speaker attribution
उत्तर देखें
Quotation paired directly with contextual attribution.
Mandatory pair in temporal narrative
उत्तर देखें
Earlier chronological date precedes later date.
Mandatory pair with repeated technical noun
उत्तर देखें
Definition precedes subsequent functional analysis.
Can a mandatory pair be interrupted by a digression?
उत्तर देखें
No, they must remain contiguous.
Identifying mandatory pairs through transitional adverbs
उत्तर देखें
Adverb like 'Furthermore' links to predecessor.
Speed advantage of finding one mandatory pair
उत्तर देखें
Often eliminates three of four options.
What to do after identifying two mandatory pairs (e.g. AB, CD)
उत्तर देखें
Combine pairs to form full chain.
Core takeaway for mandatory pair technique
उत्तर देखें
Fastest and most reliable solving shortcut.

Noun sentence precedes referencing pronoun sentence.

उत्तर देखें
Pronoun cannot precede its specific antecedent.
Order of 'Albert Einstein' and 'Einstein' in sequence
उत्तर देखें
Full formal name precedes surname alone.
Order of 'The Reserve Bank of India' and 'RBI'
उत्तर देखें
Expanded name precedes abbreviated acronym.

Demonstrative phrase 'This approach' linking rule

Demonstrative phrase 'Such problems' linking rule
उत्तर देखें
Must follow sentence enumerating actual problems.
Article rule: 'a machine' versus 'the machine'
उत्तर देखें
'A machine' precedes 'the machine'.
Definite article 'The' as a sequence indicator

Pronoun 'They' linking rule across sentences

Pronoun 'It' referencing abstract concepts

Possessive determiner 'Its' linking rule

Possessive determiner 'Their' linking rule

Sequence of title introduction and subsequent references
उत्तर देखें
'Prime Minister Narendra Modi' precedes 'Modi'.
Role of demonstrative adverb 'There' in sequence
उत्तर देखें
Follows sentence introducing specific location.
Role of temporal adverb 'Then' in sequence
उत्तर देखें

उत्तर देखें

उत्तर देखें
Arrangement according to time of occurrence.
Standard progression of historical dates in passages
उत्तर देखें
Earlier historical dates precede later dates.
Order of life events in biographical sequence
उत्तर देखें
Birth, education, career, achievements, death.

उत्तर देखें
Initially, then, afterwards, later, finally.

उत्तर देखें
Cause statement precedes resultant consequence statement.

उत्तर देखें
Cause must occur before effect.

उत्तर देखें

उत्तर देखें
Broad overview followed by detailed breakdown.

Definition of specific-to-general (inductive) sequence
उत्तर देखें
Individual observations leading to general principle.

उत्तर देखें
Observation precedes hypothesis, then experimental verification.
Progression in problem-solving paragraphs
उत्तर देखें

Progression in comparative paragraphs
उत्तर देखें

Progression in narrative storytelling
उत्तर देखें

Order of 'In the 19th century' and 'In 1995'

Order of 'ancient times' and 'modern era'
उत्तर देखें

Progression in procedural instructional texts
