Definitions, Classification & Coordinating Conjunctions (FANBOYS)

Definition of a conjunction
Word linking words, phrases, or clauses.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Three primary grammatical classes of conjunctions
Coordinating, subordinating, and correlative conjunctions.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Mnemonic used for seven coordinating conjunctions
FANBOYS.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
The seven coordinating conjunctions represented by FANBOYS
For, And, Nor, But, Or, Yet, So.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Primary structural role of coordinating conjunctions
Join grammatically equal syntactic units.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Semantic function of the conjunction 'and'
Expresses cumulative addition.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Semantic function of the conjunction 'but'
Expresses adversative contrast or exception.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Semantic function of the conjunction 'for'
Introduces reason or causal explanation.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Semantic function of the conjunction 'nor'
Adds a negative alternative idea.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Semantic function of the conjunction 'or'
Presents alternative choices or possibilities.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Semantic function of the conjunction 'so'
Indicates result or logical consequence.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Semantic function of the conjunction 'yet'
Expresses concession or unexpected contrast.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Punctuation rule when connecting two independent clauses with FANBOYS
Place comma before the coordinating conjunction.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Is a comma needed when joining two compound verbs?
No comma is required.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Syntactic requirement of parallel structure with conjunctions
Joined elements must share grammatical form.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Example of error in coordinating parallelism
He likes swimming and to run.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Corrected parallel form for swimming and running
He likes swimming and running.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Difference between coordinating 'for' and preposition 'for'
Conjunction connects clauses; preposition takes noun.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Can coordinating 'so' introduce a subordinate dependent clause?
No, it links coordinate independent clauses.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Which FANBOYS conjunctions express logical contrast?
But and yet.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Which FANBOYS conjunctions express alternatives?
Or and nor.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Clause structure immediately following 'nor'
Inverted auxiliary and subject structure.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Example of sentence featuring 'nor'
He did not call, nor did he write.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Can 'and' connect words of different parts of speech?
No, both must match grammatically.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Tone conveyed by coordinating 'for'
Formal and literary style.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें

Subordinating Conjunctions: Time, Cause, Purpose & Concession

Definition of a subordinating conjunction
Links subordinate dependent clause to independent clause.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Punctuation when subordinating clause begins a sentence
Comma separates the two clauses.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Punctuation when independent clause precedes subordinate clause
No comma is required.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Major subordinating conjunctions of time
When, while, before, after, since, until.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Major subordinating conjunctions of cause or reason
Because, since, as, that.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Major subordinating conjunctions of concession or contrast
Although, though, even though, whereas, while.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Major subordinating conjunctions of condition
If, unless, provided that, as long as.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Major subordinating conjunctions of purpose
So that, in order that, lest.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Major subordinating conjunctions of comparison
Than, as...as, so...as.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Semantic difference between 'because' and 'since'
Because emphasizes cause; since highlights result.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Can 'because' and 'therefore' appear in the same sentence?
No, creates an ungrammatical double connector.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Semantic difference between 'while' and 'whereas'
While denotes time/contrast; whereas denotes sharp contrast.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Use of 'as' indicating simultaneous actions
Expresses two actions happening concurrently.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Use of 'after' in sequence of past tenses
Followed by past perfect tense.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Example of 'after' governing past perfect
He left after the train had departed.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Use of 'before' in sequence of past tenses
Followed by simple past tense.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Example of 'before' with past tenses
The train had left before he arrived.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Modal auxiliary governed by 'so that' expressing present purpose
May or can.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Modal auxiliary governed by 'so that' expressing past purpose
Might or could.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Example of 'so that' with present purpose
He studies hard so that he may pass.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Example of 'so that' with past purpose
He studied hard so that he might pass.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Concessive partner permitted with 'although' or 'though'
Yet (or simple comma).
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Can 'but' accompany 'although' in a single sentence?
No, strictly ungrammatical in standard English.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Difference between 'as if' and 'as though'
Function identically to express hypothetical similarity.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Verb form following 'as if' describing unreal situation
Past subjunctive (were / had been).
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें

Correlative Conjunctions & Subject-Verb Agreement Rules

Definition of correlative conjunctions
Paired conjunctions joining balanced sentence elements.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Fixed partner of correlative 'either'
Or.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Fixed partner of correlative 'neither'
Nor.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Fixed partner of correlative 'not only'
But also.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Fixed partner of correlative 'both'
And.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Fixed partner of preposition 'between'
And.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Fixed partner of preposition 'from'
To.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Subject-verb agreement rule for 'either...or'
Verb agrees with the nearest subject.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Subject-verb agreement rule for 'neither...nor'
Verb agrees with the nearest subject.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Subject-verb agreement rule for 'not only...but also'
Verb agrees with the nearest subject.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Example of agreement with nearest subject in 'either...or'
Either he or his brothers are responsible.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Subject-verb agreement rule for 'both...and'
Always takes a plural verb.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Example of 'both...and' governing plural verb
Both the teacher and the boy are present.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Subject-verb agreement rule for 'as well as'
Verb agrees with the first subject.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Subject-verb agreement rule for 'along with' and 'together with'
Verb agrees with the first subject.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Example of agreement with first subject in 'as well as'
The king as well as ministers was executed.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Parallelism requirement with 'not only...but also'
Identical parts of speech must follow both.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Error in: 'He not only lost his ticket but also his luggage'
Mismatched position of verb and noun.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Corrected placement of 'not only...but also' in ticket sentence
He lost not only his ticket but also his luggage.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Can 'as well as' be used as correlative partner for 'both'?
No, both is paired only with and.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Why 'Both Ram as well as Shyam' is incorrect
Must use 'Both Ram and Shyam'.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Can 'else' be paired with 'than'?
No, else is paired with but.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Example sentence featuring 'else...but'
It is nothing else but sheer arrogance.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Can 'other' or 'rather' be paired with 'but'?
No, paired strictly with than.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Example sentence featuring 'other...than'
He had no other choice than surrender.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें

Syntactic requirement when 'hardly' begins a sentence
Triggers auxiliary-subject inversion.

Hardly + had + subject + V3.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें

No sooner had + subject + V3... than.

Should.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें

No, lest inherently contains negative meaning.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें

उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें

That.

Difference between 'so...that' and 'such...that'

उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें

उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Partner used with 'so' in negative comparisons

उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Can 'so...as' be used in affirmative comparisons?
No, reserved for negative sentences.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Can 'as...as' be used in both affirmative and negative sentences?
Yes, standard in both contexts.

Or.

Common Conjunction Errors, Redundancies & Sentence Correction

Why 'Although he is poor, but he is honest' is wrong
Redundant pairing of although and but.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Corrected form of the 'although...but' sentence
Although he is poor, he is honest.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Why 'Because he was ill, therefore he was absent' is wrong
Redundant pairing of because and therefore.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Corrected form of the 'because...therefore' sentence
Because he was ill, he was absent.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Can 'since' be paired with 'so' in the same sentence?
No, never combine since with so.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Can 'as' be paired with 'so' when introducing cause?
No, remove so from result clause.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Correct proverbs exception for 'as...so'
As you sow, so shall you reap.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Error in: 'The reason why he failed is because he was lazy'
Use that instead of because.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Grammatical rule governing 'the reason why'
Takes that, never because or due to.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Corrected form of 'The reason why... because' sentence
The reason why he failed is that he was lazy.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Error in: 'He asked that whether I was coming'
Redundant use of that before whether.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Can 'that' precede interrogative words in indirect speech?
No, that is never used before wh-words or whether.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Correct form of 'He asked that where I lived'
He asked where I lived.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Difference between 'doubt that' and 'doubt whether'
Doubt whether in affirmative; doubt that in negative.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Conjunction following 'no doubt' or 'do not doubt'
That.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Conjunction following 'I doubt' in positive declarative statements
Whether or if.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Error in: 'Between 10 AM to 5 PM'
Replace to with and.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Error in: 'From 10 AM and 5 PM'
Replace and with to.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Error in: 'He had scarcely entered inside than it started raining'
Replace than with when.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Error in: 'No sooner did the bell ring when children ran out'
Replace when with than.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Why 'unless...not' is ungrammatical
Unless inherently expresses negative condition.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Why 'until...not' is ungrammatical
Until inherently expresses negative temporal boundary.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
Difference between 'unless' and 'until'
Unless denotes condition; until denotes time.
उत्तर देखने के लिए टैप करें
