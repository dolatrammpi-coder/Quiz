Error Spotting

🔍
Major Grammatical Error Categories, Rules & Corrections
Error Type Rule Correct Example
Subject-Verb Agreement
उत्तर देखें
Intervening prepositional phrases do not affect subject number
उत्तर देखें
The quality of apples was poor.
Correlative Conjunctions
उत्तर देखें
Nearest subject determines verb number in either...or
उत्तर देखें
Either he or his brothers were present.
Parenthetical Connectors
उत्तर देखें
First subject governs verb with as well as
उत्तर देखें
The leader, as well as followers, was arrested.
Indefinite Pronouns
उत्तर देखें
Each, every, either, neither take singular verbs
उत्तर देखें
Each of the boys has arrived.
Uncountable Nouns
उत्तर देखें
Nouns like advice and furniture never take plural suffix
उत्तर देखें
All the luggage was checked.
Hyphenated Compound Nouns
उत्तर देखें
Noun acting as adjective remains singular
उत्तर देखें
A five-rupee note was found.
Tense Sequence with Since
उत्तर देखें
Present perfect main clause paired with simple past clause
उत्तर देखें
Five years have passed since he came.
Habitual Action in Continuous Tense
उत्तर देखें
Present habits require simple present tense
उत्तर देखें
He goes for a walk daily.
Conditional Clause Modals
उत्तर देखें
Never use modal auxiliaries inside if-clauses
उत्तर देखें
If it rains, we will stop.
Correlative Pairing
उत्तर देखें
Hardly and scarcely pair with when, not than
उत्तर देखें
Hardly had I arrived when it rained.
Comparative Conjunctions
उत्तर देखें
No sooner pairs with than, not then/when
उत्तर देखें
No sooner did he arrive than cheers rose.
Negative Word Pairing
उत्तर देखें
Lest governs should with no negative words
उत्तर देखें
Walk carefully lest you should fall.
Degree Redundancy
उत्तर देखें
Avoid double comparatives and superlatives
उत्तर देखें
This is much better than that.
Latin Comparatives
उत्तर देखें
Senior, junior, superior take preposition to
उत्तर देखें
He is senior to me.
Adjective vs Adverb
उत्तर देखें
Action verbs are modified by adverbs
उत्तर देखें
He spoke extremely politely to us.
Dangling Modifiers
उत्तर देखें
Participial phrases must clearly attach to logical subjects
उत्तर देखें
Walking down the road, I saw a tiger.
Frequently Repeated Competitive Exam Error Traps
Error Type Rule Correct Example
Superfluous Pairing
उत्तर देखें
The reason why takes that, never because
उत्तर देखें
The reason why he failed is that he slept.
Double Negative
उत्तर देखें
Avoid negative words with unless and until
उत्तर देखें
Unless you study, you will fail.
Redundant Prepositions
उत्तर देखें
Order, describe, enter take no direct preposition
उत्तर देखें
He entered the hall quietly.
Fixed Preposition
उत्तर देखें
Abstain, refrain, prevent require preposition from
उत्तर देखें
He abstained from alcohol completely.
Idiomatic Preposition
उत्तर देखें
Manage difficulties uses cope with, never cope up
उत्तर देखें
She could cope with the pressure.
Possessive before Gerund
उत्तर देखें
Nouns and pronouns preceding gerunds require possessive case
उत्तर देखें
I insisted on his going there.
Relative Pronoun Inversion
उत्तर देखें
One of the plus plural noun plus who takes plural verb
उत्तर देखें
One of the men who were present.
Parallel Structure
उत्तर देखें
Coordinating conjunctions connect identical grammatical forms
उत्तर देखें
She likes painting, dancing, and singing.
Subjunctive Were
उत्तर देखें
Unreal conditions use were across all persons
उत्तर देखें
If I were you, I would agree.
Article Omission
उत्तर देखें
Zero article with institutions visited for primary purpose
उत्तर देखें
He went to hospital for surgery.
Pronoun Order
उत्तर देखें
Normal polite personal pronoun order is 2-3-1
उत्तर देखें
You, he, and I must attend.
Pronoun Order (Fault)
उत्तर देखें
Confessing errors or negative acts uses order 1-2-3
उत्तर देखें
I, you, and he are guilty.

Concept of Error Spotting & Systematic Detection Strategy

Primary objective of error spotting in competitive exams
उत्तर देखें
Test grammatical accuracy and syntactic awareness.
First step in systematic sentence error detection
उत्तर देखें
Identify grammatical subject and principal verb.
Second step in systematic sentence error detection
उत्तर देखें
Verify subject-verb number and person agreement.
Third step in systematic sentence error detection
उत्तर देखें
Examine tense consistency across all clauses.
Fourth step in systematic sentence error detection
उत्तर देखें
Check modifier placement and pronoun antecedents.
Fifth step in systematic sentence error detection
उत्तर देखें
Check prepositions, articles, and conjunction pairs.
Most common category of grammatical error in exams
उत्तर देखें
Subject-verb agreement errors.
Technique to locate true subject across intervening clauses
उत्तर देखें
Eliminate prepositional phrases and parenthetical clauses.
Role of reading speed during error detection
उत्तर देखें
Careful analytical reading beats hasty skimming.
Significance of punctuation markers in error spotting
उत्तर देखें
Indicate clause boundaries and modifier scope.
What to do if no obvious grammatical flaw appears
उत्तर देखें
Examine idiomatic usage and preposition collocation.
Concept of No Error option in exams
उत्तर देखें
Grammatically sound sentences requiring no change.
Danger of relying purely on ear or colloquial sound
उत्तर देखें
Informal spoken English often hides grammatical errors.
Check for parallel structure across coordinating conjunctions
उत्तर देखें
Ensure balanced parts of speech.
Check for correct verb forms following auxiliaries
उत्तर देखें
Verify base form, past participle, or gerund.
Check for proper article usage before singular countable nouns
उत्तर देखें
Ensure presence of a, an, or the.
Check for pronoun-antecedent number and gender harmony
उत्तर देखें
Pronoun must match noun it replaces.
Check for unattached dangling participial phrases
उत्तर देखें
Ensure subject of participle opens main clause.
Strategy for identifying errors in multi-clause sentences
उत्तर देखें
Analyze each clause independently, then verify linkages.
Importance of analyzing correlative conjunction pairs
उत्तर देखें
Verify exact pairing and positional symmetry.

उत्तर देखें
Singular subject quality governs singular verb.

उत्तर देखें
Replace were with was.
Rule governing subjects connected by as well as
उत्तर देखें
Verb agrees with the first subject.
Error in: Either the teacher or his students has arrived
उत्तर देखें
Replace has with have.
Rule governing subjects connected by either...or
उत्तर देखें
Verb agrees with the nearest subject.

Replace were with was.
