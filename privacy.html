Privacy Policy

At My Study Portal, we respect the privacy of visitors and are committed to explaining clearly how information may be handled when you use this website.

Information We May Collect

My Study Portal does not intentionally require visitors to submit sensitive personal information in order to use ordinary quiz pages. However, standard technical information may be processed automatically when you visit a website. This may include information such as browser type, device type, operating system, approximate location, pages viewed, referring pages, and technical information needed to operate, secure, and improve the website.

If you contact us by email, we may receive the information that you choose to provide, such as your email address, name, message, or other details included in your communication. This information is used to respond to your request and is not intentionally collected for unrelated purposes.

Cookies and Similar Technologies

This website may use cookies and similar technologies. Cookies are small files or identifiers that can be stored on your device by a website or by services used by the website.

Cookies may be used for purposes such as remembering preferences, measuring website usage, maintaining security, displaying advertising, and understanding how visitors interact with the website.

You can control or delete cookies through the settings of your web browser. Disabling certain cookies may affect some website features or the way advertising is displayed.

Google AdSense and Advertising

My Study Portal may use Google AdSense or other third-party advertising services to display advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses, device information, or similar technologies to help serve, measure, and limit advertisements and to provide advertising-related services.

Depending on applicable settings, consent, and regional requirements, advertisements may be personalized based on information such as a visitor's interests, browsing activity, or other permitted signals. Non-personalized advertising may also be displayed where applicable.

Third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this or other websites. The use of advertising cookies enables Google and its partners to serve and measure advertisements.

Visitors may have options to control personalized advertising through Google's advertising settings and through controls provided by their browser or applicable consent mechanism.

Third-Party Services

Some features of this website may depend on third-party services. Third-party providers may process technical information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

We do not control the privacy practices of third-party websites or services. Visitors should review the applicable privacy policies of those services when interacting with them.

Quiz Pages and User Information

The ordinary quiz pages on My Study Portal are intended for educational practice. We do not intentionally ask users to submit sensitive personal information through these quiz pages.

External Links

Our website may contain links to external websites or resources. External websites operate independently and may have their own privacy policies and terms. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of external websites.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to maintain the security and reliability of the website. However, no method of transmission over the Internet or method of electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Children's Privacy

This website provides general educational content and is not intended to knowingly collect sensitive personal information from children. If you believe that personal information has been provided to us unnecessarily, please contact us so that the matter can be reviewed.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes in the website, technology, advertising services, or applicable requirements. Any updated version will be published on this page.
